## 3. Procedures

**A. Connect a private-supplier fulfillment app (Team Drop) to Shopify** [verify UI]
1. Sign up on the platform using the same email as your Shopify account (0% cost per the source).
2. Click "authorize store," then "add store," and follow the prompts — this installs an app on the Shopify dashboard.
3. Click "authorize" inside the installed app to complete the connection.
4. In Shopify, go to Settings → Shipping and Delivery.
5. Open the fulfillment-locations icon/section and explicitly add the app (e.g., "Team Drop") as a fulfillment location, then save. The source calls this step crucial — without it, checkout fails.
6. Confirm the app can see your products and orders, and that the storefront no longer throws "out of stock" or "cannot add to cart" on the connected product(s).

**B. Source and quote a product**
1. Inside the platform, go to "find products" to browse or search, or use the platform's own "winning products" list as a discovery aid (source notes this is secondary to the WhatsApp-agent route).
2. Alternative/preferred route: message the assigned agent on WhatsApp, send a photo of the product, and include an AliExpress/Alibaba link if you have one to speed matching.
3. Review the returned quote and the shipping-line options for your target country; compare cost and delivery-day range per line.
4. Optionally buy a sample before committing.
5. Either use the platform's "list to store" flow (pick store ID, enter product info and images) or create the product directly in Shopify and link it back to the platform — either path keeps the two systems in sync afterward.

**C. Fund and manage fulfillment**
1. Open the top-up/billing section, choose an amount, submit.
2. Pay via the bank transfer/wire details shown (source calls wire the easiest option).
3. Orders sync from Shopify automatically once a balance exists; the platform debits COGS + shipping per order, generally within 24 hours (source's stated norm, not a guarantee).
4. Track orders through stated states: awaiting payment → awaiting collection (purchased from the source warehouse, moving to the supplier's warehouse) → shipped → delivered → completed; a separate canceled tab exists.
5. Keep the balance topped up — a low balance stalls fulfillment.

**D. Upgrade to branded packaging (post-validation only)**
1. Confirm the product has already sold and is proven profitable — do this step only then.
2. In the platform's packaging section, choose mailer/packaging options (logo, custom mailer, thank-you card).
3. Use "packaging connection" to link the chosen packaging to the specific product so future orders use it automatically.

## 5. Diagnostics and checks

- Symptom: storefront shows "out of stock" or blocks "add to cart" on a connected product. Check: whether the fulfillment app is added under Shopify Settings → Shipping and Delivery → fulfillment locations, not just installed as an app. Fix: add it as a location explicitly and save. [7006bf8583c9]
- Symptom: orders are not fulfilling. Check: wallet/account balance on the platform. Fix: top up via bank transfer/wire; a low balance stalls processing. [a7e2993419b5]
- Symptom: customer says tracking shows delivered but they never received the item. Check: escalate to the assigned WhatsApp agent with the order details. Fix: agent traces it and can send a replacement unit, per the source. [d528b5e6c32f]
- Symptom: margins are thin despite decent sales. Check: current per-unit COGS quote versus order volume to date. Fix: ask the supplier to re-quote — the source says a good supplier lowers price with volume automatically, but says to still ask periodically rather than assume it happens. [2edf05052ea4]
- Symptom: rising refunds/chargebacks. Check: (a) shipping-line speed against the 6-10 day target, (b) whether the warehouse/product-quality pick is a known-good one. Fix: request a faster line if margins allow, and confirm with the supplier which warehouse produces the better-quality version of the product. [cac61838f02d], [38cefaf4b56b]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

- Manually punching each incoming order into a supplier's website instead of using an automated, wallet-funded sync — the source calls this outdated and says people were still doing it as of 2025.
- Skipping the Shopify fulfillment-location step after installing a 3PL app, causing checkout failures.
- Choosing a bad-quality warehouse/supplier, leading to refunds, chargebacks, withheld payments, or account closure.
- Letting delivery times run too long (associated with the 10-15 day marketplace range) and creating chargeback risk.
- Adding branding/custom packaging before a product is validated — treated as wasted cost and complexity.
- Paying a monthly subscription fee for fulfillment and also paying separately for sourcing on the same platform (source's specific complaint about certain paid tools, unnamed beyond AutoDS/Zendrop as a category).
- Assuming a supplier will lower your price with volume without ever asking — the source says to check in periodically rather than assume it's automatic.
