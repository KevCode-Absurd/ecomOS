## 1. What this source is for

It takes a beginner from zero to a running TikTok ads setup for an ecommerce store. It covers: account and Business Center setup, pixel install and test, 10 ad psychology principles, 4 inspiration tools, 3 ways to make ads (DIY in CapCut, UGC creators, AI in Arc Ads), in-feed campaign build with budget and targeting rules, search ads with keyword research, Spark Ads from own and creator posts, TikTok Shop setup with Shopify sync and affiliates, GMV Max campaigns, and retargeting audiences with budget tiers. Use it as a setup checklist and a starter structure. It is thin on measurement, scaling rules and kill rules.

## 2. Principles

1. **Be on TikTok because attention is there.** Source cites over 1 billion monthly active users, most downloaded app several years running, 95 minutes per user per day (top platform by time spent), TikTok Shop over $20 billion and heading to $30 billion by 2028 (eMarketer), and about 20% of the social commerce market. Condition: directional channel case, not a forecast for any store. `[web check: current TikTok user, time-spent and TikTok Shop GMV figures]` [4450d3d0a39e]
2. **TikTok is not only Gen Z.** Age split shown: 18 to 24 is 32%, 25 to 34 is 29%, 45+ is 18%. Source says some of his clients target 45+ and "crush it" because there is less competition, so CPC and CPM are lower. Condition: only if the product fits that age group. Evidence: presenter anecdote, no numbers. `[needs brand data: customer age mix]` [4450d3d0a39e]
3. **The algorithm is your friend.** A zero-follower account can reach millions organically with the right video. Organic winners can then be amplified with ad spend. [4450d3d0a39e]
4. **Use 4 ad types, each for a different intent, and mix them.** In-feed ads (For You feed, native look, custom CTA such as Shop Now, skippable; the most common type, best for testing, scaling and conversions; about every 4 to 5 scrolls). Spark Ads (boost an existing post, yours or a creator's; keeps likes, comments and shares). Search ads (video result for a searched term; diversifies discovery beyond the home feed; "I recommend mixing in with all the other ones"). TikTok Shop ads / GMV Max (buy inside TikTok with no card or shipping entry). [5097d23dbd00]
5. **Spark Ads beat plain ads because the creative is pre-tested organically.** Source says they feel more native, have higher CTR and lower CPA. Uses: UGC ads, influencer whitelisting, scaling winning creative. Evidence: presenter assertion only. [5097d23dbd00] [a388b241a299]
6. **Everyone wins with TikTok Shop, so TikTok pushes it.** Buyers already have card and address stored, checkout is a few clicks. Sellers get traffic and discovery. TikTok takes commission. The built-in affiliate platform finds creators who make shop videos you can then promote. Source calls Shop ads "basically the future of TikTok" and says every ecommerce store should set them up. [5097d23dbd00] [e65261264f64]
7. **Set up the account fully before spending, to avoid flags and bans.** Complete business info, upload any requested documents, add a credit card, and keep account health good or excellent. Evidence: presenter's experience and TikTok's own interface text ("reduce risk of suspension"). [bbb344647f7f]
8. **Almost everyone should set up a Business Center.** Ads Manager alone is enough only for 1 person who gives no one else access. Business Center is the "umbrella" for ad accounts, TikTok accounts, users, pixels, catalogs, audiences and shops. [fe05eddfed67]
9. **Do not run ads without the pixel, and do not trust it until events are tested.** It reports which ads work and what a sale costs, feeds optimization, and builds remarketing and lookalike audiences. If events do not fire, you cannot optimize for purchase or add to cart and "your results are not going to be great". [837875464f88] [f6953a15849d]
10. **Psychology beats budget and camera quality.** "You don't need a bigger budget. You don't need a fancy camera. You need to understand how the human brain works." The source gives 10 principles (items 11 to 20). (creative, neighbour folder 10-MCR)
11. **Authority bias.** People trust doctors, experts, scientists. Use attire (chef whites, lab coat, scrubs, stethoscope), a first-line credential ("I own a restaurant myself", "I am a chef"), showing every step of the process, or a celebrity (Mario Lopez example, GLP-1 ad). Source notes some examples only imply a credential through costume. Condition: health and similar claims carry legal and platform risk (card caveat). `[web check: TikTok ad policy on implied medical credentials]` [c9f92ea7cd01]
12. **Curiosity gap.** "The human brain cannot tolerate incomplete information." 4 hook types: story opener ("Chat, you won't believe it", resolved as a soap ad for Dr. Squatch "Sasquatch soap"), visual puzzle ("Can you remove this long bar?"), expectation with a timeline ("I used this COPD lung spray for 4 weeks. Here's what happened"), and a "Don't do it" reaction to something off screen. Source says "pretty much every single TikTok, the hook should create some sort of curiosity gap". [dcc6ad9478b3]
13. **Identity alignment.** "People do not buy products, they often buy versions of themselves." Call the identity out verbally or visually. Examples: fitness ("I eat to feed the machine", "Beast mode activated"), a ring ad ("Why real men are switching to this ring after marriage"), a reading pillow ("Kindle girlies", "physical book girlies", repeated). [333bccadffbc]
14. **Pain solution.** Source says humans are "twice as more motivated to avoid pain than we are to gain something positive". Examples: posture corrector, baby registry app ("overwhelmed by making a baby registry"), plantar fasciitis socks. Uses the 8-step formula in section 3.6. The "twice" figure has no source given. [3ba0a7c760ee]
15. **Pattern interrupt.** "The brain is a predictive machine." Anything unexpected stops the scroll. Examples: breaking or throwing things (a luggage brand shot 1 clip only for this), bowls on a head (Dr. Squatch spray deodorant), ASMR, a person covered in red (Try Beats), an AI-made extreme scalp visual with "Do you know what hard water does to your hair?". Card adds: keep it a short opening beat tied to the product. [bf90c0737d95]
16. **Price anchoring.** Show a higher price first. Anchor to a pricier product, a pricier service, or the old price with a reason for the cut. Examples: 2 near-identical bracelets at 35,000 vs 75,000; insurance at 47 vs 98. Condition: the anchor must be real and lawful (card caveat). [89b3eabfd629]
17. **Reciprocity.** "One of the most underused principles in TikTok advertising." Teach something valuable before the pitch. Tutorial ads fit. Example: a True Dental Implant ad over 22 minutes long that the presenter says is "absolutely crushing it". [d3d721f0b8ff]
18. **Scarcity.** Fear of missing out. Simplest form: "back in stock". Examples: "you've been eagerly waiting for us to be back in stock", "This anti-aging serum sold out three times this year", on-screen comments like "when is the restock, please". Condition: claims must be true (card caveat). [e19864a59d60] [b0e19b9fad56]
19. **Social proof.** "The more social proof you can have with your ads, the better off they will perform." Forms: reviews on screen, interviewing a real customer, follower counts (90K Instagram followers example), real-person stories (the pregnancy example). No benchmark given. [e19864a59d60] [f4562fe48a89]
20. **Specificity.** "Specific claims are believed, vague claims are ignored." Examples: "Lose weight fast" vs "Lose 11 lb in 23 days"; "Boost your income" vs "Made $4,387 in 12 days"; "watch how this changed my skin in 2 weeks" shown day by day; sea buckthorn day 0 to day 20; body spray "98% natural" instead of 100%; a stat list about coconut oil failures. Condition: numbers must be true. [3d9984ca94a0]
21. **Use inspiration tools, but know their blind spots.** The free TikTok library shows only opted-in ads, and smart big spenders opt out. [1da287a4b90a]
22. **3 ways to make ads, each with a trade-off.** DIY is cheapest and fastest to edit from data but is the most effort. UGC creators are "typically best converting" and scalable but often the most expensive. AI is fastest and very scalable; it was called lowest converting, but the source says new AI tools are "becoming one of the better converting ones". Evidence: presenter opinion. [4c9fd5a6c1c2]
23. **Change something on screen about every 3 seconds.** New angle or background every 3 seconds when filming. Add B-roll to keep attention ("I do recommend adding more"). Evidence: presenter preference. [5481df2dc5d2] [2de13cccd384]
24. **Start on the audio.** No dead space at the start. "You almost want the video to start right on the audio." [cd7f79fa0290]
25. **Optimize toward the conversion you want.** For ecommerce pick the Sales objective. Reach is for getting the word out when you accept poor ROAS. Consideration objectives rarely. Community Interactions only to grow an account's engagement or followers. [f6953a15849d]
26. **Broad targeting is best for most advertisers.** TikTok says so and the source agrees: "if you have a killer ad, they are going to find the right people". Presenter admits his "pridefulness" wants granular targeting, but tests show the algorithm is now "so good". [b9a9580d6fee]
27. **Creative quality is the biggest cost lever.** "Hands down, the thing that is going to affect it the most is the creative quality ... The better the ad is, the cheaper you're going to get views." Other cost drivers: targeting (broader is cheaper), industry competition (beauty and supplements cost more), seasonality (Q4 costs rise). [99979094b360]
28. **Test with control, scale with automation.** CBO off while testing audiences, on when scaling proven ad groups. Manual campaign first, Smart+ as a later or parallel test. [479be1c347e0] [097d6ce5e477]
29. **Test creative organically before paying for it.** Post first, wait 5 to 7 days, then boost only the winners. "I don't want to step in and run that as an ad if I can get free organic impressions." [260e5915e605] [8e7a9a972f04]
30. **GMV Max ROI target: low enough to deliver, not so low it loses money.** Setting it too high means ads "just aren't going to show at all". TikTok's suggestion (1.2 in the demo) is "a lot of times ... not even break even". [3f9a7828109d]
31. **Affiliates cost nothing until they sell.** Open collaborations pay commission only on sales. [f1578b0b61d9]
32. **Retargeting should exclude buyers and target real visitors.** Exclude purchasers, and switch off audience expansion because expanding "might as well just be running a broad audience". [1ee708d070fe]
33. **Grow structure with budget.** Start with 1 prospecting campaign; add interest tests, retargeting, then re-engagement and GMV Max as spend grows. [b9a9580d6fee] [ab880cedcc94]

## 3. Procedures

### 3.1 Create the TikTok Ads account [verify UI]
1. Use the sign-up page ("unlock offer" if using a credit offer). Enter email or phone and a password. [bbb344647f7f]
2. Enter business name, website, industry and phone. Website is optional, but the source recommends adding it "for settings down the road". [bbb344647f7f]
3. Check region, currency and time zone are correct. Tick the box and continue. [bbb344647f7f]
4. If you claim ad credits, read the terms under "see the details". The coupon icon top right shows current offers. `[web check: current TikTok new-advertiser credit offer]` [bbb344647f7f]
5. Go to Tools > Account setup. Fill street address, province, postal code. Ads cannot run until this is complete. [bbb344647f7f]
6. Payment type: choose automatic payments (card is charged automatically). Manual payments stop ads when a threshold (example $100) is reached. Source prefers automatic "so if an ad is working really well, I just keep them running". [bbb344647f7f]
7. Documents: click through every document item and upload anything TikTok requests. Need depends on location and account standing. Source says this raises the chance the account is not banned. [bbb344647f7f]
8. Payment method: credit card or PayPal; source recommends credit card. Add it before launch; TikTok's own text says it reduces suspension risk. [bbb344647f7f]
9. Audience controls: set default location (demo: all United States; states are possible), minimum age (demo: 18), and excluded custom audiences (advanced; skip at first). Save. [bbb344647f7f]
10. Brand safety hub: choose inventory tier (expanded, standard, limited). Source says most companies should use standard and apply it to all objectives ("add all"). Optionally exclude categories such as youth content, combat sports, gambling, lotteries, and use vertical sensitive exclusions. Save. [bbb344647f7f]
11. Account health: check customer feedback and ad group status. Keep both "good" or "excellent". Fix any error immediately. Watch notifications for account health items. [bbb344647f7f]

1. In Ads Manager hover the left menu and click Business Center. Answer the business questions. [fe05eddfed67]
2. Users: confirm you are listed. Add members by email with a role: admin, standard, finance, or financial analyst. They get an email invite. [fe05eddfed67]
3. Accounts > advertising accounts: check the ad account is linked (a new one may auto-link). If not, "add advertising account" > request access > admin > paste the ad account ID (copy it from the Ads Manager account menu). [fe05eddfed67]
4. Access to an account does not give every member access. Open the ad account, add individual users, set each person's permission, confirm (a confirmation email may be needed). [fe05eddfed67]
5. Link the TikTok (organic) account: add account > link existing or create new > "send a QR code". [fe05eddfed67]

   - TikTok account sharing: turn on if partners need it.
   - Scan the QR code on the phone logged into that TikTok account. It auto-syncs.
6. Add members to the TikTok account too. [fe05eddfed67]
7. Go back to the advertising account > Assets and make sure the TikTok account is linked to the ad account. This lets you run any organic post as an ad without a code. [fe05eddfed67] [cd344df6562d]
8. If you run a TikTok Shop, add the Shop here and link it to the same assets. [fe05eddfed67] [e65261264f64]

Other Business Center areas: paid overview dashboard across ad accounts (filterable) and custom reports; native post scheduler (calendar view; posts can be turned into ads); assets (catalogs, audiences, leads, forms, minis); partners; brand finances (payment and billing, finance or admin role needed); brand safety hub. ### 3.6 Write a pain-solution ad: the 8-step formula (creative, neighbour folder 10-MCR)
1. Hook (name the pain; example "things I wish I knew sooner about plantar fasciitis").
2. Problem ("my podiatrist said my runs have triggered my plantar fasciitis").
3. Failed solution (optional; "I tried these shoes and they didn't work").
4. Intro the product ("he recommends that I wear this").
5. Feature and benefit (as many as sound natural; "stabilized my foot to promote blood circulation", "targeted the root cause").
6. Demo the product working.
7. Desired result that answers the pain ("what I thought was a 3-month recovery ... is now 3 weeks").
8. Call to action.

### 3.7 Find ad inspiration (4 tools) [verify UI]

### 3.8 DIY ad: script and film (creative, neighbour folder 10-MCR)
1. Pick the product (demo: Oikos protein shake) and follow the 8-step formula. [5481df2dc5d2]
2. Write the script yourself or with Claude or ChatGPT. Demo prompt: "Create me a UGC style script for Oikos protein shake that focuses on the problem solution. One problem with other protein shakes is they are very thick and chalky. This one is not." Output had hook, problem, product intro, demo, solution payoff and CTA, with visual ideas. [5481df2dc5d2]
3. Film on a phone held vertically. Record the hook, pause, then each segment (problem, product intro, feature-benefit, CTA) as separate clips. Change angle or background about every 3 seconds. A tripod is optional. [5481df2dc5d2]

### 3.9 Edit in CapCut [verify UI]
1. Create project > import clips (from computer or phone; drag and drop works). Name clips 1, 2, 3... and drop them on the timeline in order. Zoom the timeline to see audio waveforms. [cd7f79fa0290]
2. Cut dead space between takes with split, "delete left" or "delete right". Remove dead space at the very start. Use undo if needed; spacebar to play. [cd7f79fa0290]
3. Select all clips > enhance audio: reduce noise, enhance voice, normalize loudness (paid). Fixes echo from a phone in an untreated room. [cd7f79fa0290]
4. Add hook text for muted viewers (demo: "Tired of chalky protein?"). Pick a preset style, scale, position. Keep it only over the hook segment. Source likes it "as natural and kind of UGC as possible". [cd7f79fa0290]
5. Add a sound effect (demo: camera click) exactly when a key claim is spoken ("30 g of protein"). Add a circle sticker and an arrow over the claim for the same moment. [cd7f79fa0290]
6. Music: source usually runs no music. If you add it, filter to "commercial sounds" or commercial use, or the ad can get a copyright problem. Favorite tracks you like. AI music is an option. Set volume "barely in the background". [cd7f79fa0290]
7. Captions: select all > captions > auto-detect language > generate (Pro; source says about $20 a month; takes about 20 to 30 seconds). Review filler-word flags (it flagged "like" that was not filler). Pick a caption template (diamond = Pro). Move captions so they do not cover product details, but not so low they get covered. [cd7f79fa0290]
8. B-roll: place it on a track above the main video (below replaces the segment). Scale to fill. Align with the line it shows (demo: someone eating chalk over "chalky protein"). Mute its audio. Keep captions above it. Add several. [2de13cccd384]
9. Export > name it > export vertical. [cd7f79fa0290]

### 3.11 AI ads in Arc Ads [verify UI]
Setup: sign up, create a folder (organize folders by video type), then "+" for a new project. The project offers 4 options; source usually starts from "see more". [59b813ebce97]

**AI talking actor** [59b813ebce97]
1. Choose AI actor. Use when you only need a talking head that does not hold the product.
2. Paste the script (demo: electric shower scrubber).
3. Pick an actor: filter all, favorites, my actors, female, male, age, situation or background. Preview on hover.
4. For A/B tests select several actors; each reads the same script (demo selected 3).
5. Add emotions (auto-detected per line, such as excited, mischievous, satisfied). Edit if needed. Makes delivery less robotic.
6. Pick model: Arc Ads 1.0 or Omni Human 1.5. Pick text-to-speech, or speech-to-speech (record your own inflection).
7. Generate: about 1 credit per video.

**Fashion try-on** [40d97732e7aa]
1. See more > presets > fashion try-on. Pick a scene (demo: closet) > remix.
2. Upload the garment image (demo: blue sweater). Aspect 9:16. Generate (1 credit).
3. Combine with a talking-actor clip in the editor.

**Product showcase** [625f6adb825f] [926e9d26c71c]
1. See more > presets > product showcase. Pick a scene (demo: woman in a bedroom) > remix. The actor will differ from the preset.
2. Upload a product photo on a white background with no people.
3. Set aspect ratio. Describe the action. Demo: "have the model sitting like she is while holding the charger and talking about how great it is for charging her phone and other device".
4. Generate (1 credit; about 2 to 5 minutes).
5. If the speech cuts off, press Extend (or presets > extend) and describe what comes next. Demo added 19 seconds to a 12-second clip. Extend works on any video, AI or not.

**Hook swap from another ad** [22e9aea82696]
1. Download a strong hook video (demo from Foreplay: "My For You page convinced me to buy something again, but I have to admit that this one is actually very worth the hype", a Rainforest Activated Magnesium ad).
2. Upload it to Arc Ads. Prompt: "Can you talk about my product Premier Supplements?" (made-up brand).
3. Generate (0.4 credits). Output keeps the hook and swaps the product. Source uses this "very often to test different hooks".
4. See section 9: this conflicts with the ecomOS rule on copying competitor text.

**Model choice** [64b11c60af24]
1. Veo 3.1 (transcript "V03.1"): cinematic, "artistic art style" that often does not suit ads; source uses it least. Fixed 8 seconds; resolution up to 4K. Demo prompt: "Create me some beautiful scene of a sunset while a man is sitting on the mountains." Can add a product image.
2. Kling 3.0: any length from 3 to 15 seconds, second by second. Demo prompt with a chair image: "make me a UGC ad for a company that sells office chairs".
3. Sora 2 / Sora 2 Pro (source's favorite, "most realistic"): lengths 4, 8 or 12 seconds only; up to 1080. Add a product image and a short description. Sometimes cuts off at an odd moment; trim after. Demos: shower scrubber, IcePod cold plunge.

**Motion control and presets to avoid** [fe68dca8051c]

### 3.16 Spark Ads: the organic-first loop and setup [verify UI]
Prerequisites: TikTok ad account; a TikTok profile (for own-account Spark Ads); profile linked to the ad account; for creators, an ad authorization code. [260e5915e605]

Loop (source calls it 6 steps but lists 5): [260e5915e605] [8e7a9a972f04]
1. Make 3 or more videos, or have influencers make them.
2. Post them on your account, or influencers post on theirs.
3. Wait 5 to 7 days for each post to reach its full organic potential. A new post usually has about a 1-week life cycle, and within the first 2 days you can tell if it is taking off.
4. Judge performance by sales, follows or engagement, and pick 1 or 2. At scale, post 20 and take the top 10%, 20% or 30%.
5. Run the winners as Spark Ads. Repeat.

Own post: [cd344df6562d]
1. Build campaign and ad group as normal (Spark works with any objective, including Sales).

Creator post: [15b3e91bb766]
1. Ad creative > TikTok post > "add post". It asks for a post code.
2. Send the creator these steps (a screenshot helps): open the video posted for you > three dots > ad settings > turn on ad authorization > set duration (ask for 365 days) > authorize > save > manage code > copy code > send.
3. Paste the code > continue > the video appears > add to library > confirm.
4. Set the destination URL to your page. The ad shows from the creator's profile (demo: "Review Base") but traffic goes where you choose.
Engagement on the ad (likes, comments) shows on the organic post, and engagement on the post shows on the ad. [a388b241a299]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

1. Launching without complete account info, documents and a card, which risks flags, suspension or a ban. [bbb344647f7f]
2. Using manual payments, so a winning ad stops at the threshold. [bbb344647f7f]
3. Ignoring account health errors and notifications. [bbb344647f7f]
4. Overlooking the brand safety hub. [bbb344647f7f]
5. Giving access without a Business Center, or assuming account access covers every member. [fe05eddfed67]
6. Letting A/B test variants post to your organic profile ("show in profile"). [fe05eddfed67]

## 9. Caveats, conflicts and what is missing

**Internal inconsistencies**
- Budget math does not match the learning-phase goal. A 2x CPA daily budget buys about 2 conversions a day, about 14 in 7 days, not 25. Reaching 25 in 7 days needs about 3.6x CPA a day (25 / 7 = 3.57). The source says to "try to get the 25 campaign results within that seven-day period" and also recommends 2x CPA. Card [153cd8af7351] states 2x CPA "so the set can accumulate about 25 results within 7 days"; that link is the card's, not arithmetic. [e413ec2ddfd9] [153cd8af7351]
- GMV Max ROI: card [153cd8af7351] says set target ROAS "slightly low" then raise it. The transcript says keep it "on the lower end" relative to an unrealistic 20, but its actual rule sets it above TikTok's suggestion (1.2 plus 1 = 2.2) and lowers it only if delivery fails. Follow the transcript. [3f9a7828109d]
- Spark example: presenter says he would boost the 2.3M-view video, though another had 8M. Likely a slide or speech error. Card [8e7a9a972f04] also cites "400+ SKUs" from the GMV Max demo, which is a different part of the course.
- AI model resolution vs length: first said Veo 3.1 offers 4K and Kling 3.0 offers exact seconds; later said "higher resolution, Kling ... exact seconds, Vio". Card [64b11c60af24] follows the first version. `[web check: current resolution and length limits per model]`
- Special promotion days numbers are muddled ("up to 50% more ... increase it by 10 times", "360", "instead of 70% and a 10 ... 50% and a one ... $90"). `[verify UI]` the actual controls and defaults. [3f9a7828109d]
- "Most people watch videos with the sound on" (captions section) vs adding hook text for people scrolling muted. Both steps are kept. [cd7f79fa0290]
- Time spent: 95 minutes a day vs "an hour and 37 minutes" in 2025 on the chart. [4450d3d0a39e]
- Retargeting budget: "at least a budget of 3,000" and "3,000 or less" in the same passage, with no period (day or month). [ab880cedcc94]
- The Spark loop is called 6 steps but 5 are listed. [260e5915e605]
- Search match types: the transcript reads the tooltip text in a confused order. The source's meaning: broad is widest, phrase is middle, exact is narrowest. [3ae33f4363ac]
- GMV Max demo: "grab the top 20" then "these 14 products". [3f9a7828109d]

**Conflicts with ecomOS rules and common ecommerce advice**
- Hook swap copies a competitor ad's script almost word for word with your product inserted. ecomOS rules say competitor ads are signals for layout and mood only; never copy text, logos, products or faces. Use the hook's structure only, with your own words and true claims. [22e9aea82696]
- Authority through costume (scrubs, stethoscope, lab coat on non-experts) can break ad policy and consumer-protection rules, mostly for health products. ecomOS also bars invented claims. `[web check: TikTok policy on implied credentials and health claims]` [c9f92ea7cd01]
- Scarcity, price anchors, specific numbers and social proof must be true. ecomOS rule: no invented claims. [e19864a59d60] [89b3eabfd629] [3d9984ca94a0] [f4562fe48a89]
- AI product videos can redraw the product. ecomOS rule: use the real product photo where accuracy matters. The source does upload a real product image, but does not warn about redraws. [625f6adb825f] [64b11c60af24]
- GMV Max ROI "suggestion plus 1" is not tied to margin. Common practice sets a floor from break-even ROAS. `[needs brand data: gross margin, break-even ROAS]` [3f9a7828109d]
- Judging by in-platform ROAS with default attribution: the source leaves attribution windows at default and never discusses incrementality, view-through, or reconciling TikTok numbers with store data. For ecomOS, period ROAS = total value / total spend, from the brand's own sales data. `[needs brand data: store orders to reconcile TikTok-reported purchases]` [e413ec2ddfd9]
- Hiding ads from the ad library is presented as a must. It is a competitive choice, and some regions may require ad transparency. `[web check: ad library opt-out rules by region]` [40f4521921d6]
- Maximum data sharing is recommended with only a light privacy note. `[web check: consent requirements (for example GDPR, India DPDP) before choosing maximum]` [837875464f88]

**Presenter opinion, not tested fact**
- UGC converts best; AI now converts well. [4c9fd5a6c1c2]
- Spark Ads have higher CTR and lower CPA. [5097d23dbd00]
- Broad beats interest; spending power and income filters do not help. [b9a9580d6fee]
- Manual vs Smart+: "mixed results"; manual first. [097d6ce5e477]
- Pain is "twice" as motivating as gain; no source given. [3ba0a7c760ee]
- Cost ranges are "typical" with no country or date. `[web check: current TikTok CPM and CPC benchmarks for target market]` [99979094b360]

**What is missing (the source is thin on how)**
- No kill rules: when to pause an ad or ad group, what CPA or ROAS counts as a winner, how long to test beyond the learning phase.
- No scaling rules: "take the winner into another campaign and scale it up" with no budget step sizes or pacing.
- No Smart+ setup walkthrough, no Events API or server-side tracking, no catalog or video shopping ads for the website, no lead gen.
- No creative testing design (how many hooks, how to read results), no frequency or fatigue rules, no creative refresh cadence.
- No reporting guidance: the Analytics area (custom reports, experiment manager, attribution analytics) is named but not taught.
- No margin, break-even or LTV math for setting CPA or ROAS targets. `[needs brand data: margin, AOV, target CPA]`
- GMV Max country list and TikTok Shop availability outside the US are not given. The course assumes US targeting and USD. `[web check: TikTok Shop and GMV Max availability in the brand's market]`
- Creator contracts, usage rights and whitelisting terms beyond the authorization code are not covered.
- "Re-engagement campaign" at 10K+ is named but not built.

**Freshness risk**: nearly every step is UI-dependent and tied to late 2025 or early 2026 features (GMV Max, Smart+, TikTok One, Arc Ads models). Re-verify before each use. `[web check: TikTok Ads Manager and Seller Center current flows]`
