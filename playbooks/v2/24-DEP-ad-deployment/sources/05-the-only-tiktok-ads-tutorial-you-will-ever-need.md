## 2. Principles

1. **Set up the account base properly before any campaign.** "A lot of people rush through this part." Condition: first-time TikTok advertiser. Evidence: presenter opinion. [4204465b105f] [41b98e976611]
2. **Use a business email domain, not a personal email.** Source says TikTok gives "better ad approval rates with business domains". Condition: new Business Center signup. Evidence: presenter claim only; not verified `[web check: does TikTok treat business-domain emails differently for ad approval]`. [4204465b105f]
3. **Keep business and personal identities separate.** Sign up with email and password even if you already have a TikTok account. [4204465b105f]
4. **Always have a backup payment method.** A failed card debit can lead to ad disapprovals; a backup (PayPal or Venmo) avoids that. Start with a credit card for faster approval. Evidence: presenter opinion. [4204465b105f]
5. **Use a 3-tier pyramid: campaign = objective, ad group = budget, targeting, schedule, placement and bid, ad = creative.** The campaign objective "affects everything else downstream". Test multiple ads inside each ad group. [832a0215542d]
6. **Treat each ad group as a mini experiment that tests 1 specific thing.** Never put all budget into 1 ad group ("I see this mistake all the time"). Condition: testing phase; the card notes high-spend accounts may consolidate differently. [832a0215542d]
7. **TikTok learns separately at each ad group.** Source says a losing ad group will not drag down a winning one, which is "huge for testing and scaling". Evidence: presenter experience, not TikTok documentation `[web check: TikTok learning phase is per ad group]`. [832a0215542d]
8. **Pick web conversions as the objective for a product store, even when starting out.** It helps the algorithm learn who is likely to buy. Lead generation is for collecting emails or phone numbers. Traffic is for "just starting out and want to test creatives", but the presenter still recommends web conversions. [832a0215542d] [e9592c1f1fb3]
9. **Keep targeting as broad as possible unless you already have a lot of customer data.** "The algorithm is so powerful that it can do most of this itself." "The best performing ads often use the simplest targeting." "The more restrictions you add, the higher your cost typically go." Condition: large enough market; the card notes small markets may need broader settings. [e9592c1f1fb3] [ce98eaba5aeb]
10. **Spend at least $50 a day per ad group.** Below that, "you're just never going to have enough data to see what actually works". Evidence: presenter heuristic from $5M+ managed spend `[needs brand data: whether $50/day per ad group fits the brand's budget, AOV and target CPA]`. [e9592c1f1fb3] [d3b3e82930e5]
11. **Do not target custom audiences directly. Use them as seeds for lookalikes.** "You don't want to be targeting these audiences directly." Condition: prospecting. Note the source also says some creatives "crush with warm traffic", so it does not rule out warm testing (see section 9). [f10b4c010429]
12. **Lookalike size: balanced for big countries, broad for small ones.** Too narrow and the ad may not spend. Too broad and it targets "anyone that has any sort of similarity". Example: balanced for the US, broad for Finland. [f10b4c010429]
13. **Interests: 2 to 3 broad, related, parent categories at most.** Selecting 20+ interests "is actually hurting their account". Pick parent categories, not sub-interests. Evidence: the fashion brand case (section 7). [ce98eaba5aeb]
14. **Use behavioral targeting sparingly.** The presenter's own setup is usually broad plus 1 behavior setting. [ce98eaba5aeb]
15. **Refine targeting in steps, 1 layer per week.** The "targeting ladder" gathers data while controlling costs. [27e3b9f1f613]
16. **Ads must not look like ads.** "They try to make their ads look like TV commercials, and that's a big mistake." "The moment that your content looks like an ad, people scroll past." Source says 90% of advertisers get this wrong (presenter's figure). [86b060cb3312]
17. **The first 3 seconds are everything.** Stop the scroll without feeling salesy. [86b060cb3312] [78d35b770fae]
18. **Every hook needs a "what's in it for me" factor, and the video must deliver on it fast.** Give a reason to watch, then pay it off. [c75f0c0a486c]
19. **Rhythm keeps retention.** Break the cadence: clips of varied length (3 seconds, half a second), speaking then silence, zoom in, zoom out, speed-ups. "Fast to slow, fast to slow." Avoid monotone videos. [c75f0c0a486c]
20. **A spoken CTA can hurt retention and rewatch rate.** Source says retention and rewatch rate are "one of the major factors" TikTok uses to push a video, organically or in ads, and the ad button appears automatically. 21. **Test creatives that differ a lot.** Each style takes "a completely different approach" so you get clear winners and losers, not marginal differences. [d3b3e82930e5]
22. **Test the same creative against cold and warm audiences.** "Some creatives bomb with cold traffic, but actually crush with warm traffic." [ad60df85b9ef]
23. **Do not optimize before $50 spend or 1,000 impressions.** Source calls this "statistical significant data"; the card notes it does not guarantee a reliable read. [75aa9cb13f3d]

## 3. Procedures

### 3.1 Business Center and advertiser account `[verify UI]`
1. Go to business.tiktok.com and click Get Started. [4204465b105f]

2. Click Get started on Web. Select your website.
3. Choose Partner integrations (makes syncing with Shopify or Google Tag Manager easier). If no partner fits, choose Manual setup and follow the steps.
4. For Shopify: allow access and start onboarding. Follow the guide ("this is going to take 2 seconds").
5. Check the pixel now shows on the right-hand side of the Events screen.

### 3.3 Campaign creation `[verify UI]`
1. In Ads Manager click Campaign, then Create. [832a0215542d] [0856c5eb5224]
2. Pick the objective: web conversions for product sales (the presenter's default), lead generation for emails or phone numbers, traffic only to test creatives early.

3. Audience 2: engagement, people who completed (watched 100% of) your videos, last 30 days. Name "30 days video views".

8. Repeat for all 3 custom audiences.
9. Add the lookalike to the ad group under Audience, then Include.

1. Week 1: broad targeting.
2. Week 2: add a basic interest.

### 3.8 Filming [1e08dca25fbe]
1. Natural light, for example facing a window.
2. Clean, simple background.
3. Phone held vertically.
4. Film in 3 to 5 second clips.
5. Get multiple angles.

### 3.9 CapCut editing workflow `[verify UI]` [1e08dca25fbe]
1. Open CapCut, click Create project, drag all raw clips into the timeline.
2. Click Adapt to convert to 9:16 vertical.
3. Before other edits, go to Filters, apply the "4K" filter and drag it across the whole timeline (base effect for all UGC ads; cleans up footage and adds sharpness).
4. Put the strongest hook first. Trim it to 3 seconds maximum.
5. Text: Text, then Default text, drag over the first clip as the on-screen hook (example "best book ever").
6. Font: Gilroy Semi Bold or the brand font. Tighten tracking slightly in character settings. Place text just above eye level.
7. If text is hard to see, add a subtle stroke or outline from presets. "Don't overdo it."
8. Animation: Typewriter (acts as a small pattern interrupt).
9. Music: Audio, then Commercial music. Do not use TikTok sounds for ads; music must be commercially safe. Lower music to 70% if there is a voiceover.
10. Keep text and pacing in the order hook, problem, solution, call to action.
11. Export: File, then Export. 1080p, codec H.264, format MP4, 60 fps, bit rate at the recommended setting.
12. Preview on a phone before uploading to any ad platform.

### 3.10 First test plan (the "3:1" framework in the transcript; the card calls it 3:2:1) [d3b3e82930e5]
1. Objective: 1 clear objective.
2. Audiences (2 ad groups):
   - A: broad interest-based, 1 or 2 relevant interests, no age restriction, no demographic narrowing.
   - B: lookalike based on purchases, top markets only (probably US or UK), no other restrictions.
3. Creatives (3 per ad group):
   - Pain-solution: hook is the pain point, quick demo of the solution, clear CTA.
   - Tutorial: how-to format, feature highlights, educational angle.
   - UGC testimonial: real users' results, before and after, authentic feel.
4. Budget: at least $50 per ad group per day for 3 to 4 days. Total $300 to $400 (2 ad groups x $50 x 3 to 4 days).
5. Check at 24 hours and at 3 days against section 4, then apply section 5.

### 3.11 3:3 creative method [ad60df85b9ef]
1. Per product, make 3 videos: solution/problem, tutorial style, lifestyle (transcript says "lifestyle/res", unclear; the card reads it as lifestyle/UGC testimonial).
2. Across them, test 3 hooks, 3 angles and 3 CTAs.
3. Test them against different audiences (cold and warm).
