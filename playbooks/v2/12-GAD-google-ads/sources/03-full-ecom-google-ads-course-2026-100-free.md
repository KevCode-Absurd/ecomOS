## 2. Principles

1. **Launch in 3 steps, bottom of funnel first.** Step 1: Search and Shopping. Step 2: remarketing (Video, Display, or PMAX) after about 4 to 6 weeks of data. Step 3: Demand Gen once bottom and mid funnel are "pretty much fully saturated". Reason given: bottom and mid funnel convert cheaper and bring higher quality traffic; top of funnel costs more per conversion. The 4 to 6 weeks is "a general guideline, not strict by any means" and varies with budget, products and sales; sooner at high budget. [9f22aab00a78] [224802f88324] [b6530a0cdc65]
2. **Exception to step order: large accounts with proven Meta creatives may bring Demand Gen in early,** at the same time as PMAX (step 2). Condition: high budget, proof on other platforms, goal of rapid scaling. With a smaller budget and no other major platform, keep Demand Gen after PMAX remarketing. [9f22aab00a78] [2380ca9a554b]
3. **Always split branded and non-branded traffic into separate campaigns.** Branded = any query containing the brand name. Non-branded = generic product queries ("non-stick fry pan"). 3 reasons: new-customer acquisition, clean attribution, and not mixing signals to the algorithm. Most scaling comes from non-branded. [cd05d756f069]
4. **Run branded campaigns anyway, as cheap training data.** Source says branded is "arguably the cheapest way to gather data" and trains the algorithm, which speeds learning across the account. He says he has never seen an extremely high-performing account without a branded campaign, and calls people who say branded is a waste "wrong". He also says "nothing is black and white": some accounts benefit less. This is his opinion; no test is shown. [4b15db679fc4] [cd05d756f069]
5. **Shopping is the core of an ecommerce account; non-branded Search is secondary.** Source says Search is "not as necessary" in ecommerce as in service businesses, and he uses non-branded Search "not that frequently". Shopping is the main scaling and new-customer mechanism. [bca4ccb4fceb] [c45d399b63a9]
6. **Search for category intent, Shopping for specific-product intent.** A broad query ("non-stick pans") wants a range, so send a Search ad to the collection page. A specific query ("Peter's Pans red large 30 cm non-stick pan") is best served by the exact Shopping listing, the "sniper approach". [bca4ccb4fceb]
7. **Optimise for purchases only.** Conversion goal = purchase in every campaign. Not add to cart, not begin checkout, or Google "will take the easy route" and deliver add to carts without purchases. Condition: this is for the campaign goal; the source does not say to remove those events from the account. [d9dc799b0bcb]
8. **Conversion tracking must be right before anything else.** Bad tracking feeds Google poor data and you will not get the results you want. The source gives no tracking setup steps. [d9dc799b0bcb]
9. **Bid on clicks until the account has conversions, then move to conversion value.** A conversion strategy with no conversion history is "like telling an archer to hit a target while blindfolded". Thresholds vary inside the source (see section 4). [a3bf86a930b0] [56cda9478c6f]
10. **Start new product campaigns with bestsellers, not the full catalog.** With a limited budget, Google cannot test 200 products and "latches on to a couple of low-quality products" at mediocre ROAS. Use 5 to 10 bestsellers, usually under 10. The source applies the same "start with bestsellers" rule to Demand Gen and calls it best practice for any new campaign type. [11a3baaf5810] [2380ca9a554b]
11. **Budget must fit AOV.** Higher AOV means higher cost per conversion and slower learning. A low budget relative to AOV takes Google longer to optimise. Check you can fund the campaign before launching it. [56cda9478c6f] [d28083dc2834] [2380ca9a554b]
12. **Keep control; distrust Google's automation.** Skip AI keyword and asset generation, turn off auto-created assets, video enhancements and optimized targeting. Reason: Google "wants to make more money from you", does not know your brand, and with automation "you don't know what you're paying for". The one exception he makes: PMAX and Demand Gen targeting can be left open. [bdfbb0a5fb12] [f06749b19e9b]
13. **Fewer, better copy assets beat filling every field.** 3 to 5 Search headlines, 1 to 3 per field on Demand Gen and remarketing. Too many combinations make split testing impossible, and the late slots get filled with low-quality copy. [4b1f9877b48b] [ea21943bdb9b] [41602f39be64]
14. **Remarketing creative must assume the viewer already knows you.** No brand introduction. Give a reason to come back, such as an offer code. [e69a94e940ad] [ea21943bdb9b]
15. **Video remarketing before Display remarketing.** Source says video remarketing is "typically much more efficient" than image Display, with better reach and engagement. Add Display later, "if the PMAX isn't doing it for you". Presenter observation, not data. [0997a8f355eb] [224802f88324]
16. **PMAX is risky for beginners and for accounts with no data.** Done wrong, it "will swallow all of your traffic, ruin your attribution" and waste money. With no conversion data "you are probably going to lose money". Beginners should prefer standard remarketing campaigns. [be2fa7635a24] [11a3baaf5810] [0ac64f9f98bb]
17. **Use PMAX as a remarketing layer, not as the new-customer engine.** Non-branded Shopping acquires new customers; PMAX "laps up" past visitors and non-converting traffic. Source claims an all-in-one PMAX gives higher ROAS but fewer new customers because it leans on retargeting and branded traffic. [be2fa7635a24] [aa3a6d301e62] [3aeb15c6850e]
18. **Every campaign has one funnel job.** Non-branded Shopping: new customers. Branded Shopping and branded Search: branded demand only. Display and YouTube: site-visitor remarketing. PMAX: product remarketing and follow-up. Demand Gen: cold top of funnel. Check each new campaign against this map. Condition: the roles only hold if branded and non-branded are separated. [3aeb15c6850e]
19. **Demand Gen is the most scalable Google type and the closest to Meta, TikTok and Snapchat,** because it finds people who "may not even know they have the problem". Video is the highest-return Demand Gen format in his experience. [b6530a0cdc65] [2d25e7734ec7]
20. **Demand Gen must stay cold.** Never feed it your own customer, remarketing or converter lists; that pushes it to warm and branded traffic, which is PMAX's job. [12168d012cf1]
21. **More spend, better results.** Source: "the more money you spend, the better results you get on Google ... as long as you're spending it correctly". Opinion, stated for Demand Gen budgeting. [2380ca9a554b]

## 9. Caveats, conflicts and what is missing

- **Dated.** Recorded around late 2024 despite the 2026 title. Video campaigns, the PMAX negative list workaround and the "new" Shopping priority behaviour are the most likely to have changed. `[web check: all 3]`
- **PMAX isolation is disputed by the cards themselves.** Cards [0ac64f9f98bb], [70544247c36d] and [aa3a6d301e62] carry an editorial correction: PMAX audience signals do not impose strict targeting, removing manual creative does not guarantee Shopping-only delivery, and Merchant Center data can generate assets automatically. Do not call the campaign remarketing-only. Test it against Shopping and watch new vs returning outcomes. Source links in cards: support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14530785 and answer/15865151. `[web check: both pages]`
- **Campaign priority claim.** Source says setting Standard Shopping to High makes Shopping serve before PMAX on the same query. Common practitioner understanding is that campaign priority ranks Standard Shopping campaigns against each other, and that since a 2024 change PMAX and Standard Shopping compete on Ad Rank. The source's mechanism may be wrong. `[web check: Google's current rule for Standard Shopping vs PMAX serving]`
- **Excluding past visitors from Shopping** is stated as part of the structure but no steps are given. Unclear if it is possible on the Shopping campaign as described. `[web check: audience exclusions on Standard Shopping]`
- **Bid thresholds are inconsistent inside the source:** 10 to 15 conversions (Search), 15+ or 20+ (Shopping), 30+ a month for Target ROAS. The source never says over what window, except "per month" for 30+. The card [a3bf86a930b0] adds "aggregate conversion volume across the account", which the transcript does not say.
- **Branded bidding conflicts with incrementality advice.** Many operators treat branded conversions as mostly non-incremental and limit brand spend. The source defends brand on data-training grounds, not incrementality, and gives no test. `[needs brand data: brand search volume, organic brand click share, a brand-off holdout if possible]`
- **Headline count conflicts with Google's advice** (Google recommends more RSA headlines and fewer pins). The source's 3 to 5 plus pinning is opinion.
- **Title order.** Source puts the brand at the end of the title. Common feed advice often puts a known brand first. Neither is tested here. `[web check: current Merchant Center title guidance]`
- **AI descriptions.** The source treats the description as a keyword store. It does not mention Merchant Center policy on keyword stuffing or misleading text. `[web check: Merchant Center description policy]`
- **Garbled Max CPC example** ("75 or 120", "bids for $5"). The card reads $1.20 vs $5. Treat as unclear.
- **"Networks uneditable" and "no Presence option"** on Video and PMAX are as recorded.
- **Card additions not in the transcript:** "aggregate conversion volume across the account" [a3bf86a930b0]; "keep budget allocated so branded campaigns do not absorb non-branded spend" [cd05d756f069]; "feed discovered winners back into bestsellers" [dbf8b8785311]; "verify products eligible to serve after bulk changes" [3b03b7f382e4]; the PMAX test framing and "compare contribution and acquisition cost" [0ac64f9f98bb] [70544247c36d] [aa3a6d301e62]; "resolve launch-blocking validation errors" [f06749b19e9b]; "a Max CPC cap set too tight can suppress delivery" [56cda9478c6f]. Use as card inferences, not source claims.
- **Missing how-to:** no conversion tracking setup (tag, GA4 events, enhanced conversions, value passing), no attribution model, no break-even ROAS method, no product-level prune threshold, no reporting rhythm, no minimum remarketing list size, no Quality Score or Ad Rank, no keyword research method, no feed optimisation beyond basics, no shopping feed diagnostics, no negative keyword process beyond brand terms, no guidance for non-US or multi-language stores, no guidance on how to judge Demand Gen beyond platform ROAS.
- **Budget splits do not add up cleanly.** Shopping 50%+, video remarketing 10 to 15%, Demand Gen about 30% (30 to 40%), plus branded and Display. They are separate rules of thumb given at different steps, not 1 plan. `[needs brand data: total budget and step the account is at]`
- **Opinion stated as consensus:** "PMAX does a bit better without signals" is attributed to an unnamed "community consensus".
- **Conflicts with common ecommerce advice, summary:** (1) branded bidding as always needed; (2) Shopping priority controls PMAX; (3) feed-only PMAX as a clean remarketing tool; (4) 3 to 5 RSA headlines and pinning; (5) brand name last in feed titles. `[web check: each]`
