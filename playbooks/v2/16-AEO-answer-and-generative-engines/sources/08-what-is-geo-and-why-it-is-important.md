## 2. Principles

1. **GEO complements SEO, it does not replace it.** Condition: always, per this source. Both are optimization tactics aimed at visibility; both need high-quality, relevant content; both work off user intent. Evidence: presenter states "you still need SEO" and frames GEO as an addition. [d92b32a284e9]
2. **Zero-click search is the trigger for the shift.** Condition: when reviewing organic search measurement. The source opens with the claim that 60% of Google searches end without a click, unsourced. [f2a1a300d335]
3. **AI platforms draw on the same underlying search-engine information pool.** Condition: regardless of which AI tool a shopper uses. The source's own assertion is that "the pool of information isn't widely different" across Google, Perplexity, Bing or ChatGPT, so a brand's content has similar potential to be pulled into any of them. But it "won't happen by default": content must be clear, credible, useful and trustworthy for the AI to consider it. [d92b32a284e9]
4. **4 differences separate SEO from GEO and should shape content strategy.** Condition: when planning content or measurement for both search engines and AI answer engines.
   - Target: SEO aims at traditional search engine results pages (SERPs); GEO aims at the AI-generated response itself (an AI Overview or an app like ChatGPT), so the goal is to be referenced inside the answer, not to land a link on a results page.
   - Ranking factors: SEO leans on keywords, backlinks and technical SEO so crawlers can find and rank a whole page; content there does not have to be narrative, which is why alt tags and hashtags still work for SEO. GEO leans on content structure, authority and citations, because the AI has to understand a passage well enough to cite it as a source; the source calls this content "a resource rather than a recommendation," so it needs expertise.
   - Traffic type: SEO is click-driven, built to pull users through a link. GEO is exposure-driven: AI answers summarize without a click, so success shows up as brand visibility and mentions inside AI responses, which the source says can lead to indirect traffic rather than a direct session.
   - Scope: SEO optimizes the whole website or page (technical SEO, keywords, backlinks). GEO prioritizes the actual content of the page so it can be retrieved by AI systems without needing the full page context; write pieces that stand alone and are easy to quote.
   [d92b32a284e9] [cf7bae2b5009]
5. **This is a gradual shift, not an emergency rebuild.** Condition: always, per this source. "This shift isn't about rushing to change everything overnight... it's about understanding how to evolve your content strategy thoughtfully." Most SEO skills the source says "translate directly" to GEO; the operator mainly needs to understand the difference. No card cites this point directly; it is stated plainly in the text.

## 3. Procedures

### 3.1 Structure a passage to be quotable by AI (from the Story Chief example) `[verify UI: none, this is a writing pattern, not a platform step]`

1. Phrase the heading as the actual question a reader (or shopper) would ask, not a keyword phrase.
2. Answer the question directly in the paragraph immediately under that heading.
3. Keep the paragraph self-contained: it should make sense if lifted out of the page on its own, since GEO prioritizes content that can be retrieved without full page context.
4. Add a quotation or a reference to a trustworthy source where relevant. The source's example cites the original research paper that proposed GEO as a method.
5. Add concrete examples or screenshots to help build authority, as the Story Chief post did.
6. Keep the writing clear and easy to read for both humans and the AI reading it.

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

1. Judging organic search success only by rank and click-through now that a large share of searches end without a click. [f2a1a300d335]
2. Assuming your content will be picked up by AI "by default" without deliberate optimization for clarity, credibility, usefulness and trust.
3. Dropping SEO work because GEO exists; the source is explicit that "you still need SEO."
4. Rushing to overhaul all content overnight instead of evolving the content strategy thoughtfully.
5. Relying on SEO-only signals (alt tags, hashtags, keyword matching) and assuming they make content quotable by AI; those help crawlers find a page, not an AI model quote a passage. [cf7bae2b5009]
