## 1. What this source is for

A short diagnostic script for a founder stuck at a revenue plateau: figure out whether the real constraint is traffic, conversion, or the founder's own missing skill, before hiring anyone. It gives a simple two-archetype model of ecommerce owners (product/sourcing vs. media buying) and argues you should hire to fill whichever side you are not, using equity or non-cash levers if cash margin is thin. It stops short of a hiring process; it is a plateau-diagnosis conversation, not a recruiting playbook.

## 2. Principles

1. **Diagnose the bottleneck before spending on a fix.** A low blended conversion rate can come from bad traffic/targeting or a genuinely weak site; segment conversion by source/campaign before deciding which. Condition: applies when conversion looks low relative to category norms and you're tempted to jump straight to CRO. Evidence: the advisor's stated diagnostic framework, no data shown for this specific business beyond the founder's self-reported 1% figure. [5013501dc466] [c1dfbfcedccd]
2. **Traffic quality can beat CRO as the first lever.** The advisor claims "the easiest way to improve your conversion is just get better people" (traffic), calling CRO-obsession one of the most overlooked failure modes. Condition: only when segmentation shows specific sources/geos/placements converting near zero while others convert normally; do not apply this as a default over CRO. Evidence: the advisor's opinion and mocking example ("these people are in Africa, they're not buying this"), not shown data. [5013501dc466]
3. **Ecommerce owners split into two archetypes: product/sourcing-focused, or media-buying-focused.** Per the advisor, "most people who own e-commerce businesses are either very product brand focused or they're hardcore media buyers" and few are strong at both. Condition: framed as a general pattern the advisor says he sees "on a regular basis," not a study. Use it to locate which side a founder is missing, not as a rule that only two types exist. [5813209a4dbd] [12805cde65c1]
4. **A founder's hedging language is a diagnostic signal.** When the founder says "I don't know" / "I'm not sure" about media buying, the advisor treats that as evidence the skill is missing, not present. Condition: a weak signal only; the source itself cautions a founder could just be modest about a skill they do have. [12805cde65c1]
5. **Sequence: fix the acquisition/revenue gap first, brand second.** The advisor's explicit order for this founder: get media buying working and drive revenue up now; branding into "a household name" is the next stage, after revenue growth. Condition: this is a sequencing opinion for one business with a first-mover product edge (unique memorabilia), not a universal rule; the source itself notes some businesses with stronger differentiation may justify earlier brand investment. [59f0f71e9914]
6. **A sourcing edge can partly substitute for brand, short-term.** Being able to buy underpriced unique inventory and resell high creates a "brand halo" effect on its own. Condition: applies when the product itself is scarce/unique (here, autographed memorabilia); the advisor still warns sourcing edges erode as competitors find the same supply, so it is not a long-term substitute for brand or retention work. [990f21a57b6e]
7. **Fast ecommerce revenue growth strains cash before it helps.** The advisor states ecommerce "scales very easily" with the right acquisition person, but flags cash flow as the problem that "comes up" right after. Model cash separately from revenue before pushing spend. [22f9b83f5cae]
8. **Compensate a scarce, high-value hire with more than base cash when margin is thin.** If margins can't support full market cash comp for a top media buyer, use equity or other non-cash/performance levers (cash, stock, deliberately structured equity) instead of assuming inventory or equity is a free substitute for needed cash comp. [826839431464]

## 3. Procedures

The source gives no numbered hiring process, no job-req steps, and no interview or vetting method. It is a diagnostic conversation, not a procedure. The closest thing to a sequence, reconstructed from the advisor's own ordering in the call:

1. Ask the founder what is limiting growth (their answer here: "leads... and then converting leads"). [c1dfbfcedccd]
2. Split that into two distinct questions: are people reaching the site (traffic/lead volume), and are they converting once there (onsite conversion)? [c1dfbfcedccd] [5013501dc466]
3. Pull conversion by traffic source/campaign, not as one blended number, to see if specific sources are dragging the average down. [5013501dc466]
4. Ask the founder to name their own core skill among the core ecommerce functions (sourcing/product, media buying, brand). Note whether they answer confidently or hedge. [5813209a4dbd] [12805cde65c1]
5. Confirm the strong side is real (here: founder can source memorabilia at ~$200 and resell at ~$2,000) so you know it does not need fixing. [990f21a57b6e]
6. Identify the missing side as the hiring/skill-building priority (here: media buying). [5813209a4dbd] [12805cde65c1]
7. Check current margin ($400k profit on $3M revenue, ~13.3%) to see what cash comp the business can actually support for that hire. [826839431464]
8. Decide: learn the missing skill yourself, or bring in a "star" and pay with a mix of cash, performance pay and/or equity rather than a full market salary the margin can't support. [826839431464]
9. Before pushing for fast growth, plan the cash flow (inventory, ad spend paid ahead of revenue) separately from the revenue target. [22f9b83f5cae]
10. Sequence effort: acquisition/revenue now, brand next. [59f0f71e9914]

No step gives a script for interviewing, comp-structuring the equity grant, or vesting; the source explicitly leaves those out (see section 9).

## 5. Diagnostics and checks

- **Check:** Is conversion low across the board or only on specific sources/geos/placements? **How:** segment conversion rate by traffic source and campaign instead of reading one blended number. **Good:** most sources convert near category norm, a few drag the average down → traffic-mismatch problem, fix targeting first. **Bad:** conversion is uniformly low across all sources → treat as a genuine onsite/CRO problem instead. **Next:** only run CRO tests (PDP, offer, checkout) once traffic is reasonably well matched. [5013501dc466] [c1dfbfcedccd]
- **Check:** Which core ecommerce function do you (the founder) actually own? **How:** ask directly whether you're strong at sourcing/product vs. media buying/acquisition; notice hedging language ("I don't know," "not sure"). **Good:** a confident, evidenced answer on one side. **Bad:** inability to name either clearly, or hedging on the acquisition side. **Next:** treat the unclear/hedged side as the missing capability and prioritize hiring or learning there. [5813209a4dbd] [12805cde65c1]
- **Check:** Can the business afford a top media buyer in cash? **How:** compute current margin (profit ÷ revenue) and compare to reported market pay for strong media buyers ("multiple hundreds of thousands of dollars a year," per the advisor, unverified [web check: current market comp for senior ecommerce media buyers]). **Good:** margin supports full cash comp. **Bad:** margin is thin (e.g., ~13%) relative to the role's market pay. **Next:** structure comp with equity, performance pay or other non-cash levers instead of assuming the margin alone can pay it. [826839431464]
- **Check:** Can cash flow support the growth push you're planning? **How:** model inventory purchases and ad spend timing against expected revenue timing, separate from the revenue projection itself. **Good:** reserves/stock levers cover the gap between spend and collection. **Next:** secure the cash/stock levers before pushing spend up. [22f9b83f5cae]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

- Obsessing over CRO when the real problem is traffic/targeting quality: "the easiest way to improve your conversion is just get better people," not tune the page. [5013501dc466]
- Hiring a generic "A player" or generalist for "management" instead of defining the role around the specific missing function (here, media buying). [12805cde65c1]
- Assuming your sourcing/product edge alone will keep driving growth without adding the missing acquisition capability. [990f21a57b6e]
- Assuming inventory or equity is a free substitute for cash compensation a hire actually needs. The advisor frames equity as something you "give up," not a shortcut. [826839431464]
- Pushing a fast growth sprint without separately planning the cash-flow strain (inventory + ad spend paid ahead of revenue) that follows. [22f9b83f5cae]
- Over-reading a founder's hedging as proof of a skill gap; the source itself warns a founder may just be modest about a skill they actually have. [12805cde65c1]
