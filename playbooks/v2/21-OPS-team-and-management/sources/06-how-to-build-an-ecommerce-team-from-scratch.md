## 1. What this source is for

A short starting checklist for a solo founder or very small team deciding which functions an e-commerce operation needs before or shortly after launch, and in what order to staff them. It gives five role silos, one hiring-order rule (do it yourself first, hire as volume forces it), and a short set of principles for each silo's job (customer service reduces hesitation, fulfillment protects post-purchase emotion, marketing should reflect brand values, developers fix drop-off). It is not a compensation, SOP, org-chart or KPI guide; those are not addressed. It is thin on the "how" and long on the "what" and "why."

## 2. Principles

1. A workable e-commerce operation needs five functional silos: administrator, customer service, order fulfillment, digital marketing, and web development/design. Condition: presented as a starting template for any e-commerce launch, not scaled to order volume or channel mix. Evidence: presenter's opinion, no data. [4c580913957e]
2. The administrator role covers buying contracts, partnerships, reporting, strategy and brand image, and is the one role the founder should default to filling personally at first. Condition: applies "if you're just starting out and don't have a big budget"; a hire replaces the founder here once volume makes it unmanageable. Evidence: presenter's opinion. [98fae764cd32]
3. Customer service exists to resolve buyer confusion so people complete checkout, not to run a general help desk. Condition: applies pre-purchase, on the product page and at checkout. Evidence: presenter's stated line, "a confused mind always says no"; no data. [6168b9f862f4]
4. Purchases are emotional, and that emotion needs to be sustained from checkout through delivery via tracking codes, receipts and packaging, since a long shipping wait can otherwise let the emotion fade. Condition: applies to the fulfillment silo's scope. Evidence: presenter's opinion, no data. [b22ff38bde5c]
5. Marketing hires and campaigns should represent the brand's actual mission and values, because buyers are said to buy from brands they can relate to. Condition: presenter's example is a vegan brand expressing care for animals in ad copy; framed as a positioning principle, not a tested lift. Evidence: presenter's opinion, no data. [a22920b70388]
6. The website behaves like a "leaky bucket": every step from landing to checkout can leak a lead, and the developer/designer role exists to find and seal those leaks by simplifying navigation and checkout. Condition: applies to any storefront with a drop-off problem. Evidence: analogy plus a restaurant-hygiene comparison; no funnel data or benchmark given. [4ccb3499516c]
7. Team member drive ("desire to go to the next level") matters as much as role skill, because a "bad team" is said to prevent good results regardless of process. Condition: presenter's belief, offered without a way to measure "drive" objectively. Evidence: opinion. [94576193b1cf]

## 3. Procedures

The source gives one implied sequence, not a numbered how-to. It is thin on mechanics: no interview questions, no job-ad templates, no SOP steps, no onboarding steps are given anywhere in the text.

**Hiring order**
1. Before hiring anyone, identify and name the five silos: administrator, customer service, fulfillment, digital marketing, web development/design. [4c580913957e]
2. Take the administrator seat yourself if budget is tight, and personally also cover core tasks in the other silos rather than hiring one generalist to weakly cover all five. [98fae764cd32] [4c580913957e]
3. Add people only as order volume and workload force it ("once you're settled"); the source gives no revenue or order-count trigger. [4c580913957e]
4. When you do hire into the administrator role, screen for the ability to coordinate across the other four silos, since the source frames this role's value as cross-functional oversight. [98fae764cd32]
5. When hiring marketing, screen for whether the candidate understands your company's objectives and ethics, not only channel skill. [a22920b70388]
6. When hiring any role, evaluate for drive toward a defined "next level" goal, alongside role competence; the source gives no interview script for this. [94576193b1cf]

No procedure is given for sourcing candidates, wording job posts, structuring interviews, setting pay, writing SOPs, running onboarding, or setting KPIs or incentives, even though the folder's stated scope covers these. This source does not reach them.

## 5. Diagnostics and checks

- Check for an unassigned silo: if any of the five roles (administrator, customer service, fulfillment, marketing, web dev/design) has no owner, named or founder-covered, the source treats this as a launch blocker. [4c580913957e]
- Check incoming pre-purchase questions for repeated confusion themes (sizing, shipping time, returns, materials); recurring questions are a signal to fix the product page or navigation, not just to answer faster. [6168b9f862f4] [4ccb3499516c]
- Check the funnel from landing to checkout for steps where shoppers hesitate, get lost, or abandon; the source calls this finding "leaks in the bucket" but gives no method beyond "map every step." [4ccb3499516c]
- Check live ad copy against the brand's stated mission and values before scaling spend; the source says to reject creative that contradicts brand values even if it performs, but does not say how to weigh that against measured performance. [a22920b70388]
- Check candidates' history for evidence of self-directed goal achievement ("how have you broken through a previous goal") as a proxy for drive; the source gives no rubric for scoring this. [94576193b1cf]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

- Under-hiring into one generalist to cover all five silos instead of the founder personally covering the extra core tasks. [98fae764cd32] [4c580913957e]
- Hiring or scaling a team before defining what "good" looks like for each role, since the source frames a "bad team" as the reason results don't come, implying role clarity should come first.
- Leaving customer questions unanswered on the product page, which the source says causes hesitation and lost sales rather than more support tickets. [6168b9f862f4]
- Letting shipping wait times erode the emotional drive behind a purchase by skipping tracking codes and considered packaging. [b22ff38bde5c]
- Running marketing or ad copy that does not reflect the brand's actual mission and values. [a22920b70388]
- Displaying every product in one place instead of directing shoppers to what they want, which the source says increases drop-off given short attention spans. [4ccb3499516c]
