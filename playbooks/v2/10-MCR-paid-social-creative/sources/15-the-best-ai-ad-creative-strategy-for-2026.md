## 1. What this source is for

It helps an operator use AI image and video tools to make paid social ads that are built on research, not bulk "slop". It gives a model for concepts (avatar + template = ad concept, with creative doing the targeting), a demo of what a context-rich AI run looks like and how to judge its output, a pack-based way to launch new creative into a Meta prospecting campaign, a 7-day minimum spend rule to force tests, and an "iteration engine" that finds winners by incremental revenue and makes big-swing variations. It is thin on how to define an avatar, on kill rules, on how to prompt without the paid tool, and on any evidence for its numbers.

## 2. Principles

1. **AI is an accelerant on top of a creative strategy, not the strategy.** Source says the brands "crushing it right now" use AI to speed up an existing strategy. Condition: you need angles, avatars and research first. Evidence: presenter's claim from his agency and community. [f4b2fe395205]
2. **Volume without quality fails.** The source's opening failure case: a brand makes "50 ads in an afternoon", loads them all, and "nothing is working". "Creating ads and creating winning ads are two completely different things." Condition: applies to any AI workflow fed little context. [f4b2fe395205]
3. **Quality beats volume because 1 winner can change the account.** "One winning ad could genuinely change your entire ad account and your whole business. So my focus here is quality." This is the presenter's reason for paying for a context-rich tool over cheaper prompting. Opinion, no data. [f4b2fe395205]
4. **Deep context is what separates good AI output from slop.** Give the tool your brand URL, your products, and a competitor set, and let it build a brand book (brand guidelines, things to say, things not to say, must-haves, don'ts). The context file "grows as we use more", and the presenter says the tool "gets smarter in time". Source: "You should give this more and more and more information to get a better result." [f4b2fe395205]
5. **Build from proven angles in your niche.** The source's "difference maker" is research specific to the brand's niche (supplement gummies in the demo): the brand's own image ads, then competitors' top ads, then angles drawn from them. Condition: in the demo, "proven" means long-running and high-impression in the public Ads Library, which is not proof of profit (see section 9). [f4b2fe395205] [924a2df7bd2f]
6. **Old way versus new way of targeting.** Old way: pick an audience, make ads for it, group them in an ad set or campaign. New way: "avatars create audiences"; "creative is your targeting"; "avatars automatically find your audience". Meta's algorithm reads the messaging in the creative to find the buyer. Condition: presenter's 2026 view of Meta; no test shown. `[web check: current Meta guidance on broad targeting and creative-led delivery]` [15769b01529d]
7. **Avatar + template = ad concept.** A concept is 1 buyer avatar paired with 1 format or template (for example iPhone Notes, "New Bestie", "60 ingredients, one gummy"). Source says a good tool can do this "all in one shot". [15769b01529d]
8. **Keep messaging intent clean by grouping ads by avatar.** Different avatars go in different ad sets, even inside the same campaign. Source's example: he would not put the (transcribed) "hoop" ads in the same ad set as the hair ads. Condition: assumes avatars are clearly distinct (card caveat). (deployment, neighbour folder 24-DEP) [15769b01529d] [ecb0d8423c9a]
9. **Expect losers even with strong context.** "I don't expect 10 out of 10 every time, but 50%" high quality after 1 click and about 5 minutes "is off the charts". The 50% is the presenter's stated result on 1 run, not a benchmark. [f4b2fe395205] [924a2df7bd2f]
10. **AI does not replace your own creative.** "This is not an all supplement to all creative. You still need to be producing your own creative." No split between AI and human work is given. [f4b2fe395205]
11. **New creative needs guaranteed spend to be tested fairly.** "You can create so many ads, but if the right ones aren't getting spend, you're really not going to get a good result." The fix is the 7-day minimum (section 3.4). The goal stated: limit wasted spend but still give new ads a fair test. (deployment, neighbour folder 24-DEP) [757303dd5a4c]
12. **Judge winners by incremental revenue, not platform-reported totals or Ads Library signals.** Incremental attribution shows "the ads that actually drove revenue incremental to the business that wouldn't have happened without this ad". Condition: requires your own account data; the public Ads Library only shows run time and impressions. `[needs brand data: per-ad incremental revenue and incremental ROAS]` [f8f574a45327]
13. **Iterations must be a real swing.** "Iterations are not one word in the headline changing. Iterations are a decent swing. In old-school Facebook, this would have just been considered a totally new ad." Keep the winning angle; change the execution a lot. [f8f574a45327]
14. **The biggest AI mistake is the "magic button".** "Create a bunch of ads, throw them into your ad account, and hope for the best." The fix is the full loop: research, concept, pack test, forced spend, incremental read, iterate. [f4b2fe395205] [f8f574a45327]
15. **Shared prompts keep a team consistent.** The presenter says a library of pre-built prompts, updated often, lets "everyone be on the same page using the right prompts", and that "creating the prompts are what's going to give you the most outsized result for high-quality creative". Opinion. [924a2df7bd2f]

## 3. Procedures

### 3.1 Set up context before generating (demo steps, tool-specific) [verify UI]
The source shows these steps in Superscale. A different tool will differ. The source says you "could do similar things" with "very complex prompts" in Nano Banana or other tools that are "mostly cheaper or free", but gives no prompt for that route.
1. Create a new brand workspace. [f4b2fe395205]
2. Enter the brand's website URL. The tool analyses the site and competitors and builds a brand book in a "projects in context" panel: branding guidelines, things to say, things not to say, must-haves, don'ts. In the demo it added 10 products to the workspace on the first output. [f4b2fe395205]
3. Pick the product the ads are for (demo: the flagship adult gummies), so outputs are specific to it. [f4b2fe395205]
4. Add more context over time. The demo added none beyond the URL; the presenter says you should add "more and more" for better results. `[needs brand data: brand voice, claims allowed, product facts, avatars, competitor list]` [f4b2fe395205]
5. Optional: connect the Meta ad account so the research can rank ads by real KPIs (conversions, purchases, cost per result, ROAS) instead of Ads Library relevance or impressions. The source says his agency does this for managed brands. Under ecomOS rules, any such connection must be read-only. `[verify UI]` [f4b2fe395205]

### 3.2 Generate a round of static concepts (demo steps) [verify UI]
1. Run a 1-line request, for example "create static ads for [brand] [product]". [f4b2fe395205]
2. The tool researches in this order, per the demo: the brand's own image ads and what is working; the niche (supplement gummies, plus greens powders such as AG1); a matched competitor set "based on brands that are actually working" (demo: AG1, Olly, Ritual, Bloom, SmartyPants, Nature Made); then each competitor's top ads by "what's run the longest and what's getting impressions" in the Ads Library (demo: AG1, Lemme, Goli). In the demo this ran about 5 minutes. [f4b2fe395205]
3. The tool names angles, then outputs concepts labelled by concept and angle, each with an "inspired by" reference. Demo output: 9 ad concepts. [924a2df7bd2f]
4. Review each concept (section 5.1). Keep, skip, or regenerate. [924a2df7bd2f]
5. Resize kept concepts: the demo turns a 4x5 into a 9x16 with 1 button. `[verify UI]` [924a2df7bd2f]
6. For repeat jobs, use the tool's "expert library" prompts (for example "winning ad iteration"): click use, enter the brand and product, add context, and save the prompt. The source also names built-in "skills" for top of funnel creative testing and weekly creative briefs, described as "brand new". `[verify UI]` [924a2df7bd2f]
7. For video, the source names a video library with "dozens and dozens of characters" pre-built, plus access to Sora and Seedance. The source gives no video steps. `[verify UI]` `[web check: current video models in the tool]`

### 3.3 Build packs in the prospecting campaign (deployment, neighbour folder 24-DEP) [verify UI]
1. Keep 2 campaigns: prospecting and retention. Test new creative only in prospecting. [ecb0d8423c9a]
2. Each new round of creative goes in a new ad set, called a "pack" (demo names: broad pack one, broad pack two, broad pack three). "New ads, new pack. New concept, new pack." [ecb0d8423c9a]
3. Group each pack by avatar. Do not mix avatars in 1 ad set. Different avatars' packs can sit in the same campaign. [ecb0d8423c9a] [15769b01529d]
4. Put 2 to 10 creatives in a pack; aim for 4 to 8. Source's reason: "enough angles these days to actually test adequately without overcompensating with too many different angles". [ecb0d8423c9a]
5. Over time, pause, keep or change packs. Pack count grows with the account: "one pack for you today" up to "100 packs for you in the future". [ecb0d8423c9a]
The source gives no audience, bid, placement or campaign budget settings here; it points to a separate video for "the exact settings".

### 3.4 Force spend with a 7-day minimum (deployment, neighbour folder 24-DEP) [verify UI]
1. Open the new pack's ad set and go to the budget section. Find "ad set spending limits". [757303dd5a4c]
2. Choose a dollar value amount and set the minimum to 1x your target CPA. Source's example: CPA target $50, enter $50. `[needs brand data: target CPA]` [757303dd5a4c]
3. If 1x CPA is too big a share of budget (source's example: $50 would be "like 30% of your budget"), cap the minimum at 20% of budget. The source says "budget" without saying daily or campaign; the card reads it as 20% of the daily budget. `[needs brand data: daily budget of the prospecting campaign]` [757303dd5a4c]
4. Leave the minimum on for 7 days. [757303dd5a4c]
5. After 7 days, remove the minimum and let the pack run freely. Winners "spend over the amount"; losers "fade and scale down". [757303dd5a4c]
Derived arithmetic, not stated in the source: if the minimum is a daily figure, each pack is pushed to spend at least 7x target CPA over the week ($350 at a $50 CPA). Check this against total budget before launching several packs at once. Ad set spending limits are tied to a campaign-level budget in Meta; confirm the setting exists for your campaign type. `[verify UI]`

### 3.5 Run the iteration engine (measurement part is deployment, neighbour folder 24-DEP) [verify UI]
1. Open the main prospecting campaign and view all ads. [f8f574a45327]
2. Open the column set, choose "compare attribution window", then "incremental attribution". `[verify UI]` `[web check: whether Meta's incremental attribution setting is available for the account]` [f8f574a45327]
3. Rank ads by incremental revenue and incremental ROAS. Take the top ads. `[needs brand data: incremental revenue and ROAS per ad]` [f8f574a45327]
4. Make variations of each winner, either by feeding it back into the AI tool and asking for variations, or by editing it by hand. "Either way is completely fine." [f8f574a45327]
5. Make each variation a "decent swing", not a 1-word headline change. [f8f574a45327]
6. Launch the variations as a new pack (section 3.3), with the 7-day minimum (section 3.4). This link is stated on the card; the source implies it through "new ads, new pack". [f8f574a45327] [ecb0d8423c9a]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

1. Generating many ads fast (50 in an afternoon), loading them all, and hoping. Fix: context-rich generation plus a structured test. [f4b2fe395205]
2. Treating AI as a "magic button" or as a standalone creative strategy. Fix: use it as an accelerant on a strategy. [f4b2fe395205] [f8f574a45327]
3. Feeding AI too little context, which yields "slop" that looks fine but does not perform. Fix: brand URL, products, competitors, brand book, and more over time. [f4b2fe395205]
4. Believing every AI output is good. "You think it all looks good, but most of the ads are actually just slop." Fix: review and cut; expect about half to be usable at best. [f4b2fe395205] [924a2df7bd2f]
5. Building ads for demographic audiences (old way) instead of avatar-based concepts. [15769b01529d]
6. Mixing avatars in 1 ad set, which blurs the message the algorithm reads. [15769b01529d] [ecb0d8423c9a]
7. Launching new ads without forcing spend, so "the right ones aren't getting spend". [757303dd5a4c]
8. Leaving the minimum on past 7 days (implied by the "remove it" step). [757303dd5a4c]
9. Making "iterations" that change 1 word in a headline. [f8f574a45327]
10. Judging ads by Ads Library relevance or impressions instead of real account KPIs and incremental revenue. [f4b2fe395205] [f8f574a45327]
11. Dropping your own creative production because AI exists. [f4b2fe395205]

## 9. Caveats, conflicts and what is missing

- **Sponsored feel.** Much of the source demos 1 paid tool. The claims that it is "so much better than basically every other AI platform" and "probably the best right now" are the presenter's, from "the ones that I've used". Do not treat tool choice as a rule. The method (context, angles, avatars, review) is portable; the button steps are not. [f4b2fe395205]
- **"Proven" is loose.** The demo ranks competitor ads by run time and impressions from the Ads Library. The source itself says this is weaker than account KPIs. Under ecomOS rules, competitor ads are signals for layout and mood only; never copy text, logos, products or faces. The "inspired by" output should be rewritten, not cloned. [f4b2fe395205] [15769b01529d]
- **The 50% figure** comes from 1 run the presenter scored himself. [f4b2fe395205]
- **Minimum spend rule is ambiguous.** The source does not say if the 1x CPA minimum is daily or lifetime, or if the 20% cap is of daily or campaign budget. It does not say if the campaign uses campaign-level budget, which ad set spending limits need `[verify UI]`. It gives no rule for what counts as a loser after 7 days or when to pause. [757303dd5a4c]
- **Incremental attribution is named but not explained.** No guidance on data needed, how it differs from the default window, or what incremental ROAS counts as a winner. `[web check: how Meta computes incremental attribution and its limits]` [f8f574a45327]
- **Avatar method missing.** The source says to build around avatars but never says how to find, write or validate one. `[needs brand data: customer research, reviews, avatar list]` [15769b01529d]
- **Cheap route missing.** Nano Banana and "very complex prompts" are mentioned with no prompt given.
- **Video missing.** Video is named (character library, Sora, Seedance) but the source says it focused on statics. No video process.
- **Claims risk not raised.** The demo concepts use GLP-1, "clinically studied" and gut-health lines. The source does not discuss ad policy or proof of claims. `[web check: Meta policy on weight-loss, GLP-1 and health claims]` `[needs brand data: substantiated claims]`
- **Conflict with source 05 in this folder (The Best Facebook Ad Creative Strategy for 2027).** Source 05 says to put all awareness stages in 1 ad set and not split audiences; this source splits new creative into many ad sets (packs) by avatar and round. These can coexist (05 splits by stage, this one by avatar), but a merge must not flatten them into 1 rule. Source 05 also warns against making many small variants of 1 winner; this source's iteration engine builds on winners, but demands a "decent swing", which is close to 05's "distinct formats". Source 05 keeps weak ads live if they fill a funnel slot; this source lets losers "fade" and says "you're going to pause things".
- **Conflict with common ecommerce advice.** Many operators test new creative in a separate testing campaign, or avoid ad set minimums because they constrain campaign budget delivery. This source tests inside the main prospecting campaign and uses a forced minimum for 7 days. Neither side is shown with data here.
- **Deployment detail deferred.** The source points to another video for "the exact settings, step-by-step". Budget levels, bids, audiences and placements are not given.
