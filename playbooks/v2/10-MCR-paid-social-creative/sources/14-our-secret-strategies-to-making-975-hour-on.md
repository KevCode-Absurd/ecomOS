## 3. Procedures

### 3.1 Plan and promote an event show (source's sequence)

1. Pick the show date 1 week or more ahead; 2 to 3 weeks is better. Schedule it in Whatnot so viewers can bookmark it. `[verify UI]` [2178d2373f73]
2. Build the offer stack: about 20% high-value named-brand items (source: 50 of 250, worth $50 to $150), about 80% low-cost fill, 1 big buyer-only giveaway prize, 1 small early giveaway. [cbe5d83e59c4] [182b43a99fbb]
3. Decide starting bids. $1 only if you expect a big, excited room (300 to 500 concurrent). Otherwise start higher. [d3129385baa4]
4. Make a static thumbnail in Canva (free). Put the biggest incentive in big text ("FREE iPad"). Add recognizable brand names of items, all "starting at $1". Keep it the same message as your posts. `[verify UI]` for Whatnot thumbnail size and rules. [e9faabac799f]
5. Promote on every channel you have: personal Facebook page, reseller Facebook groups, Instagram stories, YouTube videos. The source mentioned the show in 2 YouTube videos and posted about 3 Instagram stories. [2178d2373f73]
6. Time the posts: about 1 week before, 2 to 3 days before, and a few hours before. Message: "we're selling really good stuff, this date and time, don't miss it", plus $1 starts and the giveaway. [2178d2373f73] [182b43a99fbb]
7. Total your inventory cost including the giveaway prize. That total is your cost base (source: about $1,000 including the iPad). See the break-even caveat in section 9. [aa0ac3a2a24f]

### 3.2 Run the show

1. Dress up or wear a silly hat. The source keeps a rack of Halloween costumes. [3294414ef306]
2. Play background music at low volume. Loud music distracts. Its job is to help the seller keep talking and cut dead air. [3294414ef306]
3. Run a small giveaway at the start (source: Hello Kitty earrings). [182b43a99fbb]
4. Run auctions on a short timer. The source says auctions are "usually 15 to 20 seconds" and "we start things out at 15 seconds"; bids add time. `[verify UI]` for timer and extension settings. [dab37b41c061]
5. Ask first-time viewers to say so in chat. Several times in the show. In this show 12 to 25 people replied "first time". [3294414ef306] [bf03c599cedc]
6. Never sit idle. If nothing else, dance or chat. [3294414ef306]
7. Run the big buyer-only giveaway at the end. [182b43a99fbb]

### 3.3 After the show

1. Pull: items sold, gross sales, ASP, buyers, first-time buyers, peak concurrent viewers, total viewers, show length. [bf03c599cedc] [aa0ac3a2a24f]
2. Take payout after platform fees, shipping adjustments and rewards or coupon spend. `[web check: current Whatnot seller fee rates]` [aa0ac3a2a24f]
3. Subtract inventory cost (with giveaway prize) and shipping labor. Source: helper Kim, 6 to 7 hours at $25 an hour over 2 days. [aa0ac3a2a24f] [dab37b41c061]
4. Compare with routine shows to see what the promotion added. [bf03c599cedc]

## 8. Mistakes the source warns about

- Starting at $1 without a big room. Most sellers should not. [d3129385baa4]
- Advertising $1 starts on cheap items. People "don't really care". [2178d2373f73]
- Sitting on camera doing nothing. "The only thing you don't want to do." [3294414ef306]
- Loud music. It distracts. [3294414ef306]
- Forced humor that chat does not enjoy. It reads as cringe. [3294414ef306]
- Under-planning show length. Talking and "soap boxes" added about 1 hour. [dab37b41c061]
- Assuming you need an outside social following to succeed on Whatnot. [be1a2cbb5a77]
