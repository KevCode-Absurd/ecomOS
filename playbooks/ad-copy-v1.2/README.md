# Ad-copy playbook 1.2 — experimental

Revised after two blinded AI reviewers identified catalog repetition, weak picturable situations and insufficiently distinct variants. Uses specific imagery, credible feature-to-benefit translation and clear headlines from three additional cards read in full.

The system prompt is shorter than the previous instruction-plus-principle package and explicitly separates the angles before drafting. Full supporting cards remain optional. Original sources, v1.1 and prior outputs are unchanged.

This version is experimental until the new results are reviewed. No promise of increased conversion or performance is made. New brand facts were not introduced.
