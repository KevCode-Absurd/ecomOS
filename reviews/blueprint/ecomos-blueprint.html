ecomOS Blueprint
Draft for Kevin, 25 Sep 2026

An operating system that builds and runs an e-commerce brand, with the founder deciding.

A founder connects a brand, or starts one from nothing. ecomOS reads the brand's own data, works out where money is leaking, ranks what to do next, and puts a short list in front of the founder. The founder approves. Agents do the work, measure it, and learn. Every day the list is fresh.

This page has 5 views. The big picture, the journey for a founder starting from zero, the journey for an existing brand, the agency running many brands, and the machinery underneath. Screens are drawn as the product would look. Numbers on screens are real where we have them (the Gehna speed lab) and marked illustrative where we do not.

Door A: I want to start a brandNo product, no store. ecomOS runs the research, picks with you, sets up the store, launches the channels, then runs the daily loop.
Door B: I already run a brandAdd the website, connect the data, answer some questions. ecomOS builds the brand brain, finds the break in the pipeline, and hands you today's top 10.
Door C: I run brands for clientsOne workspace, many brands. Each brand keeps its own brain and gate. The agency adds a portfolio, approval chains, shared specialists and client reports.
The problem

Running a brand well needs about 25 specialist skills. Founders have 2 or 3.

Advice is generic

Agencies, courses and AI chat give the same answer to every brand. None of them look at this brand's orders, ads, emails and site before speaking.

Data sits in silos

Shopify, Meta, Google, Klaviyo, support, the bank. Nobody joins them, so nobody can say where the leak is or what a fix was worth.

Nothing is measured

Changes ship without a read point. Wins and losses are forgotten. The same mistake is made again by the next hire.

The product

One loop, run every day, on the brand's own numbers.

What makes it different

Four things together, not one of them.

The brand's own data first

Diagnosis comes from the metric contract, never from what is fashionable. Missing data shows as unavailable, never as 0. Windows end on the last full day.

An expert corpus turned into ideas

7,611 cards from 256 expert documents, distilled into 4,650 structured ideas across 25 domains. Each idea carries its signal, expected effect, effort, risk and test. Filtered and scored in code.

The founder decides, in plain English

Every proposal is a heading plus a short explanation. Three buttons: let the agent do it, send to a person, not now. This is the control surface of the whole system.

Everything is measured

Every approved idea gets a registry entry, a read point, a kill and scale rule. Results feed back into ranking. The system gets better for this brand over time.

Business model

Open decision. Three shapes fit the product.

Per brand, monthly

One workspace per brand. Priced by tier: brain plus daily priorities, then execution agents, then team seats. Simple, fits Door B.

Build plus run

Door A founders pay for the build journey as a package, then move to the monthly plan when the store launches.

Agency licence

Door C. Agencies run many brands in one account. Priced per active brand, with domain tools for their specialists.

Not decided: prices, tiers, whether execution is metered by run. No numbers are proposed here on purpose. The first paying shape should follow the first brand that pays, which today is Gehna through Door B.
Rules that hold everywhere

Guardrails the whole system obeys.

  • Meta is read-only until the founder switches writes on per surface. Until then every execution is a brief, a draft, a change list or a pull request.
  • Arithmetic in code, never in the model. Period ROAS is total value over total spend. Totals stay separate from breakdowns.
  • Public web research stays separate from private brand data, and every fetched fact carries its URL and date.
  • Competitor ads are signals. Layout and mood only. Never their text, logos, products or faces.
  • No invented claims or numbers. Every claim traces to the brand record, in 3 tiers: approved, site-backed, forbidden.
  • Writing on every founder surface: short sentences, digits, no jargon, below tenth-grade reading level.

STEP 4The brand brain, and how it keeps improving

The brain is a set of sections the founder can read, each with a freshness stamp and a confidence. It is not a document that goes stale. Collectors re-read the site and the sources on a schedule, diff against the last snapshot, and raise questions when something changes. Every founder decision is stored once and quoted by every agent after that.

Reads, corrects, answers open questions when they have time.

What the agency door adds to the architecture

  • Workspace above brands. One agency record, many brand brains. A brain never knows about another brain.
  • Approval chain per brand. The founder gate splits by decision type. Money, external writes and red lines default to the client.

The agents

3 run the loop. 3 keep it honest. 8 do the work.

Run the loop

  • Orchestrator. The account manager. Schedules the daily run, routes approved items to the right specialist, keeps the founder's queue, answers in the chat.
  • Diagnose. Reads the metric contract. Outputs ranked breaks with proof and money at stake. Never proposes fixes.
  • Propose. Audit, candidates from bank, audit and web, rank by marginal improvement in code, write the cards.
  • Registrar. Logs approvals, schedules read points, records results, updates brand priors.

Keep it honest

  • Brand Brain Keeper. Collectors, site diffs, claim tiers, decisions log, open questions. Mostly scripts.
  • Reference Scout. 3 to 8 references per approved idea, tagged, mood and structure only.
  • QA Gate. Before anything is delivered: claims traced, numbers recomputed, windows on full days, reading level, no competitor text, no placeholders, Meta read-only.

Do the work

  • Site 05, 07, 08, 09: briefs, page specs, tests.
  • Paid creative and copy 10, 11: concepts, briefs, copy sets.
  • Media buyer 24, 12: structure, budgets, change lists.
  • Lifecycle 14, 17, 03: flows, campaigns, offers.
  • Organic and search 13, 15, 16.
  • Product and supply 01, 18, 22.
  • Money and ops 20, 21, 06, 23.
  • Brand strategist 02, 04, 19.

Today's plan page pattern (3 buttons, saved decisions) inside the app, or the gallery page extended. This draft assumes one app with rooms.

Meta, the live site, the email platform. Per surface, by the founder. Until then every execution is a brief or a change list a person applies.

Per brand monthly, build plus run, or agency licence. Follow the first paying brand.
