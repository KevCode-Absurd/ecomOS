# Gehna Meta copy plan: 12 ads and a 4-week test

Prepared 24 Sep 2026. Built on `audit.md` and the tables in `raw/`. The 12 ads are also saved as `raw/ad_sets.json`; `raw/check_ads.py` confirms every length rule (first line under 125 characters, body under 300 words, headline under 40, description under 30, at most 4 emojis in a body and 1 in a headline). Anything marked `[confirm before launch]` is true on the site today but is a price, a count, a stock state or a policy detail that must be checked live on launch day.

## 1. Brief

### Who buys

An Indian woman, 25 to 54, with the best returns from 35 to 54. She buys for herself first and as a gift second. She wants real gold and real stones at a weight she can wear daily, and she is not sure it is safe to buy fine jewellery from a website. The average order is about ₹35,000, so a single order matters and a single doubt kills it. A second buyer is the gift-giver, often a man, who is in the account today by accident (28% of spend at ROAS 1.53) and has never been spoken to as a gift-giver.

### The buying question and the one idea, per objective

| Objective | The question in her head | The one idea every ad answers |
|---|---|---|
| (a) New buyers | "Is this real gold and real stones, and what happens if it is wrong?" | Real gold, real stones, and a real way out. Every ad names the material, the hallmark and the 7-day refund, then shows a product she can start with. |
| (b) Visitors and cart abandoners | "I liked it. Why did I not buy?" Size, delivery, money, trust. | Answer the one reason she stopped. Each ad takes one objection the site already solves (delivery time, ring size, monthly parts, other buyers' doubt) and puts it in the first line. |
| (c) Past customers | "What next, and is it worth going back?" | Your next piece is easier than your first. Same hands, same hallmark, plus things only a customer can use: complete the set, new arrivals in your size, gift the box you know, exchange the old piece. |

### Why these ideas, from the data

- The best non-sale texts in the account both name a worry in line 1 and answer it with proof: "Buying fine gold online is not as scary as you think" (ROAS 12.6 in 90 days) and "Forget the guesswork. The best gift for her..." (9.1). Brand-first lines ("From Our Hands to Yours", "Redesigning Beauty") are in the bottom 10.
- Questions and offers out-earn statements (12-month ROAS 5.87 and 4.99 against 2.70). The account has no standing offer, but the site has free shipping, COD, instalments, a free ring sizer and 7-day refunds. These are offers that cost nothing and are true every day.
- Retargeting returns 4.6 to 6.5 on one text that has not changed in 18 months and still says "5000+ customers". Fresh, specific objection copy is the cheapest test in the plan.
- Past customers have no campaign at all. Site reviews say "I have bought so many pieces from them and will continue to do so." That is a list waiting for a message.
- Earrings are proven (46% of 90-day value). Chains and rings have the most designs and the least sales. The plan leans on earrings for cold traffic and uses chains and rings where the ad can answer the objection that has blocked them (weight, size).

### Voice rules used in every ad

1. Warm, confident, short. Talk to one woman. One idea per line.
2. Name the material: 18K gold, BIS Hallmarked, natural gemstones.
3. Open with a plain question, a real customer quote, or a short rhythmic line. Never open with the brand name.
4. Stack proof at the end: hallmark, reviews, free shipping, easy returns.
5. Digits for numbers. No em dashes. No "unmatched", "opulent", "exquisite", "transcendent".
6. 1 emoji at the end of a line, at most 4 in a caption, 1 in a headline, from the brand's set (✨ 💎 💍 💖 💫 🌸 ⭐ 💛 🛍️) plus 🎁 for gifting and 💬 for quotes.
7. Concrete before-to-after: "wear it for 7 days, then decide", "pay 20% now", "order when it fits".

### Claims allowed

Approved without checks: BIS Hallmarked; 18K gold (14K on some chains and earrings); natural gemstones; handcrafted; 4.8 on Google; 10,000+ customers; free shipping (India); 7-day easy returns; 20+ years of craft.

Backed by a named site page and used with `[confirm before launch]`: full refund within 7 days (shipping page); free gift packing (shipping page); insured transit and ID on delivery (shipping page, FAQ); 2 to 3 day dispatch in stock, 10 to 14 days made to order (FAQ); cash on delivery (FAQ); instalments with 20% down over 2 to 6 months, no documents, delivery within 4 working days of the last payment (FAQ, product pages); free ring sizer and one free resize within 15 days (FAQ, ring product page); lifetime exchange at 70% (gemstone) or 80% (diamond) of value with invoice (shipping page); lifetime repair across India (product pages); design counts and prices as read on 24 Sep 2026.

Not used anywhere: discounts, sale dates, "India's largest", "astrologically powerful", "100% handcrafted", "buyback", "IGI certified", "5,000+" or "8,500" or "25,000+" or "15 years", lab-grown or diamond claims on products that do not have them.

## 3. The 4-week test plan

### Budget

Current monthly spend is ₹10,81,774, computed as the last 90 days (₹32,45,323, 26 Jun to 23 Sep 2026) divided by 3. The last 3 full months were ₹10,06,127, ₹10,19,234 and ₹13,10,492, and September is running at ₹34,759 a day, so the figure is in the middle of the range.

| Bucket | Share | 28 days | Per day | Per ad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existing campaigns, untouched (the controls) | 70% | ₹7,57,242 | ₹27,044 | as today |
| Test, new buyers (4 ads) | 14% | ₹1,51,448 | ₹5,409 | ₹37,862 over 28 days |
| Test, visitors and carts (4 ads) | 9% | ₹97,360 | ₹3,477 | ₹24,340 over 28 days |
| Test, past customers (4 ads, 2 at a time) | 7% | ₹75,724 | ₹2,704 | ₹18,931 over 14 days |
| Test total | 30% | ₹3,24,532 | ₹11,590 | |

The 30% test share is the largest slice that keeps every existing winner funded. The split between objectives follows how much each ad needs to clear its spend floor: new-buyer ads need ₹30,000 to be judged, retargeting ads ₹20,000, and past-customer ads run to a smaller list, so 2 run at a time.

### Structure

Three new campaigns, all Sales objective, optimised for purchases, manual (ad set) budgets so every ad gets an even spend. No existing campaign, ad set or ad is edited, paused or rebudgeted. One ad per ad set, so the copy is the only variable inside each campaign.

Campaign T1, new buyers. 4 ad sets, ₹1,352 a day each. Audience: India, women 25 to 54, broad, excluding site visitors 180 days, purchasers 180 days and the customer list. NB-2 runs to men and women 25 to 54 with the same exclusions, because it is the gift ad. Placement: Advantage+ placements. Creative: the existing earrings video for NB-1 and NB-2, the existing earrings "Educational" flexible assets for NB-3, and the chains dynamic images for NB-4, so the picture is a known quantity and the words are the test.

Campaign T2, visitors and carts. 4 ad sets, ₹869 a day each. Audience: site visitors 30 days plus add-to-cart 30 days, excluding purchasers 30 days. RT-2 is narrowed to people who viewed a ring product page in 30 days if the pixel has content-category events; if not, it runs to the same audience as the others. RT-1 uses the product catalogue (dynamic) with the copy above; the others use the best-seller carousel images.

Campaign T3, past customers. 2 ad sets at a time, ₹1,352 a day each. Audience: the customer list (email and phone from orders, 365 days) plus pixel purchasers 180 days, excluding purchasers 14 days. Round 1, weeks 1 and 2: PC-1 and PC-4. Round 2, weeks 3 and 4: PC-2 and PC-3. Frequency cap 2 per 7 days, since the list is small.

### Controls

New buyers are judged against the ads in "Earrings Ads, Combo Audiences, 25/06", the account's best live prospecting campaign: Gifting (ad 120244547906670138: ₹1,91,542, 43 purchases, ROAS 9.14 in 90 days), Trust overload (120244547906730138: ROAS 12.60) and Social proof (120244547906610138: ₹2,93,100, 30 purchases, ROAS 3.09). The campaign as a whole ran ₹10,48,883 for ROAS 5.16 and CPA ₹6,946 in 90 days. These keep running at their current budgets and their 4-week numbers are read side by side with T1.

Visitors and carts are judged against the two live catalogue retargeting ads: BOF "Did something catch your eye?" (120205890645610138: ₹2,69,597, 40 purchases, ROAS 4.46, CPA ₹6,740 in 90 days) and the MOF copy of it (120245468754780138: ROAS 5.11).

Past customers have no control in the account. They are judged against the retargeting baseline below and, more simply, on whether they buy at all.

### Baselines and thresholds

All from `raw/baselines.json`, 26 Jun to 23 Sep 2026, ad set default attribution.

- Account: spend ₹32,45,323, 348 purchases, value ₹1,22,21,710, ROAS 3.77, CPA ₹9,326, CTR 4.03%, CPM ₹33.
- Prospecting (TOF campaigns): ROAS 3.63, CPA ₹9,983, CTR 4.09%.
- Retargeting (BOF plus MOF campaigns): spend ₹4,93,785, 73 purchases, value ₹22,58,272, ROAS 4.57, CPA ₹6,764.
- Ads above the ₹30,000 floor in 90 days: 27; their ROAS quartiles are 1.49, 3.09, 5.49 and their CTR quartiles 2.88%, 3.98%, 6.04%.

The rules use 60% and 120% of the matching baseline. 60% is roughly the bottom quartile of live ads, so a kill removes what the account itself would rank in its worst 25%. 120% is above the median and needs at least 3 purchases, so a scale needs real orders, not one lucky ₹1 lakh sale.

| Objective | Learning spend before any ROAS call | Kill | Hold | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New buyers | ₹30,000 per ad (3 × prospecting CPA) | ROAS under 2.18, or CTR under 2.45% | ROAS 2.18 to 4.36 | ROAS over 4.36 with 3+ purchases and CPA under ₹7,986 |
| Visitors and carts | ₹20,000 per ad (3 × retargeting CPA) | ROAS under 2.74 | ROAS 2.74 to 5.49 | ROAS over 5.49 with 3+ purchases and CPA under ₹5,411 |
| Past customers | ₹15,000 per ad | 0 purchases at ₹15,000, or ROAS under 2.26 at ₹20,000 | ROAS 2.26 to 4.52 | ROAS over 4.52 with 3+ purchases |

Early stop, any objective: CTR under 2.0% (half the account average) after ₹10,000, because no ad in the top 10 of the year is below that.

Scaling means raising that ad set's daily budget by 20% every 3 days while it stays above the scale line, funded first from killed ads in the same campaign, never from the control campaigns. If every ad in a campaign is killed, its budget returns to the controls.

The test runs into the festive season (Navratri starts in mid October, Diwali is 8 Nov 2026), which lifts every number in the account. So the decision at week 4 is made on the gap between each test ad and its control over the same 4 weeks, not on the 90-day baseline alone. The baseline thresholds are the floor; the control comparison is the verdict.

### What to measure, and when

Every ad, every day, read on Mondays: spend, impressions, CTR, CPM, frequency, add-to-cart count and cost, purchases, purchase value, ROAS, CPA, all with the same attribution setting as the control. For T1 also the share of purchases that Meta marks as new customers, if the conversion is set up; for T3, orders matched to the customer list in the store back end, which is the only honest read for that campaign.

Week 1 (launch Monday): build audiences and upload the customer list on the Friday before; confirm every `[confirm before launch]` item; launch T1, T2 and T3 round 1 on Monday. No ROAS calls this week. Only the ₹10,000 CTR stop applies.

Week 2: first kill and hold calls on ads that have crossed their learning spend. Note which first lines get the highest CTR at equal spend. At the end of the week, pause T3 round 1 and launch round 2.

Week 3: kill and scale calls on everything in T1 and T2. Move freed budget to the leader in the same campaign. Write one variant of the leading first line for each objective, ready for the next round, but do not launch it yet.

Week 4: final read. For each objective, rank the 4 ads on ROAS with CPA and purchases beside them, and put the control's same-week numbers in the same table. Recommend which ads go into the control campaigns as permanent ads from week 5, which get a second round with a new picture, and which are retired.

### What the owner gets each week

A one-page sheet every Monday: the table above for all 12 ads and the 5 control ads; the calls made (kill, hold, scale) with the number that triggered each; total test spend against the ₹3,24,532 plan; any claim that needed a change; and 3 lines on what the first lines are teaching. At week 4, a short memo with the ranking, the ads to keep, the next 4 first lines to test, and the budget the winners should get in month 2.

## 4. Decisions for the owner
