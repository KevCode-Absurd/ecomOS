# Gehna on Meta: what the copy has done, and what to run next

Prepared 24 September 2026 for the owners of Gehna (gehnaindia.com). Ad account data covers 23 September 2025 to 24 September 2026, 472 ads, in rupees. Site facts were read from gehnaindia.com on 24 September 2026. All arithmetic was done in code. Where Meta did not report a purchase for an ad, the figure is shown as unavailable, not as 0.

## 1. The short version

Over 12 months the account spent ₹1,29,59,908 and reported 1,396 purchases worth ₹4,24,86,365, a return of 3.28 times spend at ₹9,284 per purchase. The last 90 days were better: ₹32,24,679 spent, 347 purchases, ₹1,29,42,450 in value, 4.01 times spend. The last 30 days were the best of the year at 5.64.

Three things drive the returns, and the copy plan below is built on them:

1. Proof and occasion sell; mood does not. Ads that name a real reason to trust (4.8 on Google, hallmark, returns), a real occasion (a gift), or a real dated offer returned 4 to 12 times spend. Ads that showcase a product with mood words, tell the founder's story, or send the catalog cold returned 0.4 to 2.2.
2. Earrings are the workhorse. They took 18.9% of spend and 22.1% of purchases, and returned 4.19 over the year and 4.85 over 90 days. Rings had 45 ads and 3 purchases. Chains, the highest ticket, returned 2.20 cold and 5.15 in the sale.
3. The funnel is thin at both ends. 86% of spend is prospecting at 2.84. The whole bottom of the funnel is 3 catalog ads with 1 text, live for a year and slowing. There is no past-customer campaign, past buyers are not excluded from cold spend, and the 30-character description slot has never been used on any of the 472 ads.

The plan: 12 new ad sets, 4 each for new buyers, site visitors and past customers, run for 4 weeks on 25% of current monthly spend (₹2,78,000), each judged against the account's own baselines, with a decision table written before launch.

## 6. What is missing or weak

- Audience. Past buyers are not excluded from cold spend, and warm audiences sit inside prospecting campaigns. Men take 28% of spend at 1.53; ages 18 to 24 take 13% at 1.17.
- Funnel. Retargeting is 1 catalog text from September 2025; its return over the last 30 days is 2.78 against 4.50 over 90 days. There is no ad that answers a cart abandoner's objection, no past-customer campaign, and no ad about the after-sale promises the site already makes (warranty, re-sizing, repair). Attribution settings differ between campaigns, so returns are not comparable.
- Offer. The only offers that ran are dated sales, which returned 5 to 9 times spend and then stopped. The site's standing offers (free shipping, gift packing, EMI, cash on delivery, free ring sizer) are stated inconsistently and never in the description slot.
- Proof. The counts contradict each other. The product facts that convince a considered buyer (gold weight, stone, setting, delivery date, monthly EMI) sit on every product page and in no ad.
- Product. Half of spend is "mixed range". Earrings, the best group, is the only one with both a proof-led and a gift-led ad. Rings never addressed the reason rings fail online, which the site already solves with a free sizer and free re-sizing.
- Craft. The most-spent headline does not say what is sold. Eight texts use "Gehna" as the headline. Descriptions are empty on all 472 ads.

## 7. The brief for the new copy

**The buyer.** A woman in India, 25 to 54, buying real gold for herself or as a gift. She wants a weight and price she can wear daily, colour from natural stones, and a reason to trust a jeweller she has not touched.

**The buying question and the one idea, per objective.**

- New buyers ask "Is this real gold, and what if I don't like it?" Idea: real 18K gold you can wear every day, with 7 days to send it back.
- Site visitors and cart abandoners ask "Is it worth the price, and is it safe to pay?" Idea: the piece you looked at is still there, and every reason not to buy has an answer.
- Past customers ask "Why buy the next one here?" Idea: your next piece is easier than your first.

**Voice.** Warm, confident, short. One woman, not "customers". Name the material. Proof at the end. 1 emoji at the end of a line, at most 4 per text and 1 in a headline. Digits. No luxury jargon. No discounts, prices or dates that are not confirmed on the day.

**Claims used.** Only the approved list plus facts read on the site: free gift packing; insured transit; tamper-proof box; full refund or exchange within 7 days of delivery with the return courier paid by the customer; cash on delivery, cards and net banking; EMI shown on the product page; 2 to 3 day delivery for in-stock pieces and 10 to 14 days made to order; free ring sizer and one free re-sizing within 15 days; 1-year workmanship warranty; 1-inch adjustable loop on chains; the lifetime exchange policy with its exact limits; custom design and 1-on-1 styling. Anything that must be checked on launch day is marked [confirm before launch].

## 9. The 4-week plan

**Structure.** Three campaigns, sales objective, 7-day click attribution on all three, ad set budgets, launched on a Monday so the weekly reads line up. The existing campaigns keep running unchanged.

| Campaign | Ad sets | Audience | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| New buyers | NB-01 to NB-04, plus the control | Broad, India | Purchasers 730 days, customer list, site visitors 30 days |
| Site visitors and cart | SV-01 to SV-04, plus the control | Site visitors 30 days plus add to cart 180 days | Purchasers 180 days |
| Past customers | PC-01 to PC-04 | Customer list plus purchasers 730 days | None |

Each new-buyer ad set launches its copy on 3 visuals the account already owns, so the read is about the concept rather than 1 file.

**Budget.** Current monthly spend is ₹11,12,000 (the mean of June, July and August 2026; the last 30 days were ₹11,52,747). The plan takes 25% for 4 weeks: ₹2,78,000, about ₹9,929 a day.

| Objective | Share of the ₹2,78,000 | Share of monthly spend | 4 weeks | Per ad set | Per ad set per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New buyers | 60% | 15.0% | ₹1,66,800 | ₹41,700 | ₹1,489 |
| Site visitors and cart | 25% | 6.25% | ₹69,500 | ₹17,375 | ₹621 |
| Past customers | 15% | 3.75% | ₹41,700 | ₹10,425 | ₹372 |

Past-customer spend is capped by frequency, not by share: customers × (CPM ÷ 1,000) × 6 impressions a month, at the account's warm CPM of ₹113.71. That is ₹6,823 a month at 10,000 customers, ₹13,645 at 20,000, ₹20,468 at 30,000. Whatever the customer file cannot absorb under 7 impressions per 30 days moves to the new-buyer campaign after the week-2 read.

## 10. Decisions for the owner

1. Customer file: can the customer list be synced to Meta, so past buyers can be excluded from cold spend and reached in their own campaign? Without it, the past-customer campaign runs on pixel purchasers only.

3. Attribution: move every campaign to 7-day click for the 4 weeks, or keep the mixed settings and accept that old and new cannot be compared.
4. GEHNA5: is the 5% code live? If not, the headline comes off the running Navratna and review ads.
