# Gehna Meta ad copy plan (Arm B)

Companion to `audit.md`. Numbers come from `raw/baselines.json`, `raw/budget_split.json` and `raw/account_totals.json`. Character and word checks on every ad are in `raw/copy_checks.json`, produced by `raw/check_copy.py`.

## 1. Brief

**Who the buyer is.** A woman in India, 25 to 54, buying fine gold for herself or as a gift. She wants real gold at a weight and price she can wear every day, colour from natural stones, and a reason to trust an online jeweller she has not touched. The account's own data says where the money is: women return 3.98 times spend, men 1.53; ages 35 to 54 return 4.0 to 4.2; ages 18 to 24 return 1.17.

**The buying question per objective.**

- New buyers: "Is this real gold, and what happens if I don't like it?" Belief now: buying gold online is risky. Belief to reach: it is BIS Hallmarked 18K gold with natural stones, and I can return it within 7 days for a full refund.
- Site visitors and cart abandoners: "Is it worth the price, and is it safe to pay?" Belief now: I like it but something is holding me back. Belief to reach: the delivery, payment and return terms remove the risk, and the price is today's gold price.
- Past customers: "Why buy the next one here?" Belief now: I was happy, but I have not thought about a second piece. Belief to reach: the next piece is a set-completer, a gift, or an upgrade on the same hallmark, with a policy that rewards coming back.

**The one idea per objective.**

- New buyers: real 18K gold you can wear every day, with 7 days to send it back.
- Site visitors: the piece you looked at is still there, and every reason not to buy has an answer on the site.
- Past customers: your next piece is easier than your first.

**Voice rules followed.** Warm, confident, short. One woman, not "customers". Name the material. Proof at the end. 1 emoji at the end of a line, at most 4 per text, at most 1 in a headline. Digits. No luxury jargon. No discounts, prices or dates that are not confirmed. First line under 125 characters. Headline under 40. Description under 30. Reading level below tenth grade, scored in code.

**Claims used, and where each is proven.**

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| BIS Hallmarked; 18K gold (14K option); natural gemstones; handcrafted; 4.8 on Google; 10,000+ customers; free shipping; 7-day returns; 20+ years | Approved list in the brand record; also on the home page |
| Free gift packing in India; insured in transit; tamper-proof box; full refund or exchange within 7 days of delivery, India only; customer pays return courier | Shipping and returns page; FAQ |
| Cash on delivery, cards, net banking | FAQ, Payments |
| EMI with monthly amounts shown on the product page | Product pages (EMI table) |
| In-stock pieces deliver in 2 to 3 days; made to order in 10 to 14 days | FAQ, Shipping |
| Free ring sizer; one free re-sizing within 15 days, customer pays shipping | Ring product FAQ; site FAQ |
| 1-year workmanship warranty; free repair of making faults with shipping paid | FAQ, After Sales |
| 1-inch adjustable loop on chains | Chain product FAQ |
| Limited lifetime exchange: gemstone pieces at 70% of prevailing market value, diamond at 80%, plain gold at the gold rate, silver excluded, invoice needed | Shipping and returns page, section 3 |
| Custom design and 1-on-1 styling consultation; trials at the Chennai store | Home page; FAQ |

Not used: buyback, IGI, "India's largest", "India's first", astrological claims, any customer count other than 10,000+, GEHNA5, "100% handmade", "video before shipment".

## 3. The 4-week test plan

### Structure

Three campaigns, sales objective, 7-day click attribution on all three, ad set budgets (not campaign budget), launched on a Monday so weekly reads line up.

| Campaign | Ad sets | Audience | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test, new buyers, Oct 2026 | NB-01 to NB-04, 1 ad set each, plus the control ad set | Broad, India | Purchasers 730 days, customer list, site visitors 30 days |
| Test, site visitors, Oct 2026 | SV-01 to SV-04, 1 ad set each, plus the control ad set | Site visitors 30 days plus add to cart 180 days | Purchasers 180 days |
| Past customers, Oct 2026 | PC-01 to PC-04, 1 ad set each | Customer list plus purchasers 730 days | None |

Each new-buyer ad set launches with the copy above on 3 visuals the account already owns (the earrings video family, product close-ups, and 1 static with the headline on the image) so the read is about the concept, not 1 file. The existing cold and warm campaigns keep running unchanged; the test does not replace them.

### Budget

Current monthly spend is ₹11,12,000 (mean of June, July and August 2026; the last 30 days were ₹11,52,747). The test takes 25% of that for 4 weeks: ₹2,78,000, about ₹9,929 a day.

| Objective | Share of test | Share of monthly spend | 4-week budget | Per ad set | Per ad set per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New buyers | 60% | 15.0% | ₹1,66,800 | ₹41,700 | ₹1,489 |
| Site visitors and cart | 25% | 6.25% | ₹69,500 | ₹17,375 | ₹621 |
| Past customers | 15% | 3.75% | ₹41,700 | ₹10,425 | ₹372 |

Past-customer spend is capped by frequency, not by the share. The rule is customers × (CPM ÷ 1,000) × 6 impressions a month, at the account's warm CPM of ₹113.71. At 10,000 customers that is ₹6,823 a month; at 20,000, ₹13,645; at 30,000, ₹20,468. Whatever the customer file cannot absorb under 7 impressions per 30 days moves to the new-buyer campaign after the week-2 read.

### Kill, keep and scale thresholds

Baselines, computed from the daily rows: cold evergreen prospecting over the last 90 days returned 3.55 at ₹10,974 per purchase (12 months: 2.76, ₹11,354). Cart and site retargeting over 90 days returned 4.56 at ₹6,814. The read spend per ad set is 3 times the cost per purchase, rounded to ₹1,000.

| Ad set type | Read at | Keep | Yellow | Kill | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New buyers | ₹33,000 spent, or day 28 | 3+ purchases and ROAS at or above 2.76 | 2 purchases: extend by ₹11,000 | 0 or 1 purchase | ROAS 3.55 or better with 3+ purchases: raise budget 20% a day, hold each step 7 days |
| Site visitors | ₹20,000 spent, or day 28 | 3+ purchases and ROAS at or above 4.56 | 2 purchases | 0 or 1 purchase | ROAS 4.56 or better: raise 20% a day while frequency stays under 7 per 30 days |
| Past customers | ₹10,000 spent, or day 28 | Any purchase with store data showing a repeat order | 0 purchases at ₹10,000 and frequency under 7: extend | Frequency over 7 per 30 days with 0 purchases | Not scaled by budget; scaled only if the customer file grows |

Rules that go with the table: judge at the ad set level, never on 1 ad's ROAS; never edit a winning ad set, fork new creative beside it; never read a window shorter than 7 days; a stock-out or tracking gap makes a test inconclusive, not lost.

### What to measure and when

Every Monday, for the previous full 7 days and for the test to date: spend, reported purchases, reported value, ROAS and cost per purchase per ad set on 7-day click; click-through rate as a selection check only; frequency by segment (new, engaged, existing) on a 30-day window; store orders split new versus returning from the sales dashboard; and the share of spend in each campaign against the plan.

- Week 1 read: delivery and cost per click only. No decisions.
- Week 2 read: first yellow or kill calls where an ad set has reached its read spend; move unspent past-customer budget.
- Week 3 read: keep, kill, scale list; brief 2 new visuals for any ad set at keep.
- Week 4 read: final list; which 3 concepts move into the standing campaigns; what the next 4 weeks test.

### What the owner gets each week

1. A 1-page table: each ad set, spend, purchases, value, ROAS, cost per purchase, frequency, and the call (keep, yellow, kill, scale) with the number that triggered it.
2. The 2 ads taking the most spend inside any ad set at keep, as the brief for the next visuals.
3. A claims log: anything marked `[confirm before launch]` and its status.
4. Next week's changes, written before they are made, so nothing is edited mid-read.

## 4. Decisions for the owner

1. Customer file: can the Meta customer list be synced so purchasers can be excluded from cold spend and targeted in the past-customer campaign? Without it, objective C runs on pixel purchasers only and the audience size is unknown.
2. Customer count: how many unique past customers are in the file? It sets the past-customer cap (₹6,823 a month at 10,000; ₹13,645 at 20,000).
3. Attribution: switch all campaigns to 7-day click for the test, or leave the mixed settings and accept that the test cannot be compared with the old campaigns.
4. GEHNA5: is the 5% code live? If not, the headline on the running Navratna and review ads should go.
5. Lifetime exchange: confirm the team can process an exchange as the shipping page states it before PC-03 runs. If the policy has changed, PC-03 is cut.
6. Buyback, IGI certification, "450+ verified reviews", "video of your piece on WhatsApp", "EMI above ₹10,000": each is in running copy and none is on the approved list or the pages read. Keep or remove.
7. Review permission: written consent from Jai Singh (SV-03) and from any reviewer already quoted in live ads.
8. Diwali order-by dates for in-stock and made-to-order pieces, for NB-03 and PC-02.
9. Gender and age: the data says men return 1.53 and ages 18 to 24 return 1.17. Run the new-buyer campaign as women 25+, or stay broad and let creative do the selection.
10. Rings: NB-04 is a small bet on a group that has 3 purchases in a year. Run it, or move its ₹41,700 to a second earrings concept.
11. Target margin: no gross margin or break-even ROAS is in the data. The thresholds use the account's own history. If the real break-even is above 2.76, the keep line moves up.
12. PC-04 objective: custom design leads may need a lead or WhatsApp objective rather than purchase. Choose which, and who answers the enquiries.
13. Test share: 25% of monthly spend is the proposal. A smaller share means fewer ad sets reach their read spend within 4 weeks.
14. Overlap in objective B: 4 ad sets on the same warm audience will compete. Accept the overlap for a per-objection read, or run 1 ad set with 4 ads and read spend share only.
15. Stock: confirm the hero products (Celestial Navaratna bracelet, Tantalizing Tourmaline ring) stay in stock or made-to-order for the 4 weeks; a stock-out ends that ad set's test.
