Gehna homepage speed lab: two agents, one mirror, fix by fix

The live homepage was mirrored offline with all 199 responses it loads, first and third party. Two agents audited the same mirror, ranked the top 10 limiters, then applied fixes one at a time to their own copy. Every step was measured 3 times on a throttled phone profile (slow 4G, 1.6 Mbps, 150 ms RTT, 4x CPU slowdown, cold cache) and checked to be the same page. Press Fix it to walk through each agent's sequence and watch the numbers move.

Verdict

Every measurement

Medians of 3 cold runs per variant on the replay server. Absolute numbers are kinder than the live site (no server time, no real CDN, HTTP/1.1 on localhost) but every variant was measured the same way, so the deltas are fair. Fidelity: same title, body text within 3 percent, same images in view, no new console errors, nothing first-party unserved, in at least 2 of 3 runs. Code and data: speed_lab/ in the ecomOS repo.
