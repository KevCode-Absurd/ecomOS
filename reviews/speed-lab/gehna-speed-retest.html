What a shopper feels now

The page paints as fast as before and the banner shows a third of a second sooner. Nothing is downloading in the background for 14 seconds any more, so the phone stays cool and the data bill is half. But for about 11 seconds the page looks ready and is not: taps on search, the cart, the menu and the product carousels do nothing. The code that makes the page work is held back by Rocket Loader, a Cloudflare add-on that runs your scripts one at a time after the load event.

Off, the page responds at 6.4 s instead of 10.5 s. But on its own it hurts: the banner slips to 2.4 s because 1.58 MB of script now competes with it on the slow link, and the load event moves out to 15.5 s because the trackers fire before it. The same pattern showed in September.

Ship as one release: Rocket Loader off, the trackers moved to load after the page is ready (fix 3), and the banner preloaded (fix 6). Together they keep the fast banner and bring the page to life at about 5 to 6 seconds on this profile. Then fix 8, then fix 9. The developer briefs for each are on the speed plan page.
