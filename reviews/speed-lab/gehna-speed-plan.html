What we cannot fix from outside

After the page arrives, its code keeps the phone busy for about 2.4 seconds building the page. None of the 8 fixes change that. It needs your developers to rework the app itself, which is a bigger project. Worth doing after the 8 fixes above are live.

What happens next

"Let the agent do it" asks ecomOS to prepare the change on a copy of the site for you to review; nothing changes on the live site until you say so. "Send to developer" opens the exact instructions to copy and send. Fixes 1 and 2 go live together. Fix 3 waits for your marketing team's yes. The rest can ship any time.

How we measured: a Pixel 5 phone profile on a slow 4G connection, 3 loads per version, the middle result. "Fully loaded" is when everything on the page has arrived.
