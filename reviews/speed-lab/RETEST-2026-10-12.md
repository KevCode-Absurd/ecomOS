# Gehna homepage retest, 12 Oct 2026

Same lab as 25 Sep: an offline mirror of www.gehnaindia.com replayed on a Pixel 5 profile, slow 4G (1.6 Mbps, 150 ms), 4x CPU slowdown, cold cache, 3 runs, median. New this time: the browser keeps watching for 25 s after the load event, because the marketing tags now start on a timer after the page is interactive. The 25 Sep mirror was re-measured the same way so the columns compare. Results in `speed_lab/results/baseline-settle25.json`, `live-1012-settle25.json`, `live-1012-no-rocket.json`. ## What the shopper feels now

The page paints as fast as before and the banner shows a third of a second sooner. The big change is that nothing is downloading in the background for 14 s any more. But the page does not respond to taps for about 10 s on this profile: the search box, cart, menus and carousels wait for hydration, and hydration waits for Rocket Loader to fetch and run the scripts one at a time after the load event.

## The next limiter, measured

Rocket Loader off, nothing else changed (`live-1012-no-rocket`): interactive at 6.4 s (from 10.5 s), tags at 6.1 s. But on its own it hurts: the banner slips to 2.4 s because 1.6 MB of script now competes with it on the 1.6 Mbps link, load moves out to 15.5 s because the tags now fire before the load event, and layout shift rises to 0.018. This is the same pattern the 25 Sep lab saw. The fix is still a pair: Rocket Loader off together with fix 3 (tags on `lazyOnload`) and fix 6 (banner preload). Shipping order unchanged: 1 with 3 and 6 in one release, then 8, then 9.
