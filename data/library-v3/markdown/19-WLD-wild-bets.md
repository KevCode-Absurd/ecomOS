## Run low-cost wild bets first: handwritten notes and keep-it refunds
Use when: You want to test unscalable retention tactics on your store but need a low-cost, low-risk starting point before committing budget.
- Stage 1, run this month: rank customers by lifetime spend, take the top 50, and send each a handwritten thank-you note or a 60-second personal founder video. Cost is roughly $1-$3 per note plus time.
- In the same month, turn on keep-it refunds (refund without requiring a return) for items under $20. This removes customer hassle and avoids reverse-logistics costs that can run 20%-65% of COGS.
- Benchmark for expansion: compare repeat-purchase rate or 90-day reorder rate of that top-50 group against a control group. If it rises, expand the handwritten/founder touch to the top 200.
- Stage 2, next 90 days: launch a named invite-only VIP tier for the top 1% of customers with early drop access and a direct founder channel. Add a surprise insert (sticker plus note) to every VIP order. If VIP retention and AOV beat non-VIP, formalize the tier and add a second tier below it.
- Stage 3, next 6 months: run a tiered referral waitlist for your next launch using product rewards (not cash), and test a limited drop on an SMS list. If referral signups exceed 30% of total signups and drops sell through at full price, make drops a regular cadence.
