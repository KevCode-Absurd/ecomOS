## Setup support

### 1. Take a room-by-room look

Identify reachable edges, corners, sockets, cupboards, fridge access and doors. Also check for hazards beyond this kit's scope, including unsecured furniture, stairs, cords and accessible medicines or cleaning products.

### 2. Check suitability before you buy

Review the component fit and installation requirements for your furniture, surfaces and fittings. Don't assume that one socket guard or lock will suit every location.

### 3. Install with guidance

Follow the relevant installation instructions for each component. Use the included 1-to-1 installation video support for setup guidance; no installation time is promised.

### 4. Keep checking as exploration changes

Continue adult supervision. Check installed components regularly for loosening, wear or damage, and reassess your rooms as your child becomes more mobile.
