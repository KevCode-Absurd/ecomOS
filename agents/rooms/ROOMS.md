## Today (LOOP)

**Jobs**

- see which pipeline stage is losing the most money today. Founder asks: "where is the business actually broken right now". Capabilities: DIAG-C01, ORCH-C01
- know how much the break is costing before deciding to act. Founder asks: "what is this worth in dollars this week". Capabilities: DIAG-C01
- act on the top 10 ideas for the break in under a minute each. Founder asks: "what should I approve, send out, or park". Capabilities: PROP-C06, ORCH-C03
- see gross orders next to what they are worth after returns and chargebacks. Founder asks: "are we actually growing or just booking orders that will be returned or disputed". Capabilities: LOOP-C23
- catch a mid-day number that has already crossed a line, before end of day. Founder asks: "did anything blow past a limit today". Capabilities: LOOP-C40
- see what is waiting on me across every queue, not per person. Founder asks: "what needs my decision right now". Capabilities: ORCH-C02, REG-C08
- catch a test that would contaminate one already running. Founder asks: "will this new test mess up a read I am already waiting on". Capabilities: LOOP-C35
- for an agency, see which brand needs attention first across the whole book. Founder asks: "which client fire do I put out first today". Capabilities: LOOP-C21
- get a short brief before a named meeting with a vendor, investor or platform rep. Founder asks: "what do I need to know walking into this call today". Capabilities: LOOP-C17
- approve or park a card from my phone without opening the page. Founder asks: "can I just reply to decide". Capabilities: LOOP-C42, LOOP-C42
- know the seasonal deadline that is closest and whether prep is on track. Founder asks: "what is due before the next festival or sale window". Capabilities: LOOP-C05
- see a single-item escalation that is too small for crisis mode but too risky to sit in the queue. Founder asks: "does this one review or one stock issue need my eyes now". Capabilities: LOOP-C06
- trust that a number moved because of something we did, not something external. Founder asks: "did we actually cause this, or did something else". Capabilities: LOOP-C02
- see what is unreadable today and why, instead of assuming it is fine. Founder asks: "what can't the system see right now". Capabilities: DIAG-C02

**Insights, as rules**

- top break named: highest-stake stage from Diagnose, ranked by stake then confidence then upstream position → "{stage} is costing about {amount} this {window}, since {date}" (action PROP-C06)
- trend alert: a stage's delta sign matches for 2 consecutive trailing windows → "{stage} has moved {direction} for 2 windows running" (action DIAG-C03)
- Reversals widening: the gap between gross and net orders widens for 2 or more windows running → "Gross orders look flat but net orders after returns and chargebacks have fallen {pct} over 2 windows" (action LOOP-C23)
- same-day trigger crossed: a live metric value crosses its stored hard threshold mid-day → "{metric} crossed its {threshold} trigger at {time} today" (action LOOP-C40)
- experiment overlap: two running registry entries share an audience or metric window → "{idea_a} and {idea_b} share the same audience; the newer one is staggered" (action LOOP-C35)
- brand gone quiet: brand_quiet_days exceeds 7 for a brand with an active queue → "{brand} has had no founder decision in {n} days while {m} cards wait" (action LOOP-C21)
- unreadable stage: a stage has no full day of data or a failed connector → "{stage} cannot be called healthy or broken: {source} has not reported since {date}" (action DIAG-C02)
- external cause check: a metric move coincides with a dated external event (platform change, gateway outage, courier strike, festival date shift) → "{stage} moved {pct}, but {event} on {date} may explain part of it" (action LOOP-C02)
- reads due this week: registry entries whose read_point falls in the current week → "{n} experiments hit their read point this week" (action REG-C02)
- seasonal prep at risk: a specialist's pre-window prep item has fewer days left than its own stated lead time → "{item} is due in {n} days for {window} and is not yet marked ready" (action LOOP-C05)
- single-item escalation: an incoming escalation is below the crisis threshold but above the normal queue bar → "{item} needs a look today; it is not a crisis but it is not routine either" (action LOOP-C06)
- chargebacks rising: chargeback_rate rises 2+ windows running or crosses 0.9 percent → "Chargebacks reached {rate} percent of paid orders, up {delta} in {n} windows; representment and the reason codes are in the Money room" (action LOOP-C31)

**Not elsewhere**

- money-at-stake per stage, computed in code from your own trailing history and a peer band, before any fix is proposed (vs Shopify Analytics and Klaviyo dashboards show revenue, sessions and conversion rate, not a stage-level stake number with a peer band)
- gross orders shown net of expected returns and chargebacks and split by payment method before naming a bottleneck (vs generic ecommerce dashboards report gross orders only; no packaged Shopify or Triple Whale view nets out India RTO by default)
- one ranked list across every brand an agency runs, by stake and urgency on the same scale (vs AgencyAnalytics and Whatagraph give per-client reports and roll-ups, not a single cross-client priority queue)
- same-day alert on a pre-agreed hard trigger, separate from full crisis mode and from tomorrow's batch ranking (vs most ecommerce dashboards check once a day; ads and analytics platforms alert on their own metrics only, not a founder-defined cross-channel trigger)
- approve, reject or park a specific card by a single SMS or Slack reply, matched back to its card id (vs SMS tools like Attentive and Postscript handle customer-facing templates and support replies, not an internal founder decision channel tied to a proposal card)
- a test flagged before it starts if it shares an audience or metric window with one already running (vs enterprise experimentation platforms like GrowthBook check this inside one engineering team's own test list, not across marketing ideas drawn from a bank, an audit and the web)

## Brand brain (LOOP)

**Jobs**

- see how fresh and how trustworthy each part of what the system knows about the brand is. Founder asks: "can I trust what this page is telling me right now". Capabilities: BRN-C12
- see which claims are approved, which are only site-backed, and which are forbidden. Founder asks: "what can and can't we say about this product". Capabilities: BRN-C03
- see every founder decision with its reason, dated, in one log. Founder asks: "why did we decide that, and when". Capabilities: BRN-C08
- see open questions the system cannot answer on its own. Founder asks: "what does the system need from me to get unstuck". Capabilities: BRN-C07
- see where two sources disagree before trusting either. Founder asks: "the document says one thing and the site says another, which wins". Capabilities: BRN-C05
- see what changed on the site since the last check. Founder asks: "did a price, a policy or a claim change without me knowing". Capabilities: BRN-C01
- see which connectors are working and which failed. Founder asks: "is any of our data actually stale right now". Capabilities: BRN-C02, BRN-C14
- see vendor and platform contracts with renewal dates before they lapse. Founder asks: "what contract needs my attention before it auto-renews". Capabilities: LOOP-C28
- check a page or invoice carries the disclosures US rules require before it ships. Founder asks: "is this page legally compliant before it goes live". Capabilities: LOOP-C27
- see who can see which part of the brand's data. Founder asks: "does the agency staff see more of our brain than they should". Capabilities: LOOP-C29
- see the brand's own marketplace listings against the D2C site. Founder asks: "is someone undercutting our price on Amazon or Walmart". Capabilities: PROD-C46
- export the whole brain cleanly when a relationship ends. Founder asks: "if we part ways with this agency, can we take everything with us". Capabilities: LOOP-C22

**Insights, as rules**

- claim untiered too long: a claim has sat untiered for more than 7 days → "{claim} has been untiered for {n} days and needs a founder call" (action BRN-C07)
- source conflict: a document fact and the live site disagree on the same field → "{document} says {value_a}, the site says {value_b}; the site wins by default until you say otherwise" (action BRN-C05)
- site changed unnoticed: a price, stock, policy or claim diff appears between two crawls → "{field} changed from {old} to {new} on {date}" (action BRN-C01)
- connector failing: connector_health_pct falls under 90% for a source over its own cadence → "{source} has missed {n} of its last {m} expected pulls; its fields show unavailable, not zero" (action BRN-C02)
- contract renewal approaching: contract_renewal_countdown falls inside the alert window sized for that vendor type → "{vendor} renews in {n} days; last terms were {rate} agreed on {date}" (action LOOP-C28)
- contract missing renewal date: a stored contract has no renewal date field → "{vendor}'s contract has no renewal date on file and is marked incomplete" (action LOOP-C28)
- disclosure missing: a live or about-to-ship page fails a required US disclosure check → "{page} is missing {disclosure}; this blocks going live" (action LOOP-C27)
- MAP violation: a marketplace listing price sits below the brand's stated minimum advertised price → "{marketplace} lists {product} at {price}, below your {map_price} minimum" (action PROD-C46)
- unauthorized reseller: a marketplace listing's seller name does not match the brand's own account → "An unrecognised seller is listing {product} on {marketplace}" (action PROD-C46)
- permission gap: a brain section has no assigned permission entry → "{section} has no permission set and defaults to restricted" (action LOOP-C29)
- competitor snapshot stale: competitor_snapshot_age exceeds 7 days → "{competitor} was last captured {n} days ago" (action BRN-C11)
- founder question round due: open_questions_count reaches 4 or more, or one open question blocks a running item → "{n} open questions are waiting; it is time for a founder round" (action BRN-C07)

**Not elsewhere**

- claims tiered approved, site-backed or forbidden, tied to red lines and forbidden-claim detection, not generic content staleness (vs Guru, Slite and MangoApps flag stale or unowned pages, but they tier general company docs, not ecommerce product and marketing claims against founder-set red lines)
- a conflict between a document and the live site resolved with a stated default (the site wins) rather than left for a person to notice (vs enterprise wikis surface drift for a human to review; none apply a default-winner rule between two specific source types automatically)
- marketplace listings diffed against the brand's own price, stock and claims the same way the D2C site is crawled (vs MAP-monitoring tools exist for large brands on Amazon, but nothing in this stack ties marketplace diffs to the same claims-tier and site-diff pipeline as the brand's own site)
- vendor and platform contracts extracted into renewal alerts sized to how long a renegotiation takes, inside the same brain as everything else about the brand (vs generic contract-management SaaS tracks renewal dates but is not fed from or feeding the same brand brain a founder already uses for decisions)
- US legal disclosure gate (FTC, CAN-SPAM, CCPA privacy policy, sales tax on invoices, return policy, auto-renewal) checked before a page ships, as a blocking check, not a general CMS compliance plugin (vs generic CMS and Shopify legal-page apps exist, but they are not tied into this brand's own claims tiers and decisions log)
- a permission map enforced the same way whether a person opens the brain directly or asks a question in chat (vs role-based access in most knowledge tools governs direct reads only; it is not applied to an AI chat answer drawing from the same sections)

- see which reads are due and which are overdue. Founder asks: "what result am I waiting on, and is anything late". Capabilities: REG-C08, REG-C02
- see the plain-word result the moment a read point lands. Founder asks: "did it work, in numbers I can read in one line". Capabilities: REG-C03

- see what was parked and why, and when that reason expires. Founder asks: "what did we put off, and is it time to look again". Capabilities: REG-C05
- catch two running tests that would contaminate each other before either's read. Founder asks: "are two experiments about to step on each other". Capabilities: LOOP-C35
- know every idea has an agreed metric, read point, and kill or scale rule before it starts. Founder asks: "did we actually agree what winning and losing look like before we started". Capabilities: LOOP-C08
- see why the ranked list put one candidate above another, regardless of where it came from. Founder asks: "why is a web idea beating a bank idea, or vice versa". Capabilities: LOOP-C09
- see every founder reason turned into a standing weight, without repeating myself. Founder asks: "why does the system already know I don't like ideas like this". Capabilities: LOOP-C10
- see an internal check on whether the ranked pick matches what I'd actually approve. Founder asks: "would the top pick actually be my pick". Capabilities: LOOP-C36
- run the same retro shape every time an initiative closes. Founder asks: "what did we learn, and would we do it again". Capabilities: LOOP-C25
- hand a new person the reasoning behind past decisions, not just the outcomes. Founder asks: "why did we do it this way, for someone new joining". Capabilities: LOOP-C10

**Insights, as rules**

- read point reached: today equals or passes an entry's read_point → "{idea} on {brand} hit its read point; result is {up_down_flat_unreadable}: {number}" (action REG-C03)
- stale unread entry: today exceeds read_point by more than a few days with no result recorded → "{idea} passed its read point {n} days ago with no result recorded yet" (action REG-C02)
- parked reason expired: a parked entry's stored expiry date has passed → "{idea} was parked because '{reason}'; that reason has now expired" (action REG-C05)
- unreadable result: a read point landed but required data is missing → "{idea}'s read is unreadable: {field} is missing, not zero" (action REG-C03)
- overlap collision: two running entries share an audience or metric window → "{idea_a} and {idea_b} collide on {dimension}; stagger one" (action LOOP-C35)
- repeated reject reason becomes weight: the same founder reason appears 3 or more times against similar idea attributes → "'{reason}' has come up {n} times; ideas like this now rank lower automatically" (action LOOP-C10)
- ranked pick versus prediction mismatch: the code ranker's top card and the predicted founder decision disagree → "The top-ranked card is a likely {predicted_decision} based on your past reasons; it is shown anyway" (action LOOP-C36)
- read point too small: an entry's read point duration is below the minimum for its metric type → "{idea}'s read point is sized too short to reach a real signal for {metric}" (action LOOP-C08)
- retro missing: an initiative closed with no retro note filed → "{initiative} closed {n} days ago with no retro filed yet" (action LOOP-C25)
- brand prior updated: a new result changes the brand's own weighted effect estimate for an idea → "{idea} now shows a {new_estimate} effect on this brand, based on {n} attempts" (action REG-C04)
- would-we-again check: a retro's stored answer to 'would we do this again' is no → "The retro for {initiative} says we would not do it the same way again: {reason}" (action LOOP-C25)

**Not elsewhere**

- a per-brand prior effect size for a marketing idea, built only from what happened when this brand tried it, separate from the bank's brand-agnostic basis (vs GrowthBook, Eppo and VWO run and score individual A/B tests but do not carry a cross-idea, cross-attempt brand memory tied to a founder-facing idea bank)
- a founder's repeated approve, reject or park reason turned automatically into a ranking weight, without being asked to restate it (vs decision logs in Notion or similar tools store the reason as text; nothing turns a repeated reason into a scoring input for the next similar idea)
- an overlap check across marketing ideas from a bank, an audit and the web, not just inside one engineering team's own test list (vs GrowthBook's namespace collision checks cover its own hosted experiments, not ideas gathered from three different sources with different owners)
- the same retro shape (what worked, what did not, would we do it again) applied automatically to any closed initiative type: launch, crisis, onboarding, replatform (vs most retro tools are generic templates a team fills in by hand per project; nothing here triggers automatically from a registry close event and files the answer as a reusable checklist)

## Site and speed (SITE)

**Jobs**

- find why a page is slow and what it is costing in dollars. Founder asks: "why is my page slow and what is it costing me". Capabilities: SITE-C21
- cut image weight without hurting quality. Founder asks: "which images are too big and how do I fix them". Capabilities: SITE-C22
- defer or remove scripts that block the page. Founder asks: "which scripts are slowing this page down and can they wait". Capabilities: SITE-C23
- fix font loading. Founder asks: "why do fonts delay the page and how much do they weigh". Capabilities: SITE-C24
- stop the page jumping while it loads. Founder asks: "what is causing layout shift and on which template". Capabilities: SITE-C25
- fix the largest paint element. Founder asks: "what is the LCP element on each template and what fixes it". Capabilities: SITE-C26
- make add-to-cart, filters and search feel instant. Founder asks: "which tap or click feels slow and why". Capabilities: SITE-C27
- confirm caching and CDN are actually working. Founder asks: "are my pages cached and served from the CDN or not". Capabilities: SITE-C28
- get ready for a sale-day traffic spike. Founder asks: "will the site hold up on Black Friday, Cyber Monday or another big sale day (or a festive sale day in India)". Capabilities: SITE-C29
- cut and reorganize code weight. Founder asks: "how much CSS and JS can be removed, split or inlined". Capabilities: SITE-C30
- speed-tune a headless or custom storefront. Founder asks: "what is slow in our own framework code, not the platform's". Capabilities: SITE-C31
- hand a developer a brief they can execute without guessing. Founder asks: "what exactly should the developer change, and how do we know it worked". Capabilities: SITE-C32
- catch a speed regression before it reaches customers. Founder asks: "did the last deploy or app install make the site slower". Capabilities: SITE-C33
- pick a landing-page build method that will not wreck speed. Founder asks: "will this new page-builder page load fast enough to run ads to". Capabilities: SITE-C61

**Insights, as rules**

- unused fonts: a font file is requested but 0 glyphs from it render on the page → "{n} font files nobody sees cost {kb} KB on every visit" (action SITE-C24)
- third-party scripts over budget: script_count_by_route for third-party routes exceeds the page's stored script budget → "{n} outside scripts add {ms} ms and {kb} KB, over your budget of {budget}" (action SITE-C23)
- speed regression since last ship: regression_delta_pct worse than 10 percent since the last recorded baseline → "the page got {pct}% slower since the last check on {date}, likely from {suspect_change}" (action SITE-C33)
- LCP element still un-lazied or animated: the element flagged as the LCP source has loading=lazy, an active CSS animation, or is not preloaded → "the {element} that decides how fast the page feels is still {problem}" (action SITE-C26)
- layout shift culprit: a single element accounts for over half of total CLS on a template → "{element} on the {template} page causes most of the jumping; it has no reserved space" (action SITE-C25)
- render-blocking code still present: a CSS or JS resource has renderBlockingStatus blocking and is not marked async or deferred → "{n} files still block the page from showing anything until they arrive" (action SITE-C30)
- dead analytics tag still firing: a request matches a known sunset tracking endpoint (for example old Universal Analytics) → "a tag that stopped working on {sunset_date} still loads and costs {kb} KB" (action SITE-C23)
- app adds weight with no clear revenue link: an app's script bytes rank in the top 5 by weight and the app inventory (SITE-C13) shows no attributed revenue → "{app} adds {kb} KB on every page and nothing in your sales data ties back to it" (action SITE-C13)
- font variation count too high: more than 2 weights or styles of the same font family are loaded → "{n} versions of {family} load when the page only uses {used}" (action SITE-C24)
- cache headers missing on static assets: a static asset (image, font, script, css) returns a cache-control max-age under 1 week or no cache-control header → "{n} static files are re-downloaded on every visit because caching is not set" (action SITE-C28)
- no CDN detected on a key page: response headers show no CDN-identifying header (for example cf-ray, x-served-by) on a page expected to run through one → "{page} is not being served through the CDN; every visit hits the origin" (action SITE-C28)
