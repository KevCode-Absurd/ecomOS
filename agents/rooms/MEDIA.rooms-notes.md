# MEDIA rooms: what they show that common tools do not

Three rooms, built like the Meta gallery and speed lab: metrics with a formula and freshness rule, insights as code-checkable rules with a plain sentence, and a founder-gate queue for calls the agent should not make alone.

## Paid social (Meta and TikTok)

Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads Manager, Revealbot and Optmyzr already do rule-based kill/scale and suggestion feeds well. This room's edge is combining that with data those tools cannot see: same-day stock level, competitor discounts and site speed, fanned out automatically the moment a spend or ROAS anomaly fires. It reconciles blended MER (from the brand's own margin ledger) against platform ROAS before any scale call, which Triple Whale and Northbeam approximate but do not tie to the brand's own numbers. TikTok's organic-to-Spark loop gets a real timeline with the 365-day authorization clock, which TikTok Ads Manager does not track. Hardest data: the brand revenue ledger (does not exist yet), a live stock feed, and a landing-page snapshot for message-match checks.

## Google Ads

The job here is control, not reporting Google already gives free. It flags Performance Max defaults that hide control (URL expansion, auto assets) in one view, checks feed titles against the brand's real converting search terms, and gates any scale call behind a fixed 5-gate checklist plus a blended-MER reconciliation, none of which Google's recommendations page does. Hardest data: everything, since the Google Ads API is not connected yet (only Meta is read-only today) — this room is unavailable until that connector lands.

## Affiliate, marketplace and retail media

Covers three things no single tool spans: affiliate fraud and payout reconciliation checked against the brand's own order data, not just the network's tracker; handoff briefs for Amazon, Flipkart and quick commerce since no connector reads those accounts; and one invoice-to-ledger reconciliation across Meta, Google and TikTok billing, including GST/RCM credits. It stays honest that Amazon and Flipkart performance is unavailable, showing brief status only, never an invented ROAS. Hardest data: affiliate click-level data for fraud rules, marketplace ad performance (no connector by design), and a connected accounting ledger for GST reconciliation.

## Common gap

All three rooms need a brand revenue ledger and margin data (blended MER, CAC, break-even CPA) that does not exist yet. Until the brand brain is built, they show Meta-only numbers plus honest "unavailable" states elsewhere.
