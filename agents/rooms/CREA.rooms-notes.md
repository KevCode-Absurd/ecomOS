# CREA rooms: Creative and Copy

Two rooms, built on the Meta gallery's pattern (ad-level data, verdicts, fatigue flags, studio, competitors) plus the speed lab's measured-chain-and-decision pattern.

## Creative

Shows winners and losers broken into hook, structure, proof, offer and CTA with a written hypothesis on file, a fatigue diagnosis that tells a worn-out ad apart from one with a shrinking pool, the brief-to-launch-to-hit pipeline as a funnel, real cost per finished asset against budget, and rights or authorization expiry on live ads.

Not shown elsewhere:
- Checks whether an ad set's own reach has stopped growing before calling it fatigued. Meta's native flag only reads frequency and CTR.
- Ties a why-it-worked write-up to the account's own numbers and its brief. Foreplay and Motion tag hooks and formats but store no account-specific hypothesis.
- Tracks real cost per finished asset against budget, which Triple Whale's Creative Cockpit does not do.
- Flags stock, music, font and creator-whitelisting rights expiring on ads that are live right now.
- Checks a pre-spend predictive score against the account's own later verdict instead of leaving it unchecked.

Hardest data to get: a brief log matched to launched ads, a real production cost log, and a rights and authorization log with expiry dates. None exist today; each needs a new logging habit before the room can go beyond what the gallery already has.

## Copy

Shows claim checks against the brand's own claim tiers, message match between a live ad and its page (specific terms, not a vague score), objection coverage ranked by real customer frequency, a reading-level and voice-rule trend, and payment and delivery reassurance line coverage (BNPL, free returns, delivery date; COD-to-prepaid and RTO-trust line coverage for an India-market brand, markets IN, null for a US brand).

Not shown elsewhere:
- Checks claims against this brand's own approved claim tiers and current platform policy, not generic grammar or readability, which is all Grammarly and Hemingway do.
- Scores message match on the ad's actual offer, price and headline terms against the brand's own page copy blocks, unlike Instapage's AdMap, which needs the page built inside that tool.
- Ranks real objections from the account's own support tickets and reviews and checks copy coverage against that ranking.

Hardest data to get: the brand's claim tiers (need the brand brain, not built yet), live page copy blocks kept in sync with edits, and an objection log built from support tickets and reviews.
