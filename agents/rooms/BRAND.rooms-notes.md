# BRAND rooms: what they show that common tools do not

Three rooms, all under the founder-approval pattern from the Meta gallery and the speed lab.

**Brand and customers.** One gated avatar record per segment, with every field traced to a real quote, plus a language bank that tracks whether customers' own words actually make it into live copy. Voice-of-customer tools like Mopinion or GummySearch summarize themes from reviews and Reddit; none of them gate a single avatar record against a completeness checklist or measure phrase reuse in live assets. The voice compliance audit walks the live site, ads and email together on one cadence, something brand-voice checkers (ClickUp, Averi, Sprites) cannot do since they score one pasted document at a time. Brand health joins branded search, mentions, sentiment and repeat purchase into one line tied to the brand's own order data, which paid trackers like Brand24 or Brandwatch ($200 to $800 a month) do not do. The mystery-shopper audit and AI persona pretest are automated and repeatable, unlike a one-off paid audit.

**Competitors and market.** The positioning map plots the brand against every real alternative, including doing nothing, not just direct rivals the way price trackers (Prisync, Priceva) or ad-spy tools (Foreplay, PowerAdSpy) do. Price position is judged against the category's learned range and the brand's own premium signals, not a raw price diff. Win-loss interviews and battlecards use language from real almost-buyers, something no pricing or ad tool collects.

**Wild bets and PR.** The bet pipeline enforces an 8-field bound (budget cap, downside limit, control, cohort, stop rule, read point, legal review) before launch and reads results against a real control in code, closer to the speed lab's measured fix chain than any PR or project tool. The reputation monitor ties a mention spike straight to a drafted holding statement, and partnership sourcing runs through one screen for category overlap and reputation risk together, which Muck Rack, Prowly and generic CRM tools do not combine.

**Hardest data to get:** branded search volume and mention/sentiment feeds need a search console or listening connector not yet attached; competitor price and ad capture (weekly) has no script yet, only the existing meta_gallery competitor files as a starting point; win-loss interviews, reputation checks on partners, and the wild bet 8-field bound all depend on someone actually running the interview or filling the bound, since no tool observes these passively.
