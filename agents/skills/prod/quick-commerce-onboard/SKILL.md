# Onboard a brand on rapid-delivery and quick-commerce platforms

## Purpose
Rapid-delivery/quick-commerce onboarding requires a specific document set and each platform runs its own review
process and its own unit-economics validation window (commonly around 90 days) before the brand is judged to be
worth investing in further. Missing a required document is the most common cause of onboarding delay. India: the
same job onboards to Blinkit, Zepto or Instamart with GST and FSSAI documents instead (markets: IN).

## Steps
1. Assemble the business licence, resale certificate, UPC/GS1 barcode and packaging documents the platform's own
onboarding checklist requires, confirming every item is present before submission (India: GST registration, FSSAI
licence, PAN, bank and GS1 barcode documents instead; markets: IN).
2. Submit through the platform's own onboarding channel (Instacart Retailer/CPG portal, DoorDash Marketplace,
Gopuff vendor channel; india: Blinkit Seller App, Zepto category-manager channel, Instamart seller portal), never
a workaround or a third-party shortcut.
3. Track the document review status and respond to any query the platform raises within its stated window.
4. Prepare the initial catalogue listing using the matching platform listing skill.
5. Confirm the platform's own unit-economics validation window and what it requires the brand to demonstrate
during it.
6. Get the brand live and confirm the first order flows correctly end to end before calling onboarding complete.

## Checks
- Every document the platform's checklist requires is present before submission.
- The validation window and its specific requirement are both logged, not assumed.

## Output
{document_packet, review_status, initial_listing, validation_window, go_live_confirmation}

## Example
Documents assembled: business licence, resale certificate, UPC registration, packaging compliance declaration.
Submitted through Instacart's Retailer portal. First order confirmed to flow correctly on day 12 of a stated
90-day validation window. India: the same brand's Blinkit onboarding instead used GST, FSSAI licence and GS1
barcode documents (markets: IN).
