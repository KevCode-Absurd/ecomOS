name: pricing-and-naming/product-naming
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: a new product, method or proprietary system needs a name that
  does real selling work
inputs: product/system facts, the benefit or story it should carry, any real
  named person attached
outputs: name candidate list, ranked shortlist
---
Purpose: draft names that carry a real benefit or a real, permissioned
person's story, never an invented credential, and always as a founder
decision, never a final call CREA makes itself.

Steps
1. Pull the single strongest benefit or mechanism the name needs to carry,
   stated as one sentence, one idea, since a name trying to say two things
   usually says neither clearly.
2. Draft 8 to 15 candidates that tie directly to that benefit, or that
   attach to a real named person's real story, rather than a generic
   invented word chosen for sound alone.
3. Where a real person is attached, a founder, a formulator, confirm the
   story and any implied credential is real and on file; an implied
   credential with nothing behind it is cut, not softened.
4. Screen the shortlist with a quick public search for an obvious trademark
   or existing marketplace-listing conflict.
5. Rank the conflict-screened shortlist with a stated reason for the order
   and present it as a founder_gate decision; naming never resolves inside
   this skill alone.

Checks: every candidate ties to the benefit; no invented credential.

Output: the full candidate list and the ranked, conflict-screened
shortlist.

Example: a joint-support supplement formulated by a named physiotherapist
gets candidates built around mobility and that real person's actual title,
not an invented "Dr." credential.
