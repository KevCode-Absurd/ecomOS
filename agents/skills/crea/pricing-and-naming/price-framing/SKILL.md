invoke_when: a price or offer needs framing in copy, honestly and matching
  what operations can actually honor
inputs: brand pricing and policies, positioning
outputs: price copy block, price-framing line
---
Purpose: word a price so it is clear and honest, matched to the brand's real
positioning, never rounding a guarantee or a deadline up past what
operations actually offers.

Steps
1. Pull the exact live price and the real policy limits (return window,
   guarantee terms, EMI eligibility) from the brand brain, never a cached or
   remembered figure.
2. Choose the ending style, charm pricing for a value-positioned brand,
   round numbers for a premium one, matching the stated positioning rather
   than defaulting to habit.
3. Build a cost-per-day or cost-per-use reframe only when it makes the price
   feel honestly smaller, showing the underlying math so it can be checked.
4. Whenever a price looks unusually low next to the category, state the real
   reason, a clearance, a factory-direct model, a holiday sale, rather than
   leaving it to look suspicious or inventing urgency.
5. Match any guarantee or urgency line exactly to what operations has
   confirmed it can honor; a wording that rounds up gets stopped for a
   founder decision.

Checks: numbers match the current live price; guarantee wording matches ops
exactly.

Output: the price copy block and the specific price-framing line used.

Example: "just 33 cents a day" is shown with the math ($120 a year divided
by 365) rather than stated as a bare claim.
