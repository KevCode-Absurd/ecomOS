owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: a new creative test needs its research refreshed before angles get
  written
inputs: brand brain voice-of-customer packet, own account's last 90 days of
  ad-level results
outputs: sourced objection map, metric-backed format scan
---
Purpose: assemble the research a new angle needs, using the brand's own
already-mined customer language and this account's own real recent results,
rather than a generic best-practice list.

Steps
1. Pull the current voice-of-customer packet from the brand brain, which the
   Brand Brain Keeper owns and refreshes; this skill does not re-mine raw
   customer data itself, it assembles what already exists for this decision.
2. Pull this account's own last 90 days of ad-level results and rank which
   formats currently deliver the best hook rate, hold rate and CPA, since
   "what's working now" should mean this account, not the category in
   general.
3. Tag every objection carried into the map with its real quoted source and a
   date, never a paraphrase presented as a quote.
4. Tag every format claim to the specific metric and window that backs it.
5. Flag anything in the pull that reads as a genuine surprise rather than a
   confirmation of what the team already believed, since surprises are where
   a new angle usually hides.

Checks: source and date on every objection; metric and window on every format
claim.

Output: the objection map and the format scan, both ready to feed the
angle-builder skill.

Example: the scan shows static ads with a price-anchor angle now outperform
video 2-to-1 on CPA over the last 90 days, a fact the format-economics step
later uses directly.
