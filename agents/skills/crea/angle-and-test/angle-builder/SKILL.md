---
name: angle-and-test/angle-builder
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: research exists and a small set of testable angles must be
  defined before any copy gets written
inputs: objection map, persona/avatar record, product facts, claim tiers
outputs: 2 to 4 angle candidates, each fully specified
---
Purpose: turn raw research into a small, genuinely distinct set of testable
angles, each disciplined to one persona and one dominant appeal, so a test
batch actually isolates what it claims to test.

Steps
1. Write the angle as one sentence for one named persona; an angle that tries
   to speak to two personas at once gets split into two angles instead.
2. State the exact buying question the angle answers, in the persona's own
   words where the research supports it.
3. Pick a single dominant appeal, price, status, ease, fear-avoidance or
   aspiration, and hold to it; an angle mixing two appeals is not one testable
   thing.
4. Set the awareness stage (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware,
   product-aware, most-aware) the angle is built for, since the hook, proof
   and CTA all depend on it.
5. Check the angle against the brand's claim tiers immediately, before any
   copy is written on top of it, so a bad angle is caught at its cheapest
   point.
6. Produce 2 to 4 distinct candidates this way, not near-duplicates of one
   idea.

Checks: one persona, one appeal, one buying question per angle; no
forbidden-tier claim.

Output: the angle brief, each candidate fully specified and ready for the
test-plan-builder or a founder-video argument outline.

Example: from an objection map full of "will this actually fit my small
kitchen," one angle targets space-anxious apartment cooks on the ease appeal,
answering "does this take up counter space I don't have."
