name: briefing/native-format-craft
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: the platform is TikTok- or Reels-style and the ad must not read
  as an ad
inputs: angle brief, the product's real feature-worthy trait, current
  platform editorial conventions
outputs: native-format concept with script or shot notes
---
Purpose: fit the concept into how the platform's own users actually post,
picked from something real about the product, not a trend with no connection
to it.

Steps
1. Pick the native format (a POV, a "get ready with me," a quick-tip list, a
   duet-style reaction) from a real, specific trait of the product, for
   example a satisfying unboxing motion or a genuinely surprising use case,
   never a trending format bolted on with no fit.
2. Write in the platform's own pacing: shorter beats, native captions style,
   the on-screen text conventions real creators use, not a repurposed TV-ad
   script.
3. Deliberately avoid the polished, brand-commercial look; a rougher, native
   feel is the point, since it is what keeps the ad from breaking the
   feed's own pattern.
4. Check the angle's claim and the CTA still survive inside the native
   structure; native does not mean the ad forgets to sell.

Checks: format ties to a real product trait; claim and CTA present despite
the native wrapper.

Output: the native-format concept plus script or shot notes in the
platform's own voice.

Example: a phone-case brand's "drop test" becomes a native-feeling POV of
someone actually dropping their phone on tile, not a studio slow-motion ad.
