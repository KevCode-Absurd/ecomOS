invoke_when: an idea is approved and the fixed brief must exist before any
  design tool or shoot is booked
inputs: approved idea record, angle brief, product facts
outputs: one-page concept brief, every field filled
---
Purpose: produce the same fixed-field brief every time, so a designer, video
editor or creator always gets the same shape of instruction regardless of
which angle or format is behind it.

Steps
1. Fill every fixed field in order: persona, angle (one sentence), dominant
   appeal, buying question, offer, destination, format, awareness stage.
2. Confirm the destination link actually sells the offer stated in the brief;
   a brief promising a bundle that the linked page does not show is sent back,
   not shipped with a note.
3. Confirm the hook, the planned image or video direction, and the copy plan
   all target the same awareness stage; a cold hook paired with a
   product-aware landing page is a mismatch caught here, not after launch.
4. Attach the reference set used (layout/mood only) and the claim sources
   behind any stated benefit.
5. Return the brief only once every field is filled; a blank field is treated
   as a stop, not a placeholder to fill in later.

Checks: no blank field; destination matches offer; one shared awareness
stage.

Output: the concept brief document, ready to feed the format-specific
production brief.

Example: brief for the "price-anchor" angle states destination as the
bundle page, not the single-unit page, since the offer field says "3-pack
bundle discount."
