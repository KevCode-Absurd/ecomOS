---
Purpose: find real, already-existing customer content worth paying to run as
an ad, rather than commissioning something new when a genuine post already
does the job.

Steps
1. Scan the brand's own tags, mentions and review-platform video exports for
   content that shows the product in genuine use, not a staged studio
   shot.
2. Trace every candidate to a real, public, still-live post, recording its
   source and date; a screenshot with no traceable source is dropped, not
   kept on a maybe.
3. Vet each candidate against the bar set by the brand's own past creator
   work: does it read as authentic, does the product actually show clearly,
   does the tone match the brand's voice.
4. Keep only clips that score above the bar on all three criteria, not
   clips that pass on two out of three.

Checks: every clip has a real, traceable source and date; every kept clip
clears all three vetting criteria.

Output: the shortlisted candidate clips, ready for the UGC creator-brief
workflow's rights and usage step.

Example: a customer's own unboxing video, tagged and public for 3 weeks
with strong product visibility, is shortlisted; a low-resolution repost
with no traceable original is dropped.
