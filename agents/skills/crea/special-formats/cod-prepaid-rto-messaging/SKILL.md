  delivery-trust facts
outputs: prepaid nudge copy, delivery-trust copy block
---
Purpose: write the India-specific lines that move a cash-on-delivery buyer
toward prepaid and lower the risk of a return-to-origin, using only real
incentives and real courier facts, since this messaging often touches
checkout trust directly.

Steps
1. Pull the account's current COD share and RTO rate, so the messaging
   effort is sized to how much this actually matters for this brand right
   now, not applied uniformly regardless of the real numbers.
2. Write the prepaid nudge as a specific, honest line: a real discount
   amount, a real faster-dispatch promise, or a real ID-verified courier
   fact, never a vague "pay online for a better experience."
3. Write the delivery-trust line using the real courier name, the real
   delivery window, and a real call-before-delivery fact if that is
   actually offered.
4. Check both lines against current operations policy before they ship,
   since a delivery-time or discount claim here is a common source of
   drift between what marketing promises and what ops can do.

Checks: discount and speed claims match what ops actually offers; every
delivery fact matches current policy.

Output: the prepaid nudge line and the delivery-trust copy block.

Example: "pay online, save 50 rupees and get it a day faster" only ships
once ops confirms both the discount and the faster timeline are real.
