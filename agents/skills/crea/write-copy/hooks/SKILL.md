name: write-copy/hooks
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: the copy needed is an opening line, before hook rate, for cold
  video, VSL or static formats
inputs: angle brief, past hook grades, product facts
outputs: graded hook bank with variants
---
Purpose: produce and grade enough real hook variety that a genuinely strong
opening survives the cut, rather than shipping the first idea that came to
mind.

Steps
1. Draft 8 to 20 hooks spanning different opening mechanisms: a direct
   question, a bold or surprising claim, a pattern interrupt (visual or
   verbal), and a relatable scene the viewer recognizes.
2. Grade every hook on 5 criteria: clarity (understood in one read), relevance
   (matches the persona's real problem), novelty (not the account's own
   overused opener), specificity (a real detail, not a vague promise) and
   credibility (believable without proof yet shown).
3. For video, take the top-graded hook and build layered variants: a different
   audio delivery, a different on-screen text treatment, a different opening
   visual, so one strong idea gets 3 or 4 real chances to test.
4. Check that no hook names the product or the offer; a cold hook earns the
   next 3 seconds first.
5. Rank the full bank and flag the top 3 to 5 with the reason they ranked
   there.

Checks: 8-20 hooks before grading; every hook scored on all 5 criteria; no
premature product/offer name.

Output: the graded bank, angle-tagged, format-tagged, with the top hooks
flagged and video variants attached.

Example: for a scalp-serum brand, hooks span "why does my scalp itch by
noon?" (question), "your shampoo is the problem" (bold claim) and a silent
close-up of flaking that cuts before a word is said (pattern interrupt).
