name: write-copy/primary-text
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: the copy needed is body text under a static, carousel card or
  marketplace module, not a headline or spoken script
inputs: angle brief, cleared claim list, platform character/space limit
outputs: primary text block with a reading-level score
---
Purpose: write body copy that opens on the dominant appeal, proves it in order
of evidence strength, and closes on one clear action, fit to the exact space
the placement gives it.

Steps
1. Open on the angle's one dominant appeal, stated as a specific outcome, never
   a generic greeting or a restated headline.
2. Order proof points from strongest real evidence (a number, a named review,
   a demonstrable mechanism) to weakest, cutting anything with no evidence
   behind it.
3. Close on the offer stated plainly and exactly one CTA; a second competing
   CTA is cut, not softened.
4. Fit the text to the placement's real limit: a feed ad tolerates a short
   punch, a carousel card needs far less, a marketplace module has its own
   fixed character cap.
5. Score reading level in code and cut or simplify until it clears grade 10.

Checks: one dominant appeal, one CTA, no claim outside the cleared list, fits
the real space limit, reading level under grade 10.

Output: the finished primary text string, its word and character count, and
its reading-grade score.

Example: a mattress ad's primary text opens "sleep hot? this one breathes,"
then cites the one clinical measurement on file, then closes with "try it 100
nights, free returns" and a single Shop Now CTA.
