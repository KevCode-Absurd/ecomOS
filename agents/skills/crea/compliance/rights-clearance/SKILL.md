---
name: compliance/rights-clearance
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: a video uses licensed stock, fonts or music, or a shoot needs
  releases, before anything ships
inputs: finished asset with licensed elements, license documents or release
  forms
outputs: license terms per asset, cleared asset set, clearance log
---
Purpose: catch a license or release mismatch before an ad ships, since a
takedown or a rights claim on a live ad is far costlier than a swap caught at
this stage.

Steps
1. List every piece of stock footage, font and music track actually used in
   the finished timeline, checked against the real timeline, not remembered
   from the brief.
2. Pull each asset's real license document and read its actual terms:
   whether commercial use is covered, which territory, how long the term
   runs, and whether it is exclusive to this brand.
3. Match the video's real use, a paid ad, running in a specific territory,
   for a specific campaign duration, against each license's actual limits.
4. Where a mismatch turns up, swap the asset for a compliant one before the
   video ships; if no compliant replacement exists, this stops and the
   founder decides whether to reshoot, re-license or drop the asset.
5. Log the clearance for each asset with its expiry or renewal date, so a
   later renewal risk is tracked, not rediscovered by surprise.

Checks: every asset has a logged, matching license; no mismatch ships.

Output: the per-asset license terms, the cleared set, and a dated clearance
log entry.

Example: a background music track's license covers organic use only; it is
swapped for a paid-ad-licensed track before the video enters CREA-W01's
launch checklist.
