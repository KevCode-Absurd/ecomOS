name: compliance/ai-use-governance
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: a new AI tool enters the pipeline, or the standing AI-use policy
  needs its periodic check
inputs: current AI-use policy, new AI tool or capability description
outputs: AI touchpoint map, classified list, updated policy
---
Purpose: keep a clear, current line between where AI may draft or automate
grunt work unsupervised and where a person must originate the idea or the
final call, across every workflow CREA runs.

Steps
1. List every point in CREA's workflows where AI drafts, generates, scores or
   grades something, from a headline draft to a predictive creative score.
2. Classify each: drafting or grunt work (readability scoring, resizing,
   tagging, first-pass variant generation) can run unsupervised; final
   ideation or unsupervised authorship (the angle itself, the founder's
   claims, a name) never can.
3. Confirm every drafting touchpoint is actually fed the brand's own facts,
   claim tiers, voice notes, product data, rather than defaulting to general
   knowledge with no brand grounding.
4. When a new AI capability arrives, an AI-actor generator, a voice clone
   tool, classify it under this same rule and add it to the policy before
   its first real use, never after.
5. Publish the current policy where every workflow can read it and log the
   update with a date.

Checks: no final-ideation step is unsupervised; every drafting step names
its brand-fact input.

Output: the touchpoint map and the current, dated AI-use policy.

Example: a new AI voice-clone tool is classified as drafting-only, gated
behind documented consent, and added to the policy before CREA-W20 uses it.
