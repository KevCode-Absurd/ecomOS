---
name: derivatives/video-cutdown
owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: one finished long-form video needs cutting into shorter length
  derivatives, each with a different opening hook
inputs: master long-form video, hook bank
outputs: 15-second and 6-second derivative cuts, hook-tagged
---
Purpose: get more real hook variants out of one production instead of
treating a long-form master as a single, single-hook asset, since each cut
gets its own chance at a different opening.

Steps
1. Cut a 15-second version that opens on one strong hook from the bank,
   keeping the mechanism or proof beat that best supports that specific
   hook, not just the first 15 seconds of the master by default.
2. Cut a 6-second bumper version keyed to a different hook from the same
   bank, so the two derivatives are not just different lengths of the same
   opening.
3. Confirm the two cuts genuinely open differently; if both defaulted to the
   master's original first seconds, redo one of them.
4. Check both derivatives still land on the single CTA from the master,
   even at the shorter runtime.

Checks: distinct hooks per cut; CTA intact in every length.

Output: the 15-second and 6-second cuts, each tagged with its hook id.

Example: the 15-second cut opens on the "why does my scalp itch" hook using
the proof beat; the 6-second bumper opens on "your shampoo is the problem"
straight into the CTA.
