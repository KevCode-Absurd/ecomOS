Purpose: get a genuine, rights-cleared UGC-style video into production, from
whichever real source fits the concept, without ever sending a creator to "make
content" with no concept.

Steps
1. Decide the source: a creator to brief fresh, an already-scouted organic post
   to license, or (only when the founder wants a fast test with no real creator)
   a synthetic AI-actor stand-in, which routes to make-creatives/ai-actor-ugc
   instead of this skill's own path.
2. For a fresh creator, pick a wide brief (let them discover the angle) or a
   specific brief (replicate a proven structure), then write the concept, a
   do/don't list and the number of takes allowed.
3. For an organic post, confirm it cleared the organic-ugc-scout vetting bar
   (authenticity, product visibility, tone) before treating it as usable.
4. Set the rights scope, paid whitelisting or organic-only, and the expiry date,
   before any spend touches the content.
5. Check the delivered or sourced clip against the angle brief: does it argue
   the same claim, or did the creator drift.
6. Log source, concept and rights to the registry.

Checks: concept present, not just an instruction; rights logged before paid use;
message match to the angle.

Output: creator brief or organic-sourcing note, with rights terms attached.

Example: angle is "finally, a stroller that fits the trunk"; brief asks a parent
creator to film their real trunk before and after, specific brief, 2 takes,
paid-whitelist rights for 6 months.
