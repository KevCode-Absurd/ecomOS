owner: crea-paid-creative-copy
version: 1
invoke_when: format is video and the source is the founder or a named authority
inputs: founder fact sheet, angle brief, claim tiers, existing hook bank
outputs: script variants, shot or frame-direction note
---
Purpose: script and direct a founder or authority-led ad as a real argument for
the viewer's problem, never a life-story reel, using only real, permissioned
facts.

Steps
1. Pull the founder's or authority's real stories, credentials and reviews from
   the brand brain, with a source and date for each; nothing invented, and stop
   if a strong story has no written permission on file.
2. Frame the video as claim, proof, mechanism, stating the viewer's problem in
   the first line, not the founder's history.
3. Script scene by scene in the proven order: hook, bridge, mechanism, product,
   proof, objection, CTA, sized to how much the product actually needs to be
   explained.
4. Draft 2 to 3 variants that vary setting, delivery style and the specific
   proof point used, so repeated founder appearances do not read as one ad
   recycled.
5. Set shot direction: gaze toward the CTA, the real product visible and
   accurate, no AI redraw of the founder's face or the product itself.
6. Run the copywriting and claim-tier pass on every spoken and on-screen line
   before handoff.

Checks: no invented fact; single CTA; no redrawn face or product.

Output: script variants (scene, visual, line, purpose) plus a shot list.

Example: founder explains why a formula works using a real customer's return
review as proof, three variants swap the review quoted but keep the same claim
and mechanism.
