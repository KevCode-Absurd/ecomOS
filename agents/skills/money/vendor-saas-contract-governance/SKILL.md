# Govern SaaS renewals, contracts, vendor risk and freelancers

## Purpose
Keep tool spend matched to revenue stage, stop a bad clause from getting signed, and never let a vendor touch customer data without a signed agreement.

## Steps
1. Audit the store's software spend against its current revenue stage; flag anything added too early, like an ERP for a brand that does not need one yet.
2. Watch every SaaS contract's auto-renew date and usage data, and draft a renegotiation or cancellation email citing the brand's own usage before the notice window closes.
3. Before a new SaaS tool, ESP, 3PL or agency gets data access, run the standard risk questionnaire and confirm a data-processing agreement is signed.
4. Run every incoming contract (NDA, MSA, vendor agreement, employment agreement) through the redline checklist.
5. Run a freelancer or contractor engagement's full lifecycle: scope of work, IP assignment, rate agreement, milestone invoicing, and renewal or offboarding.

## Checks
- No new system is proposed without a named owner.
- No vendor gets customer or payment data access without a signed DPA confirmed.
- IP assignment is explicit in every freelance engagement.

## Output
The requested scope's result: tooling audit, renewal watch and draft email, risk assessment, redlined contract, or freelancer engagement record.

## Example
A SaaS tool's usage drops to 20 percent of licensed seats 3 months before renewal; the draft cancellation email cites that exact usage number rather than a general request to lower the price.
