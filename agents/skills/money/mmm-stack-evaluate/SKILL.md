# Evaluate a third-party attribution or MMM stack

## Purpose
Decide whether a paid tool (Triple Whale, Northbeam, Rockerbox or similar) earns its subscription cost against what the brand can already do with its own blended MER and lift tests.

## Steps
1. Pull the current attribution gap report showing where platform and GA4 or blended numbers disagree.
2. Pull the tool's real cost and its claimed accuracy improvement, cited to its own source and date.
3. Compare that claim against the brand's own current lift-test accuracy and blended-MER track record.
4. Recommend adopt, reject or park, stating the cost and the accuracy gap side by side.

## Checks
- A vendor's accuracy claim is never repeated as fact; it is labeled as a vendor claim to verify.
- The recommendation states the real cost against the brand's own current accuracy, not against a competitor's case study.

## Output
Tool name, cost, claimed accuracy gain, the brand's own current accuracy, and a recommendation.

## Example
A tool costing 2,400 dollars a month claims a 15 percent accuracy gain; the brand's own recent holdout tests already track within 8 percent of platform-reported ROAS, so the recommendation is park until the gap widens.
