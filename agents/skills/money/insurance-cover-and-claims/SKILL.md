# Procure insurance cover and file claims

## Purpose
Match real coverage to the brand's real risk, and never let a claim slip past the insurer's evidence requirements or the filing deadline.

## Steps
1. Identify the coverage types that apply: product liability, cyber, business owner's policy, and D&O once outside investors are involved.
2. Compare quotes on premium, deductible and exclusions together, never premium in isolation.
3. Track every active policy's renewal date and flag it at least 30 days ahead.
4. On a loss (transit, stock, cyber, product liability), assemble the claim with the exact evidence that insurer requires and track it through to resolution.

## Checks
- The quote comparison always shows deductible and exclusions next to premium.
- A claim always cites the specific policy clause it is filed under.

## Output
A coverage checklist, a quote comparison table, a renewal calendar, or a tracked claim file.

## Example
A cheaper cyber policy excludes ransomware payment cost; the comparison flags that exclusion even though its premium looks like the better deal.
