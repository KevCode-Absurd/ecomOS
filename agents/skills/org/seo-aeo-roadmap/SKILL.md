# SEO and AEO roadmap and prioritization

## Purpose
Decide whether SEO/AEO is worth the spend at all before sequencing any work, since organic work is slow and can be wasted on a low-demand or thin-margin category.

## Steps
1. Check category demand trend and per-SKU margin first; a category failing either does not get recommended spend.
2. Sequence work: technical floor first, then money pages, then one content cluster at a time, then AEO.
3. Filter the idea bank by the brand's current bottleneck and store tags to fill each stage with ranked candidates.
4. Write an expectation memo stating a realistic timeline in months, not weeks, so the founder does not compare it to paid media's speed.
5. Package as a founder-gate card, running a founder-question round on anything the data cannot settle.

## Checks
- No content-cluster work proposed before the technical floor passes.
- Every recommendation ties to a demand or margin number.
- Timeline expectation is realistic, not inflated.

## Output
{roadmap{}, expectation_memo, open_questions[]}

## Example
Category "premium baby strollers" shows rising search volume and healthy margin: roadmap opens with the technical floor fix, then the stroller-v2 product page, before any blog cluster.
