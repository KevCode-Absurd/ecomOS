# SEO, link and AEO vendor vetting

## Purpose
Screen any SEO agency, freelancer, link vendor or GEO/AEO vendor pitch for red flags before the founder signs anything, treating every vendor statistic as a snapshot rather than a guarantee.

## Steps
1. List every claim the vendor makes and check each against known red flags: guaranteed rankings, PBNs, link farms, fabricated case studies.
2. Verify at least 2 claimed case studies independently where possible.
3. Check proposed tactics against current platform spam policies for penalty risk.
4. For an AEO/GEO vendor, check whether their method is a real repeatable audit or a one-off scan resold as ongoing monitoring.
5. Score the vendor with a specific recommend/reject reason.
6. Package as a founder-gate scorecard before any signature.

## Checks
- Every red flag actually checked.
- Case studies verified, not taken at face value.
- Reason is specific, not a gut feel.

## Output
{scorecard{}}

## Example
Vendor claims "we guarantee page 1 in 30 days": flagged as a red flag no legitimate SEO vendor can back, recommendation: reject.
