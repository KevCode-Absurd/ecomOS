# Idea supply and repeatable format design

## Purpose
Keep the content pipeline fed from three sources the brand actually owns (comments, product facts, founder stories) plus structure-only competitor signal, and turn a proven one-off into a named series the team can run without the founder.

## Steps
1. Mine comments and DMs for recurring questions, jokes or requests.
2. Mine product facts and the founder story log for first-hand material.
3. Tear down 5-10 competitor or category posts for pacing, hook type and shot count only; never copy their words, logos, products or faces.
4. Check recent performance for a post that clearly beat the account baseline.
5. If one qualifies, name the format and write a fixed template with a variable SKU/buyer slot, noting exactly what a team member (not the founder) needs to run it.
6. Log every new idea and format with its source and date.

## Checks
- Teardown notes carry structure and mood only, never copied material.
- A new format states what makes it runnable without the founder.
- Every idea has a source and date.

## Output
{idea_bank_additions[], teardown_notes[], new_format{}}

## Example
Format "60-second unboxing reaction": fixed structure (box open, first reaction, one spec called out, CTA), any team member can film it; sourced from a founder post that beat baseline reach by 4x on 2 Sep.
