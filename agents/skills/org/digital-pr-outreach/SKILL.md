# Backlink and digital PR outreach

## Purpose
Earn links and mentions through disclosed, relevant outreach: journalist queries, guest posts, broken-link and content-upgrade pitches, and review-site or listicle placements, never a templated blast or an undisclosed paid link.

## Steps
1. Build the prospect list from journalists, guest-post sites, broken-link targets and listicle/review editors relevant to the category.
2. Match each prospect to a real, disclosed pitch type.
3. Draft a personalized pitch referencing the prospect's actual past coverage.
4. Log every outreach with date, prospect, pitch type and status.
5. Track responses and hand a confirmed placement's link check to org/content-refresh-audit's tracking.
6. Flag anything reading as undisclosed or manipulative link building for the founder before sending.

## Checks
- Every pitch is personalized.
- All outreach is disclosed.
- Every send is logged with a status.

## Output
{prospect_list[], pitches[], outreach_log[]}

## Example
A broken-link pitch to a parenting blog references the exact dead link on their existing "best strollers" post and offers the buying guide as its replacement.
