# Founder LinkedIn and employer-brand content

## Purpose
Ghostwrite two related but distinct B2B-facing streams: the founder's own LinkedIn thought leadership, and the brand's employer-brand and recruiting content, both aimed at trust rather than buyer conversion, and kept separate from founder-on-camera buyer content.

## Steps
1. Start every LinkedIn draft from a real opinion or experience the founder actually holds; never invent a take.
2. Draft as text or carousel in a B2B tone.
3. Run the claims gate on any business number or milestone mentioned.
4. For an open role, draft with real day-to-day detail, not a generic job-ad template.
5. For a culture or milestone post, name real team members only with their consent already on file.
6. Send both streams back for the founder's or hiring manager's own-voice pass before anything publishes.

## Checks
- Every opinion traces to a founder-supplied note.
- Team members named only with consent on file.
- Business numbers pass the claims gate.

## Output
{linkedin_drafts[], employer_brand_drafts[]}

## Example
Founder note: "we nearly lost a shipment to a customs delay in July" becomes a LinkedIn post about supply-chain resilience, not a generic "leadership lessons" post with no real event behind it.
