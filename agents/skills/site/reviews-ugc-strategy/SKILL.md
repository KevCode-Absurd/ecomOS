# Reviews and UGC display strategy

## Purpose
Decide the review programme deliberately, build volume honestly, and never let a suspicious pattern pass as real proof.

## Steps
1. Pick a reviews app and request reviews from past customers before launch if none exist yet.
2. Decide deliberately whether reviews require a verified purchase, and write down the reason.
3. Build review volume first below 100 reviews per product, then shift the sort order to relevance and media-first sorting once past that.
4. Check review dates for suspicious clustering before trusting a sudden spike as real.
5. Show reviews from repeat buyers as continuity proof specifically for consumable products.
6. Confirm review-request automation is actually sending by checking a real recent order's timeline.

## Checks
- The verified-purchase decision is documented, not ad hoc.
- A suspicious review-date cluster is investigated before being used as proof anywhere on the site.

## Output
A reviews configuration and a display strategy spec.

## Example
"41 reviews landed in one 48-hour window right after a giveaway; flagged as suspicious clustering, excluded from the 'verified' proof count until manually checked."
