# Event and pop-up design

## Purpose
The best in-person activation targets a real place the exact customer already gathers, sized to what a small team can actually run by hand, not a scaled-up event-marketing fantasy.

## Steps
1. Identify a real, checkable place or occasion the target avatar already gathers: a wedding expo, a college fest, a local market, a festival pop-up, drawn from the avatar record, not a generic venue guess.
2. Size the activation to what the team can actually staff and run, stating the exact number of people needed.
3. Design the specific format matched to the venue's real constraints: a sampling booth, a shop-in-shop pop-up, a small guerrilla activation.
4. Cost the activation (space, staff, materials, permits or vendor fees) and check it against the budget ledger before proceeding.
5. Define the read plan: the specific metric (footfall, samples given, codes redeemed, follow-on online sales in the days after) and the measurement date.

## Checks
- The venue or occasion named is real and independently checkable.
- The activation states exactly how many people are needed to run it.
- Budget is checked against the ledger before any commitment.

## Output
`{venue_candidate_list, sized_activation_concept, activation_budget, read_plan}`

## Example
Venue: a 3-day wedding expo in the brand's home city, footfall ~4,000/day. Activation: a 6x6ft sampling booth, staffed by 2. Read plan: redemption codes given at the booth, tracked against online orders in the following 14 days.
