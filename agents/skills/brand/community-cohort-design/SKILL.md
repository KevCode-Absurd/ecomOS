# Community and loyalty cohort design

## Purpose
A loyalty cohort that means "our best customers" vaguely does not build advocacy; a cohort with precise, checkable membership criteria and one real, sustainable privilege does.

## Steps
1. Write specific, checkable membership criteria: a purchase count, a referral made, a value shared publicly, never a vague "our best customers."
2. Identify customers who already advocate unprompted (unsolicited reviews, repeat referrals, organic social mentions) as the seed group, built from real behavior data.
3. Design one real privilege the cohort controls or gets first, sized to what the brand can sustain past the launch month, not just for the first announcement.
4. Draft the invitation copy and the rules for both gaining and losing membership; the rules for losing membership should be as clear as the rules for gaining it.

## Checks
- Membership criteria are checkable against real data, not self-declared by the customer.
- Rules for losing membership are as explicit as rules for gaining it.

## Output
`{membership_criteria, seed_advocate_list, cohort_privilege_design, invitation_and_rules_draft}`

## Example
Criteria: 3+ purchases in 12 months AND one unprompted public mention. Privilege: first access to new drops 48 hours early. Losing membership: no purchase in 18 months, reviewed quietly, not publicly announced.
