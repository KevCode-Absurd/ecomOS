# Thought-leadership and awards sourcing

## Purpose
Both lanes are proactive sourcing sweeps that build a citable, checkable credibility asset over time, separate from reactive press pitching around a launch.

## Steps
1. Decide which lane this sweep covers: founder thought-leadership placements, or awards and best-of list submissions.
2. Thought leadership: search for podcasts, panels, bylined-article slots, and expert-commentary requests that fit the founder's actual, demonstrable expertise and the brand's real story, not a generic "founder available for interviews" blast.
3. Awards: track industry awards and "best of" list calls the brand genuinely qualifies for (by category, size, region), so a real win becomes a citable, checkable claim later.
4. Draft the pitch (thought leadership) or the submission copy (awards), claim-checked.

## Checks
- Every thought-leadership opportunity matches a real, demonstrable area of the founder's expertise.
- Only award calls the brand actually meets the qualifying criteria for are shortlisted.

## Output
`{thought_leadership_opportunity_list: [...], award_call_tracker: [...]}`

## Example
Award tracker flags a regional "best emerging D2C brand" award with a qualifying-revenue threshold the brand now meets, submission deadline in 6 weeks.
