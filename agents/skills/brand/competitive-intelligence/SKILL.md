# Competitive intelligence: win-loss and battlecards

## Purpose
Real comparison language comes from people who actually chose between this brand and a named competitor, not from guessing what a competitor's customer probably thinks. This skill runs that research and turns it into something support can actually use.

## Steps
1. Pull a small sample of people who almost bought this brand and instead bought the named competitor, and people who chose this brand over that competitor.
2. Ask why, in their own words, using open questions that do not lead toward a feature the brand wants validated.
3. Verify every difference named against what is actually true today (price, feature, delivery time, policy), checked against a current, dated source, never against what used to be true.
4. Turn the verified differences into a short, internal battlecard support or the founder can use in a real conversation.
5. Distribute the battlecard to support with a version number, and refresh it whenever a named competitor materially changes price, features, or policy.

## Checks
- Every difference is checked against a current, dated source.
- The battlecard is explicitly internal-facing; it is never published externally as a comparison page without a separate legal and claim-check pass.
- Win-loss questions are open, matching the interview-guide standard, never leading.

## Output
`{win_loss_transcripts, verified_difference_list, internal_battlecard}`

## Example
Verified difference: "this brand's exchange window is 30 days vs the named competitor's 7 days" (checked against both brands' current live policy pages, dated). Added to the battlecard as a support talking point for a hesitant near-miss customer.
