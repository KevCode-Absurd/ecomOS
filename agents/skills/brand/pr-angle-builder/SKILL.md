# PR angle builder

## Purpose
Journalists do not cover product announcements; they cover a story. This skill turns a brand fact into something a journalist would actually want to write, not a press-release restatement of a launch.

## Steps
1. Turn the brand story, a documented category frustration (something the avatar's own objections already point to), or a real origin fact into a pitchable angle: what is new, surprising, or timely about it right now.
2. For a launch, consider seeding the angle through a few respected insiders in a high-trust community first, chosen for real relevance to the avatar, not follower count, before a broader press push.
3. Draft the pitch itself in brand voice, run through `shared/claim-check` so nothing in it overstates what the brand can prove.

## Checks
- The angle would be newsworthy to an independent journalist, not just interesting to the founder.
- Every factual claim in the drafted pitch passes claim-check.

## Output
`{pitch_angle, seed_list_note, drafted_pitch}`

## Example
Angle: not "we launched a new collection" but "why Indian jewellery brands are quietly moving away from disposable trend pieces," using the brand's own durability positioning as the hook, seeded first to 2 respected stylists before a wider press push.
