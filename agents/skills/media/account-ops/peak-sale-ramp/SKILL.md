---
    name: peak-sale-ramp
    owner: media-buyer
    version: 1
    invoke_when: a known sale is 3-4 weeks out
    inputs: sale calendar, stock levels, current CPM trend
    outputs: peak sale media plan, change list, post-sale review template
    ---
    Purpose: ramp into a known sale and wind back down on a fixed schedule set
    before the sale starts, instead of reacting during it.

    Steps
    1. Set the budget-step schedule, new creative needs and structure changes for
       the ramp window.
    2. Fix the wind-down date and budget step-down before the sale opens.
    3. Prepare Google Shopping, Search and brand campaigns for the sale date in
       advance.
    4. Check stock levels against the planned ramp before committing budget.
    5. Write the post-sale review checklist and its read point.

    Checks
    - A wind-down date exists in writing before the sale starts.
    - The ramp plan was checked against stock, not assumed.

    Output: peak sale media plan, change list, post-sale review template.

    Example: Black Friday is 3 weeks out -> budget ramp starts this week, wind-down
    date fixed for 3 days after the sale ends, stock checked against the ramp.
    (India only: Diwali sale is 3 weeks out.)
