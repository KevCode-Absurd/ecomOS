---
    name: google-youtube-demandgen
    owner: media-buyer
    version: 1
    invoke_when: Search/Shopping are stable or proven Meta creative exists
    inputs: AOV, creative library, video references
    outputs: launch plan, creative script brief, change list
    ---
    Purpose: decide honestly whether the brand is ready for a video-first funnel
    layer, and if so, script and structure it so it earns a fair read.

    Steps
    1. Confirm Search and Shopping are already stable, or proven Meta creative
       exists to adapt.
    2. Check fit: AOV supports the CPM, the product demos visually, and there is
       creative and budget for 4-6 choppy early weeks.
    3. Restrict cold Demand Gen to in-stream and Shorts; keep other placements off.
    4. Size the budget from AOV, not a flat test amount.
    5. Script creative as hook, then demo, then prove, then ask.
    6. Route cold traffic to a real conversion mechanism and capture email.
    7. Judge results with traffic-light KPIs at the campaign level; hold off
       target-based bidding until CPA is steady over several days.

    Checks
    - The fit checklist passed before spend started.
    - Cold prospecting never targeted the brand's own customer list.

    Output: launch plan, creative script brief, change list.

    Example: AOV is 1,800 rupees and the product cannot be demoed visually in 15
    seconds -> fit check fails; recommend against launching yet.
