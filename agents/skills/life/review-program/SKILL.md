# Review collection, response and syndication

## Purpose
Times and words review requests around delivery, drafts honest responses
(including to negative reviews), checks which destinations may be incentivised,
and pushes the strongest existing reviews and UGC into the feed, ads and
marketplace listings without ever rewriting them.

## Steps
1. Set request timing about 3 days post-delivery, or defer to the reviews app's
   own optimisation if it already exists.
2. Draft honest, non-pushy request copy.
3. Check which destinations in this request may be incentivised with loyalty
   points (own site: usually yes; Google, most marketplaces: no) and word the
   ask accordingly.
4. Draft an honest, unfiltered response to each new review, especially negative
   ones; flag any review revealing a real defect back to the brand record.
5. Rank reviews and UGC by usefulness (photo attached, specific detail, recency),
   not star rating alone.
6. Select feed rating fields and pull top quotes as ad-copy candidates, noting
   the permission basis, and push allowed content to marketplace listings.

## Checks
No incentive wording where a destination forbids it. No manufactured-sounding
response. Reviews selected and quoted, never rewritten.

## Output
Request copy and timing, draft responses, a defect-flag list, feed-ready fields,
and a review-quote bank for ad copy.

## Example
"Request sent day 3 post-delivery, no points offer since it CCs Google reviews.
Top quote selected for feed: '...true to the photos, fits true to size' (5-star,
photo attached, day 4)."
