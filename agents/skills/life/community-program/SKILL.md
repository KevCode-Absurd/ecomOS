# Branded customer community design

## Purpose
Checks whether a peer-to-peer, always-on community actually fits the brand's
repeat-buyer base before building one, then designs the structure, moderation and
a light engagement calendar distinct from the campaign calendar and the support
inbox.

## Steps
1. Check the brand's repeat, high-engagement buyer count and engagement level
   against what sustains a community; recommend go or no-go with the evidence.
2. Choose the platform (Slack, Discord, Circle) and design the channel structure
   around named use cases (product help, early access, feedback).
3. Write the moderation policy, especially how a public complaint routes to the
   real support queue instead of being handled in public.
4. Build a light, sustainable engagement calendar (prompts, early-access drops,
   AMAs), distinct from the weekly campaign calendar.
5. Set when a founder or team member should show up in person versus stay quiet.
6. Set the path for a recurring community theme to reach the VoC record.
7. Track membership, active-member share, and the repeat-purchase rate of
   members versus non-members, in code.

## Checks
Go/no-go is grounded in real evidence, not enthusiasm. Complaints always route to
support. Member-vs-non-member comparison computed in code.

## Output
A community structure and moderation policy, an engagement calendar, and a
recurring health note.

## Example
"1,400 repeat buyers, 30% opened last 3 emails - enough base to test. Discord,
3 channels (help, early-access, feedback). Members' repeat-purchase rate tracked
against a matched non-member cohort from launch."
