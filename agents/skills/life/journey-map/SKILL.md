# Post-purchase journey and touchpoint mapping

## Purpose
Maps every touchpoint from order to delivered-and-used, classifies each by the
Kano model, finds the negative peak with real evidence, and designs the delivery
communication and unboxing reveal as the peak worth funding.

## Steps
1. List every real touchpoint (confirmation, shipping update, delivery, unboxing,
   first use, review request).
2. Classify each as basic, performance, or delight, with reasoning.
3. Find the negative peak by reading support tickets and reviews for quoted
   evidence, not assumption.
4. Design the order-status/delivery message set and tracking page aimed at
   keeping WISMO tickets under 5 percent of orders.
5. Design the unboxing reveal and insert tiering as the main peak to fund, capped
   to 2-5 percent of product cost.
6. Name at least one concrete change per touchpoint mapped.

## Checks
Negative peak is evidenced by quotes. Delivery-estimate language never outruns
courier data. Packaging spend stays inside the cost cap.

## Output
A journey map with Kano classes, a delivery communication set, and/or a packaging
brief with its cost model.

## Example
"Negative peak: 'no idea when it's arriving' (14 tickets quoted). Fix: add a
branded tracking page with a live estimate, cutting WISMO by an estimated third."
