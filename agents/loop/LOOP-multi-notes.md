# The 99-multi folder and cross-cutting capabilities

Date: 25 Sep 2026. Covers `ideas/v1/99-multi.json`, 421 idea records (not the roughly 1,100 the brief estimated).

## What the multi folder mostly contains

Three groups, roughly even in size.

1. **Whole-account and whole-P&L discipline.** Margin definitions, CAC-vs-gross-profit checks, cash-conversion levers, payback gates, break-even math, opex creep. Reads like extra FIN, OPS, MEA and SEC entries, and routes the same way: to MONEY.
2. **Media-buying and creative-production mechanics.** Meta and Google account structure, bidding, creative cadence, creator and affiliate deals, hook and script rules, on-page CRO and trust. Splits between MEDIA (account, budget, bidding), CREA (the creative asset) and SITE (page-level trust and CRO).
3. **Founder-level judgment and sequencing.** Diagnosing a bottleneck before picking a fix, shifting from acquisition to retention, running a peak season across every channel, crisis response, capital and exit decisions. No specialist owns this; it belongs to the loop agents.

## Cross-cutting capabilities found (`LOOP.json`, 15 records)

Weekly business review, founder claim skepticism and education, cross-channel budget allocation, launch playbooks spanning channels, a seasonal calendar (gap noted below), crisis response, OKR goal setting, an experiment design standard (read points, kill and scale rules), a source-blind ranking method, a decision log that turns founder reasons into weights, board and investor updates, a pre-delivery integrity gate, a competitor and reference intake standard, capital and exit briefing, and growth-stage or archetype diagnosis.

Five searches (fractional CMOs, growth OKR practice, crisis playbooks, investor updates, ICE/RICE prioritization) confirmed the bank's shape matches real operator practice, and added the OKR cadence and investor-update template the bank lacked.

## Doubts

- **Seasonal calendar is a real gap.** The bank's peak ideas are generic Western Q4. Category peaks and any non-US calendar have to come from the brand brain and web research. Flagged on LOOP-C05.
- **MONEY vs LOOP on diagnosis.** Financial constructs ("walk the P&L waterfall") went to MONEY; cross-domain "which lever next" ideas stayed in Diagnose. Worth a second look with whoever builds both.
- **MEDIA vs CREA.** The creative asset (hooks, scripts, casting, format) went to CREA; account, bidding, budget and creator terms went to MEDIA. A few cadence and pacing ideas could go either way.
- **Team and culture ideas went to MONEY, not LOOP**, since 21-OPS already covers hiring and founder archetype. Exit, succession and M&A ideas went to LOOP, since no specialist owns that surface.
- **PROD vs MONEY on COGS.** Data-collection (tag COGS, vet a supplier) went to PROD; decision-use (never set CAC off product margin) went to MONEY, matching how FIN and COG already split this.
