## What wave 1 missed most
Affiliate and influencer-paid program operations were entirely absent from the 48 wave 1 capabilities despite being a named domain: recruiting and vetting affiliates, fraud detection (click-timing, coupon leakage, brand-bidding), and commission/payout reconciliation. Marketplace ads (Amazon, Flipkart) and India retail media (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart) were also missing, as was any billing/invoice/GST reconciliation capability distinct from spend-anomaly detection, and any handling of ad policy rejections and account-quality appeals (wave 1 covers filtering platform suggestions, not fixing and appealing actual disapprovals). Cross-channel marginal-ROAS budget allocation, pre-launch trafficking QA, and governance for platform-native agentic bidding features (Advantage+, PMax auto-everything, Google Marketing Advisor, TikTok's new agent protocol) were also new.

## What is possible but not sold yet
A fully agentic cross-platform budget orchestrator that reasons jointly over marginal ROAS, fatigue, stock and competitor signals and acts within founder guardrails (today's tools are single-signal rule engines, not this). A predictive ad-policy compliance pre-check trained on the brand's own rejection history, catching a likely violation before submission rather than after. A same-day invoice-to-ledger reconciliation agent that resolves the invalid-traffic-credit timing gap automatically instead of a monthly manual pass. An outreach agent that discovers creators already organically mentioning the brand and drafts whitelisting terms proactively. Automatic root-cause fan-out that links a flagged spend/ROAS anomaly to same-day site speed, stock and competitor data instead of leaving the founder to check each domain by hand.
