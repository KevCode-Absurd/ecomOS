## What wave 1 missed most

Wave 1's capability list was strong on the strategy and writing side (research, angles, briefs, scripts, claims, tests) but light on three things that a working creative pipeline runs on daily:

1. **The last mile of production**: resizing one master into every placement, cutting one long video into shorts, captioning, and packaging a batch into the exact sheet or draft a bulk-upload tool or Ads Manager itself expects. These are mechanical but constant, and several categories of SaaS exist purely to do them (Celtra, OpusClip, AdManage).
2. **Synthetic and semi-synthetic production**: AI-actor UGC, URL-to-ad drafting, voice cloning and AI dubbing are now a full tool category (Arcads, Creatify, HeyGen, ElevenLabs, LipDub) that lets a brand test a concept before booking a real creator or shoot. Wave 1 covered briefing real creators well but had nothing for this synthetic layer.
3. **India-specific creative economics**: COD-to-prepaid messaging and RTO-reduction copy are a distinct, high-value writing task tied directly to the brand's own return-rate cost, not covered anywhere in wave 1's localisation record.

## What is possible but not sold yet

Marked `found_in: possible`: live alerts that interrupt a founder the moment a running test's kill or scale rule is crossed (tools report on a dashboard, none push proactively); a reserved experimental creative lane tracked on its own hit-rate curve; a standing audit catching a live ad drifting out of sync with its own landing page after the site changes post-launch; a consistent AI character or mascot sheet reused across every later generation; and real production cost-per-asset tracked against plan rather than assumed. None were found for sale as a packaged tool, but each is buildable from data ecomOS already has.
