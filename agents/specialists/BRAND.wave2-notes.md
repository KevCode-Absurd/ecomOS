## What wave 1 missed most

Wave 1 covered the research-to-positioning-to-guidelines pipeline deeply (avatars, naming, messaging, wild bet stage-gates) but almost nothing about **running PR and community as ongoing operations**: no media list building, no crisis monitoring, no ambassador-program day-to-day, no press-coverage/earned-media tracking, no award or thought-leadership sourcing. It also had one generic "research competitors" capability (C07) but nothing for **continuous competitor surveillance, battlecards, or win-loss interviews against a named competitor** : all standard in B2B competitive intelligence tooling and directly portable to DTC. On wild bets, C44-47 covers the generic stage-gate process but not the **concrete deliverables specific to each bet type**: a licensing deal memo, a wholesale line sheet, or a new-country regulatory, tax-nexus and VAT/GST feasibility check (GST applies to an India-market entry, markets: IN). Brand guidelines (C36) and brand health (C48) were treated as one-time documents/metrics rather than **living, versioned assets with governance and continuous small-sample tracking**, which is how the actual tools in this space (Frontify, Tracksuit) work.
