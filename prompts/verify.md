# Independent verification contract — draft for provider integration

Review each candidate against the original passages, adjacent qualifications and relevant conflicting candidates. Do not rely on the extraction model's explanation as evidence. Output supported / unsupported / partially supported / ambiguous for each claim, condition and numerical assertion, with exact passage references and correction reasons.

Check whether quoted words actually entail the proposed action, whether a personal example has become a universal rule, whether multiple changed variables have become a claim of single-variable causality, whether a historical platform instruction is presented as current, and whether generated headers have been mistaken for original evidence.

Return uncertainty explicitly. Unsupported additions go to review or are removed; never silently fill gaps from model memory. Structural validity, an exact quote and agreement between models are necessary controls, not proof of truth. Execution readiness requires the separate publication policy and current brand facts.
