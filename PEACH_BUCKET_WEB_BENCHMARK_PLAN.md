## Eight natural owner requests

1. “Sales aren't where I want them. Where's the biggest bottleneck, and what should I fix first?”
2. “Who's most likely to buy this, and what are parents actually worried about?”
3. “Build me a landing page for the customer you think we should target.”
4. “Give me three fresh ads my designer can make this week.”
5. “How can we make the offer more compelling without giving away our margin?”
6. “People seem interested but don't buy. What should we change on this page?”
7. “Write the follow-up messages for people who leave without buying.”
8. “If you were running this account next week, what would you do? Give me a client update and a plan for the team.”

Detailed acceptance criteria are hidden from generation. The owner prompt stays conversational. Missing facts may trigger useful questions; questions, assumptions and unfinished work are logged rather than answered with invented data.

## Paired experiment

Use Luna, Terra and Sol if web-tool compatibility is verified. Start with one end-to-end smoke test before dispatching the matrix. Compare the SAME model and reasoning configuration with:

A. Real brand data + web search.
B. Identical brand data + web search + intelligence search available throughout the workflow.

Both arms have equal web access and equal execution/reasoning limits. The tool may choose not to search when it adds no value; actual usage is reported. For public voice-of-customer work, require real cited research, including relevant Reddit discussions where accessible. Do not force wasted searches just to equalize search-call counts. Distinguish ecological comparison (independently chosen sources) from controlled comparison; source differences can contribute to outcome differences.

Use model-authored research queries and knowledge-tool calls, not prewritten retrieval queries disguised as autonomous search. Persist intermediate artifacts: audience hypotheses, cited pain-point evidence, offer rationale, final copy and handoff checks. The landing-page workflow must retain this chain. In B, allow retrieval at each step; do not demand cards for calculations or force inclusion of irrelevant guidance.

Public claims and current operational settings require appropriate verification. Reddit comments supply examples of concerns, not proof of audience prevalence, medical facts or product performance. No posting, campaign changes, messages or publishing.

## Logging and review

Record exact owner prompt, brand snapshot, model/version/settings, web queries/actions/sources, search failures, knowledge queries, returned and used card IDs/text, intermediate artifacts, final output, follow-up questions, model input/output/reasoning/cached tokens, search charges, retries and wall-clock stage/total latency. Do not imply total provider cost is captured until search billing and usage accounting are verified.

Blind business-owner and agency-owner perspective reviews assess clarity, fit, correctness, practical completeness and editing burden. Label them simulated reviews; retain disagreements and evidence. Compare cost/time to an acceptable deliverable, not just response length. A real operator rating sheet accompanies results.

Final deliverable: side-by-side trace and readout, showing what improved, what got worse, missing evidence, and where retrieval replaced external research versus simply adding cost.
