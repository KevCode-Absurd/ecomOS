**The 52% open rate and $14,000 attributed revenue show recorded engagement and purchase associations—not reliable evidence of incremental, profitable sales.** Email may be helping, but the current evidence cannot establish how much.

### What the numbers actually tell us

- **Opens:** Privacy features and machine opens can inflate the 52%. It does not prove strong subject lines, healthy inbox placement or purchase intent.
- **Attributed revenue:** $14,000 ÷ $45,000 Shopify revenue = **31.1%**. Describe this as “Klaviyo-attributed revenue equivalent to 31.1% of store revenue,” not “email generated 31.1% of sales.” Its five-day click plus five-day open window can credit purchases that would have happened anyway.
- **Overlapping credit:** Meta reports $70,400 and Klaviyo $14,000: together **$84,400 against $45,000 in Shopify revenue**. These figures are not additive. Overlap and unreconciled reporting prevent a causal interpretation.
- **Business context:** Shopify revenue fell from $54,000 to $45,000, or **16.7%**, while AOV stayed at **$90** ($54,000/600 and $45,000/500). Email could be cushioning that decline—or receiving credit for existing demand. We lack prior-period email data to distinguish these possibilities.
- **Clicks and orders:** The reported 1.1% click rate and 0.18% order rate deserve more attention than opens, but their denominators and campaign/flow coverage need confirming. They are not automatically good or bad against a universal benchmark.

### 1. Reconcile measurement and separate campaigns from flows

Separate **more sending** from **better performance**. Compare delivery volume, click rate, order rate and revenue per delivery; also count distinct people reached across the month. The 12,000 marketable profiles are not necessarily 12,000 recipients per campaign.

Match attributed orders to Shopify order IDs where available. Compare the current attribution view with click-only reporting, if available, as a sensitivity check—not an incrementality estimate.

### 2. Audit delivery health and lifecycle plumbing
