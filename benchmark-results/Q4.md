## Implied link CPC

Formula: CPC = CPM / (1000 × CTR decimal).

Previous: $12 / (1000 × 0.015) = $12 / 15 = $0.80. Check: $18,000 spend / (1,500,000 impressions × 1.5%) = $18,000 / 22,500 clicks = $0.80.

Current: $15 / (1000 × 0.012) = $15 / 12 = $1.25. Check: $22,000 spend / (1,466,667 impressions × 1.2%) = $22,000 / 17,600 clicks = $1.25.

CPC rose $0.45, or 56.25%. The CPM increase alone would have taken CPC from $0.80 to $1.00 if CTR had held; the CTR decline added the remaining $0.25. Both auction pressure and weaker creative response are contributing.
