Both agents fixed the same page in nearly the same way, and the one without the idea bank finished slightly ahead. Arm A cut load from 15.4 s to 5.2 s (66%), LCP from 1.70 s to 1.22 s (28%), first paint from 740 ms to 528 ms (29%) and bytes from 4.34 MB to 1.98 MB (54%). Arm B cut load to 5.8 s (62%), LCP to 1.30 s (24%), bytes to 2.09 MB (52%) and did not move first paint. Neither moved blocking time, which is React hydration and needs the app rebuilt.

What decided it. Both arms found the same 7 core fixes: subset the two 3 MB Korean fonts, turn off Cloudflare's Rocket Loader, load the 7 marketing tags after the page, shrink the 640 px category icons, convert Lato to WOFF2, preload the hero image, and remove the dead Universal Analytics tag. Arm A added one more that Arm B did not rank: inlining the two render-blocking stylesheets, which is the whole first-paint gain. Arm A also ordered Rocket Loader before the fonts, which avoided the trap both arms hit when fonts go first: the load event fires early and Rocket Loader re-fetches 20 scripts one at a time, so the carousel is not on the page yet.

What the idea bank did. It matched 4 of Arm B's 8 fixes (fonts, Rocket Loader, hero preload, WOFF2) and supplied the tracking-risk rule. Arm B's own audit had already found all four. It challenged 6 bank ideas as not firing on this page or overstated, and ranked its list by measured marginal effect as instructed, so the bank neither misled it nor added a fix the audit missed. On this page, with this model, the bank was confirmation, not discovery.

What this means for the loop. The audit of the brand's own surface produced the fixes. The value the bank can add is on pages and brands where the auditing agent lacks the specific trick, and as a checklist against misses like the stylesheet one. The measured before-and-after loop itself worked: every fix has a number, every step was checked to be the same page, and two ordering traps were caught by the harness rather than by a reviewer.

What to ship on the live site, in order. Latin subsets of the Nanum and Lato fonts as WOFF2, with Rocket Loader switched off in the same release. Then the tags to lazyOnload once marketing accepts that visitors who bounce inside about 5 s are not counted. Then icon sizes and the hero preload, the inlined critical CSS, and the removal of the Universal Analytics tag. Then profile hydration.
