## C. Our top jobs for every screen

### 00 Today

- **T1.** When I sit down in the morning with 20 minutes before the team starts asking me things, help me see the one place the business is losing the most money right now, in dollars, and the fixes ranked by what each is worth, so I spend today on that and not on whatever is loudest *Metric: dollars at stake at the weakest step from visit to repeat order, 7 day, against its own 4 week median.*
- **T2.** When yesterday's orders are in and bills are coming, help me see whether yesterday made money after every cost and whether cash covers the next 4 weeks, so I know if I can keep spending today *Metric: yesterday's contribution after product cost, shipping, fees, returns and ads, and weeks of cash cover.*
- **T3.** When something breaks during the day, like checkout failing, a campaign spending double, a best seller running out or a pixel going quiet, help me know within the hour and whether we caused it, so I stop the loss before tomorrow's report *Metric: minutes from a break to the founder seeing it, and dollars lost per break.*
- **T4.** When people are waiting on me across Slack, email, WhatsApp and the agency's threads, help me see every decision and task in one board, decide each in a minute, and hand it off with the numbers, so no one is blocked by me *Metric: decisions waiting on the founder longer than 2 days.*
- **T5.** When revenue is up and the agency says the account is doing well, help me see whether new customers are growing or we are rebuying the same people, so I do not mistake a loyal base for growth *Metric: new customer revenue share and cost per new customer, 28 day.*

### 01 Products and research

- **T1.** When sales dip for a quarter and I cannot tell if it is us or the market, help me see whether our category is growing or shrinking quarter by quarter, and where we grow slower than it line by line, so I know whether to fix us or ride it out *Metric: our growth minus category growth, per product line, quarter against the same quarter last year.*
- **T2.** When a supplier quote, freight rate or tariff moves and margin keeps slipping, help me see which input costs are rising on my top products and who can squeeze my margin, in plain words, so I act before margin shrinks *Metric: landed cost per unit for the top 10 SKUs against last quarter.*
- **T3.** When sales on our own site flatten, help me see where buyers in my category are moving and which nearby categories they also buy are growing, so we follow the buyer instead of fighting it *Metric: share of category demand by channel (brand sites, Amazon, TikTok Shop, search, AI answers), quarterly.*
- **T4.** When I am about to put $20k or more into a new product, or an idea excites the team, help me check demand from more than one source and margin after every real cost in an hour, and force a go or no-go, so I do not sink cash into a product that will not sell *Metric: share of launches that hit their 90 day plan.*
- **T5.** When I am shaping a product or a price, help me see the price bands my category buys in, the price buyers call fair and the complaints no one has fixed, so we build and price for what buyers want *Metric: share of our revenue from products priced inside the busiest band.*

### 02 Brand and customers

- **T1.** When I brief an ad, a page or a product and cannot say who buys from us and why, help me see the real buyers and the job they hire us for, backed by orders, reviews and their own words, so money stops going to a customer we made up *Metric: share of orders that map to a named avatar with evidence.*
- **T2.** When plenty of people visit and few buy, or returns climb, help me name the top reasons they hold back or send it back and what changed the mind of those who bought, so the page answers the doubt instead of shouting louder *Metric: conversion rate on the top 5 product pages, and not as expected return rate.*
- **T3.** When I or the copywriter sit down to write, and when a new wave of reviews lands, help me use the exact words customers use and see what they love and hate as it changes, so the copy sounds like them and a quality slip does not hurt sales first *Metric: share of live ads and pages that use a language bank phrase, and review rating trend.*
- **T4.** When I decide where to spend to win new buyers, help me see which buyer groups by age, gender, placement, first product and channel make the most gross profit over 6 months and which are getting pricier, so budget goes to buyers worth having *Metric: 6 month gross profit per new customer, by buyer group.*
- **T5.** When a small group of customers makes a big share of our profit and some of them go quiet, help me know them by name, treat them quietly well and hear why any stop, so they stay and bring others *Metric: 12 month repeat rate of the top 5 percent of customers.*

### 03 Competitors and market

- **T1.** When I catch myself scrolling rival sites and Instagram at night, help me see only the rival moves that touch our sales this week, like a sale, a launch, a new policy or a stock out, so I can act on those and stop watching the rest *Metric: rival moves flagged per week with our sales touched in dollars.*
- **T2.** When our creative team runs dry, help me see how rivals spread their ads across awareness levels and which angles they keep paying for, turned into principles we can test in our own words, so we reach new buyers without copying anyone *Metric: tests launched from rival principles each month and their hit rate.*
- **T3.** When shoppers compare us with a named rival and some leave for them, help me learn from rival reviews and lost buyers what they fail at and what they win on, so we pitch against their weakness with proof *Metric: conversion rate of chats and pages where a rival is named.*
- **T4.** When I set prices or plan a sale, help me see where our price and discounts sit against rivals per unit and when they run their big sales, so we are not priced out, not training buyers to wait and not drowned out *Metric: our price per unit against the rival median, per product.*
- **T5.** When I set next quarter's budget, help me see how loud we are next to rivals in ads, brand search and Amazon, and who is growing faster, so I know if we are being outshouted or paying a toll on our own name *Metric: our share of active ads and of brand search impressions.*

### 04 Offers and pricing

- **T1.** When someone on the team or at the agency wants to run a discount or change an offer, help me see what it costs in gross profit, how many extra orders it needs, the new most we can pay per purchase, and whether the ad, page and cart all show the same offer, so I do not give away the month's profit *Metric: gross profit per order during offers against normal weeks.*
- **T2.** When sales only come during promotions and every visitor gets a code, help me see how much revenue depends on discounts and which discounts go to buyers who would have bought anyway, so I stop training buyers to wait *Metric: share of revenue on a discount code, new vs returning.*
- **T3.** When order size is stuck and shoppers hesitate, help me raise order value and remove the biggest doubt with thresholds, bundles, gifts and guarantees that still clear the margin floor, so each order earns more without a deeper discount *Metric: gross profit per order and average order value.*
- **T4.** When a tariff, freight or supplier cost rise lands, or a new product needs a price, help me choose between raising price, changing the pack or absorbing it, using real costs and the rival range, so margin holds without losing buyers *Metric: gross margin percent on the top 10 SKUs.*
- **T5.** When Black Friday or a rival's big sale is coming, or an offer has gone stale, help me plan an answer that adds profit and does not just pull orders forward or match their price, so the season lifts the year *Metric: season gross profit against normal weeks, counting the dip after.*

### 05 Brand brain

- **T1.** When I bring a brand onto ecomOS, help me turn its site, ads, reviews, tickets and my own answers into one record filed on the right shelves and linked like a graph, so I can trust every agent, freelancer and screen to start from the same checked facts *Metric: record sections signed by the founder in the last 90 days, share.*
- **T2.** When I hand a brief to an agent, a freelancer or an agency, help me give them the facts, voice, customer words and proven standards I would give, so I stop rewriting drafts that should have been right the first time *Metric: drafts sent back for a brand reason, share of all reviewed drafts, 30 days.*
- **T3.** When a draft or a live ad carries a claim, help me see whether we can prove it and have permission to use it, so I do not get ads rejected, a refund wave or a legal letter *Metric: claims in live ads, emails and pages with proof on file, share.*
- **T4.** When the site, a product, an offer or a policy changes, help me find every ad, email and page still saying the old thing the same day, so I never have shoppers see a promise we stopped keeping *Metric: facts that differ between the record and the live site, count, daily.*
- **T5.** When the team keeps asking 'can we say this' or 'what did we decide', help me let them find the answer and the reason without me, so I get my day back and decisions stick *Metric: brand questions answered from the record without the founder, share, 30 days.*

### 06 Store health

- **T1.** When the store or checkout stops taking orders at night, on a weekend or on one payment method, help me know within minutes and where it broke, so I stop paying for ads that send shoppers who cannot buy *Metric: minutes from checkout breaking to someone being told, per incident.*
- **T2.** When a developer, an agency or I change the theme, add an app or edit a setting, help me make the change on a copy, walk the buying flow and check the numbers the next morning, so I never lose a week of sales to one change *Metric: days a break stays live before it is found, per incident.*
- **T3.** When app bills stack up and old app code stays in the theme after the app is gone, help me see which apps earn nothing and what they cost in fees and lost sales, so I cut both *Metric: monthly app bill, and seconds of load time added by apps on product pages.*
- **T4.** When paid orders stall, stock is wrong or money leaks out after the sale, help me find them before the customer writes in, so I never forget a paid order or lose margin I cannot see *Metric: paid orders not shipped past the promised ship date, count, daily.*
- **T5.** When a sale, a launch, a new country or a peak day is coming, help me prove the store is ready with real orders and timed checks, so I do not watch it fail on the best day *Metric: minutes from scheduled promo start to confirmed live, per promo.*

### 07 Landing pages and CRO

- **T1.** When I am paying for clicks that do not buy, help me see from scroll depth, recordings, polls and GA4 the one block where shoppers stop and what it costs, so I fix that first instead of redesigning everything *Metric: conversion rate from paid sessions on the top 10 landing pages, mobile, 30 days.*
- **T2.** When a shopper taps an ad and lands on a page that says something else, help me make the page keep the ad's promise, starting with the ads that spend most, so I stop paying for clicks that leave feeling tricked *Metric: ad to page match score on the top 20 ads by spend, and bounce rate from paid clicks.*
- **T3.** When the same questions and doubts stop shoppers, help me answer each one on the page, next to the choice it blocks, in the customer's own words, so I get fewer tickets and more buyers *Metric: pre-sale tickets per 1,000 sessions, and product page conversion rate.*
- **T4.** When a test is proposed or called a winner, help me rank ideas by money and evidence, read results on profit per visitor, and promote only real wins to the page standard, so I stop rolling out wins that fade or lose money *Metric: shipped page changes with a measured profit per visitor gain, share.*

### 08 Site and speed

- **T1.** When a shopper taps our ad on a phone on a slow connection and waits, help me get the product, price and buy button on screen in under 2.5 seconds on the pages that sell, so I stop paying for clicks that leave before they see what they came for *Metric: share of mobile visits with the main image and price painted in under 2.5 seconds (field LCP p75) on the top 10 paid landing pages, 28 days.*
- **T2.** When apps, scripts and old code pile up on the pages, help me cut what earns nothing and put a dollar figure on what stays, so I pay for speed only where it makes money *Metric: script weight and main thread blocking time on product pages, and what slowness costs per month in our own dollars.*
- **T3.** When anyone ships a speed fix or a site change, help me test it on a copy of the theme, check tracking and the buying flow, and merge only if it wins, so I never lose sales to a speed fix *Metric: speed changes with a measured before and after and no break after publish, share.*
- **T4.** When I pay a developer or an agency for speed, help me brief only the fixes that change what shoppers see on the pages that sell, so I pay for results, not scores *Metric: dollars spent on speed per second saved on money pages, field data.*

### 09 Creative

- **T3.** When cost per new customer climbs and most new ads speak to people who already know us, help me aim more of them at shoppers who have never heard of us, using what rivals and buyers teach us, so I grow with new buyers *Metric: share of spend on unaware and problem aware ads, 28 day.*
- **T4.** When we can make 50 new ads a week with AI and editors, help me make the ones that deserve test budget and know what each cost to make, so I do not pay for ads that never pay back *Metric: production cost per winning ad, 90 day.*
- **T5.** When I decide which creators to brief again, help me see who drives purchases, not just views, and keep their rights in order, so I put the creator budget into people whose videos sell *Metric: cost per purchase on creator and partnership ads vs brand handle ads, 28 day.*

### 10 Copy
