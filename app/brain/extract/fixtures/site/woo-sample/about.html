Our story

Hearth & Wick was founded in 2019 by two friends in a Burlington garage. We wanted a candle that did not leave soot on the wall.

Hearth & Wick candles are designed for slow evenings at home. We pour every one by hand, in batches of 48.

For every candle sold, we donate one to a local shelter.

Hand-poured in small batches. Clean soy wax. Cotton wicks.
