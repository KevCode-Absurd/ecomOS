Shipping and returns

Shipping

Free shipping on orders over $60. Standard shipping is $6.95 flat rate for orders under $60.

Standard - $6.95: 3 - 5 business days. Express - $14.00: 1 - 2 business days.

Returns

You can return unused candles within 45 days of delivery. Refunds go back to your original payment method. Exchanges are available for a different scent at no cost.

Our Glow Guarantee is our promise that if a candle tunnels or will not stay lit, we replace it for free.
