## What Kevin said with the notes (summary)

- Make the jobs tactical, on the ground, executable. Not "in the air".
- Example 1: levels of awareness. Are we only running bottom of funnel (product aware, most aware) ads and not top of funnel (unaware, problem aware) ads like native ads? How are competitors spread? Classify our whole ad library and competitors' ads by awareness level (and product, solution, problem). Where we have thin coverage, filter the inspiration library by that level and suggest what to make. Same view on Google: top, middle, bottom of funnel. The job: publish more top of funnel creatives to reach new buyers instead of circling the same audience. The enabler: an awareness heat map, which powers many insights at the back end.
- Example 2: AI video ads. Needs access to a video tool (Higgsfield or similar). Write the script first, then variations of the script, then cut each variation to 15, 30 and 30 to 45 seconds, then pick one from each stack. What informs it: past winners, awareness gaps, the brain, competitor principles.
